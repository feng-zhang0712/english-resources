# 关系与联系

> 本章收跨领域「建立、维系、切断联系」的通用动词、名词与形容词：事物之间如何相关、相连、合并或分离。**不**收人际社交关系（friend, rapport, connection 表人脉 → `02-02`）；**不**收以心理联想为主的 connect / associate（→ `01-04`）；**不**收纯空间路径与路口（junction, merge → `05-03`）；**不**收对照、等价、平行（equivalent, parallel → `06-03`）；**不**收纯因果链（cause, effect → `07-04`）。一词一条：`connect`、`relationship` 等已在它章立条，本章在 `[联]` 中标注，不重复写满格词条。

**relate**
英/美 /rɪˈleɪt/
v. 关联；涉及；叙述
[义] To show or make a connection between two or more things; to be connected with or refer to something; to tell a story or give an account. 表明或建立事物之间的联系（relate A to B 把 A 与 B 联系起来）；与某事有关（The issue relates to safety. 此事涉及安全）；叙述经历（relate an anecdote 讲轶事）。比 connect 更偏「逻辑、叙事上的关联」，不限物理接通。
[形] related / relating；第三人称单数 relates
[例] ① The study relates high stress to poor sleep quality. 研究将高压与睡眠质量差联系起来。 ② She related how she had first met her mentor years ago. 她讲述了多年前初遇导师的经过。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *referre*（带回、提及），re- + ferre（携带）→ 把两件事「带回一起」→ 关联、叙述。
[族] relation 关系, relative 相对的, relationship 关系（人际义另章）, correlation 相关
[搭] relate to 与……有关；理解/共鸣, relate A to B 把 A 与 B 联系起来, closely related 密切相关, relate a story 讲述经历
[联] connect 连接：更偏接通或建立联系, link 链接, associate 联想（心理）, involve 牵涉, concern 涉及, refer 提及, explain 说明, relevant 相关的, unrelated 无关的
[记] relate to 口语极常见：① 与……有关；② 理解、感同身受（I can relate to that. 我懂）。related 形容词见下条

**relation**
英/美 /rɪˈleɪʃn/
n. 关系；联系；亲属
[义] The way in which two or more people, groups, or things are connected; a person connected by blood or marriage. 事物或人之间的关联方式（the relation between diet and health 饮食与健康的关系）；亲属（a close relation 近亲）。比 relationship 更中性、更书面，可用于抽象逻辑关系；人际亲密关系多用 relationship（另章）。
[形] 复数 relations
[例] ① Scientists are studying the relation between screen time and attention spans. 科学家在研究屏幕时间与注意力时长之间的关系。 ② Diplomatic relations between the two countries have improved. 两国外交关系有所改善。
[搭] in relation to 关于, bear no relation to 与……毫无关系, diplomatic relations 外交关系, close relation 近亲, relation between A and B A 与 B 的关系
[联] relationship 关系（偏人际）, connection 联系, link 关联, correlation 相关性, relative 亲属/相对的, interaction 互动, tie 纽带, kinship 亲属关系（家庭章）
[记] 可数/不可数依语境。in relation to 正式，表「就……而言」。relations 复数可指「国际关系」「劳资关系」

**related**
英/美 /rɪˈleɪtɪd/
adj. 相关的；有亲属关系的
[义] Connected with or belonging to the same family or group. 与某事有逻辑或因果联系（related issues 相关问题, genetically related 有基因关联）；有血缘或婚姻亲属关系（we are distantly related 我们是远亲）。
[例] ① The two products are related but serve different markets. 两款产品相关，但面向不同市场。 ② Are you related to the author of this book? 你和这本书的作者有亲属关系吗？
[搭] closely related 密切相关, related to 与……有关, related party 关联方, related field 相关领域, distantly related 远亲
[联] unrelated 无关的, relevant 相关的, connected 有联系的, associated 关联的, linked 联结的, similar 相似的, kin 亲属
[记] be related to 兼表「与……有关」和「与……是亲戚」。related 作定语：related documents 相关文件

**unrelated**
英/美 /ˌʌnrɪˈleɪtɪd/
adj. 无关的；无亲属关系的
[义] Not connected with something else; not belonging to the same family. 与某事无逻辑或因果联系（two unrelated incidents 两起无关事件）；无亲属关系（completely unrelated strangers 毫无亲戚关系的陌生人）。
[例] ① The police concluded that the two burglaries were unrelated. 警方认定两起入室盗窃案彼此无关。 ② She raised an unrelated point during the discussion. 讨论中她提了一个与主题无关的观点。
[搭] completely unrelated 完全无关, unrelated to 与……无关, unrelated incidents 无关事件, unrelated matters 无关事项
[联] related 相关的, irrelevant 不相关的, separate 分开的, independent 独立的, random 随机的, coincidental 巧合的
[记] unrelated to 后接名词或动名词。irrelevant 更强调「不 pertinent、跑题」；unrelated 强调「无关联」

**link**
英/美 /lɪŋk/
n. 链接；纽带；环节
v. 连接；联系
[义] A connection between people, things, or ideas; one part of a chain or system; to connect two or more things. 人、事、观念之间的联系或链条中的一环（a weak link 薄弱环节, missing link 缺失环节）；把事物连起来（link A to/with B 把 A 与 B 相连）。比 connect 更偏「链环、环节」意象；网页 link 亦常用。
[形] linked / linking
[例] ① Researchers found a link between air pollution and asthma rates. 研究者发现空气污染与哮喘发病率之间存在关联。 ② The report links poor housing to long-term health problems. 报告将恶劣住房与长期健康问题联系起来。
[源] 古北欧语 *hlenkr*（链环）→ 链上的一环 → 联系、链接。
[族] linkage 联结, linkup 连接, hyperlinked 超链接的
[搭] link between A and B A 与 B 的关联, link to/with 与……连接, weak link 薄弱环节, missing link 缺失环节, hypertext link 超文本链接
[联] connect 连接, join 接合, tie 系牢, bond 结合, chain 链条, association 关联, disconnect 断开, separate 分开
[记] link A to/with B；link up with 与……连接起来。名词可数：a link。link 作动词比 connect 略正式

**linkage**
英/美 /ˈlɪŋkɪdʒ/
n. 联结；联动；连锁
[义] The action of linking or the state of being linked; a system of links connecting parts. 连接的行为或状态；部件之间的联动装置或逻辑连锁（genetic linkage 基因连锁, trade linkage 贸易联动, mechanical linkage 机械联动装置）。偏正式、技术或学术语体。
[例] ① The study examines the linkage between education spending and economic growth. 研究考察教育投入与经济增长之间的联动关系。 ② A broken linkage in the engine caused the failure. 发动机中一段联动装置损坏导致了故障。
[搭] genetic linkage 基因连锁, trade linkage 贸易联动, mechanical linkage 机械联动, linkage between A and B A 与 B 的联结
[联] link 链接, connection 连接, bond 结合, mechanism 机制, chain 链条, network 网络, correlation 相关
[记] 可数/不可数均可。linkage 比 link 更抽象、更系统。政治语境 trade linkage 指「将议题挂钩谈判」

**join**
英/美 /dʒɔɪn/
v. 连接；加入；参加
[义] To connect or fasten things together; to become a member of a group or take part in an activity. 把事物接合在一起（join the pieces 把零件拼上）；成为成员或参与（join a club 加入俱乐部, join the meeting 参加会议）。本义「接合」，引申「加入某个整体」。
[形] joined / joining
[例] ① Join the two boards with screws before painting. 上漆前用螺丝把两块板拼起来。 ② She joined the team as a project manager last month. 她上月以项目经理身份加入团队。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *iungere*（绑在一起）→ 连接、加入。
[族] joint 关节, junction 交叉点（空间章）, conjunction 连词, adjoin 毗邻
[搭] join together 接合, join in 参与, join forces 联手, join the dots 连点成线, join hands 携手
[联] connect 连接, link 链接, combine 合并, unite 联合, separate 分开, split 分裂, attach 附着, member 成员
[记] join A to B 把 A 接到 B；join sb. for lunch 与某人共进午餐。join 作「参加」时不及物（join the army）或 join in（加入活动）

**joint**
英/美 /dʒɔɪnt/
n. 关节；接合处；接头
adj. 联合的；共同的
[义] A place where two parts of the body or object are joined; shared by two or more people or groups. 身体或物体的接合处（knee joint 膝关节, joint in the pipe 管道接头）；联合的、共同的（joint effort 共同努力, joint account 联名账户）。
[例] ① He injured his shoulder joint during the match. 他在比赛中伤了肩关节。 ② The two companies announced a joint venture. 两家公司宣布成立合资企业。
[搭] knee joint 膝关节, joint effort 共同努力, joint venture 合资企业, joint account 联名账户, out of joint 脱臼；失调
[联] join 连接, junction 交叉点, combine 合并, shared 共享的, mutual 相互的, separate 分开的, bone 骨头, partnership 合伙
[记] 可数：a joint。out of joint 字面「脱臼」，比喻「乱了套」。joint 俚语可指「大麻烟卷」——语境区分

**combine**
英/美 /kəmˈbaɪn/
v. 结合；合并；兼有
n. 联合企业；收割机
[义] To join or merge things to form a single unit; to have two or more features at once. 把事物合成一体（combine the ingredients 混合原料, combine work and study 工学兼顾）。名词指联合企业或联合收割机——次要义。
[形] combined / combining
[例] ① Combine the flour and eggs in a large bowl. 在大碗里把面粉和鸡蛋混合。 ② The program combines theory with hands-on practice. 该项目将理论与动手实践相结合。
[源] 拉丁语 *combinare*，com-（一起）+ *bini*（两个一组）→ 合成一对 → 结合。
[族] combination 组合, combined 联合的, recombine 重组
[搭] combine A with/and B 把 A 与 B 结合, combined effort 共同努力, combination of ……的组合, combine forces 联手
[联] unite 联合, merge 合并, mix 混合, join 连接, separate 分开, integrate 整合, blend 混合, split 分裂
[记] combine A with B / combine A and B 均可。combined 作形容词极常见（combined income 合计收入）

**combination**
英/美 /ˌkɒmbɪˈneɪʃn/
n. 组合；结合；联合体
[义] A joining or merging of different things; a mixture of qualities or elements. 不同事物合成一体；多种特质或元素的混合（a winning combination 成功组合, combination of factors 多种因素, in combination with 与……联合）。可数。
[例] ① The dish is a combination of Thai and French flavors. 这道菜融合了泰式与法式风味。 ② A combination of luck and skill got her the promotion. 运气加实力让她获得了晋升。
[搭] combination of ……的组合, in combination with 与……联合, winning combination 成功组合, combination lock 密码锁
[联] combine 结合, mixture 混合物, blend 混合, union 联合, set 一套, pair 一对, integration 整合
[记] 可数：a combination。combination of A and B 极常用。combination lock 指「转盘密码锁」

**unite**
英/美 /juˈnaɪt/
v. 联合；团结；统一
[义] To join together as a group or cause to become one; to come together for a common purpose. 使联合或团结；为共同目标走到一起（unite against a threat 团结应对威胁, united front 统一战线）。比 combine 更强调「政治、社会、情感上的同心」。
[形] united / uniting
[例] ① The community united to rebuild the school after the flood. 洪水过后，社区团结起来重建学校。 ② The two parties united behind a single candidate. 两党团结在一位候选人身后。
[源] 拉丁语 *unire*，uni-（一）+ -ire（使成为）→ 使成为一体 → 联合。
[族] unity 团结, union 联合, united 联合的, reunion 重聚
[搭] unite against 团结对抗, unite behind 团结支持, united front 统一战线, unite in 在……上团结一致
[联] combine 结合, join 加入, merge 合并, split 分裂, divide 分开, solidarity 团结, coalition 联盟, separate 分离
[记] unite 不及物（The groups united.）或 unite to do。United 作国名/专名：the United States, United Kingdom

**separate**
英/美 /ˈseprət/
adj. 分开的；各自的
英/美 /ˈsepəreɪt/
v. 分开；分离；区分
[义] Forming a unit apart from others; to divide or keep apart; to tell apart. 各自独立、不在一起的（separate rooms 各一间房）；使分开或区分（separate the wheat from the chaff 去芜存菁, separate fact from fiction 区分事实与虚构）。动词重音在末音节；形容词重音在首音节。
[形] separated / separating
[例] ① They sleep in separate bedrooms but remain close. 他们分房睡，但感情很好。 ② It is hard to separate personal feelings from professional judgment. 很难把个人情感与职业判断分开。
[源] 拉丁语 *separare*，se-（分开）+ *parare*（准备、安排）→ 分置 → 分离。
[族] separation 分离, separately 分别地, separable 可分离的
[搭] separate from 与……分开, separate into 分成, separate fact from fiction 区分虚实, separate accounts 独立账户
[联] split 分裂, divide 划分, disconnect 断开, detach 脱离, join 连接, combine 合并, distinct 不同的, isolate 隔离
[记] 形容词 /ˈseprət/，动词 /ˈsepəreɪt/。separate A from B 固定搭配。separated 可指「分居」（legally separated）

**separation**
英/美 /ˌsepəˈreɪʃn/
n. 分离；分开；分居
[义] The action of separating or the state of being separate; a period when a couple live apart. 分开的行为或状态；夫妻分居（a long separation 长期分离, separation of powers 权力分立, trial separation 试分居）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① The separation of church and state is a basic principle in many democracies. 政教分离是许多民主国家的基本原则。 ② After years of separation, the siblings were reunited. 多年分离后，兄妹重逢了。
[搭] separation of powers 权力分立, separation anxiety 分离焦虑, trial separation 试分居, separation between A and B A 与 B 的分离
[联] separate 分开, division 分割, split 分裂, divorce 离婚, reunion 重聚, distance 距离, isolation 隔离
[记] 可数/不可数。separation of powers 政治固定术语。separation anxiety 儿童心理学常用

**split**
英/美 /splɪt/
v. 分裂；分开；分担
n. 裂口；分裂；份额
[义] To divide or cause to divide into parts, especially along a natural line; to share something between people. 沿自然纹路或意见分裂（split the log 劈柴, the party split over the issue 党内因此分裂）；分担（split the bill 分摊账单）。过去式/过去分词 split（不规则但同形）。
[形] split / split / splitting
[例] ① Let's split the cost of the taxi. 出租车费咱们分摊吧。 ② The committee split into two factions after the vote. 投票后委员会分裂成两派。
[搭] split up 分手；拆分, split the difference 折中, split second 一瞬间, split decision 分歧裁定
[联] divide 划分, separate 分开, break 打破, crack 裂开, unite 联合, merge 合并, share 分享, faction 派系
[记] split 三态同形。split up 可指情侣分手或团队解散。split second 表「一刹那」

**attach**
英/美 /əˈtætʃ/
v. 附着；附上；使附属；使依恋
[义] To fasten or join one thing to another; to assign someone to a group; to feel affection for. 把一物固定或连到另一物（attach a file 附上文件, attach importance to 重视）；派遣隶属（attached to the embassy 派驻使馆）；产生依恋（become attached to 对……产生感情）。
[形] attached / attaching
[例] ① Please attach the receipt to your expense report. 请把收据附在报销单上。 ② She was attached to the London office for six months. 她被派驻伦敦办事处六个月。
[源] 古法语 *atachier*，a-（向）+ 日耳曼根「钉、固定」→ 附上。
[族] attachment 附件, attaché 随员, detachable 可拆卸的
[搭] attach to 附在, attach importance to 重视, attach a file 附上文件, attached to 隶属于, become attached to 依恋
[联] detach 脱离, connect 连接, link 链接, fasten 系紧, stick 粘住, remove 移除, assign 派遣
[记] attach 作邮件用语：attachment 附件。no strings attached 指「无附加条件」。attaché case 公文包

**detach**
英/美 /dɪˈtætʃ/
v. 拆卸；脱离；派遣
[义] To disconnect or remove something from what it is joined to; to send a unit away on a special mission. 从所附之物上拆开或脱离（detach the cable 拔下电缆）；派遣分遣（troops were detached 部队被遣出执行专项任务）。与 attach 反义。
[形] detached / detaching
[例] ① Detach the keyboard before packing the laptop. 装箱前先把键盘拆下来。 ② A small team was detached to handle the emergency. 一支小分队被遣去处理紧急情况。
[搭] detach from 从……脱离, detach oneself from 使自己超然于, detached house 独立式住宅, emotionally detached 情感疏离的
[联] attach 附着, remove 移除, separate 分开, disconnect 断开, isolate 隔离, independent 独立的
[记] detach from 固定搭配。detached 形容词可指「超然、冷淡」或「独立住宅」（英式）

**bind**
英/美 /baɪnd/
v. 捆绑；约束；使结合
[义] To tie something tightly; to force someone to keep a promise or obligation; to stick together physically or metaphorically. 捆扎、系牢（bind the package 捆包裹）；约束（bound by contract 受合同约束）；使紧密结合（shared values bind the team 共同价值观凝聚团队）。过去式 bound /baʊnd/。
[形] bound / bound / binding
[例] ① They bound his hands with rope before questioning him. 问话前他们用绳子捆住了他的双手。 ② The treaty binds both nations to reduce emissions. 条约约束两国减排。
[源] 古英语 *bindan*，日耳曼底层词，本义「捆、系」→ 约束、结合。
[族] bond 纽带, bound 受约束的, binding 有约束力的
[搭] bind together 捆在一起, bind sb. to 约束某人, legally binding 具有法律约束力, bind up 包扎
[联] tie 系, link 链接, connect 连接, unite 联合, loosen 松开, contract 合同, obligation 义务
[记] bound 作形容词 /baʊnd/「必然的、受约束的」；作 bound 过去式同形。bind 与 band、bond 同源

**couple**
英/美 /ˈkʌpl/
v. 连接；结合；使成对
[义] To join or combine two things together; to link one thing with another. 把两物连接或配对（couple A with B 把 A 与 B 配对）；与……结合（coupled with 加上、连同）。名词义「一对、夫妻」见家庭章；本章取动词「连接、结合」义。
[形] coupled / coupling
[例] ① The device couples a sensor with a wireless transmitter. 该装置将传感器与无线发射器相连。 ② High inflation, coupled with slow growth, worried investors. 高通胀加上增长放缓，令投资者担忧。
[搭] couple A with B 把 A 与 B 连接, coupled with 连同, coupling 联轴器, closely coupled 紧密耦合
[联] link 链接, connect 连接, pair 配对, combine 结合, separate 分开, attach 附着, integrate 整合
[记] couple A with B 技术语体常见。coupled with 书面语，表「再加上」。a couple of 表「几个」属数量义，见数字章

**integrate**
英/美 /ˈɪntɪɡreɪt/
v. 整合；融入；使一体化
[义] To combine parts into a whole; to make someone become a full member of a group or society. 将部分合成整体（integrate systems 整合系统）；使融入群体或社会（integrate into the community 融入社区）。强调「纳入同一体系、运转协调」。

v. 与……产生共鸣；契合
[义] To evoke a strong sympathetic response; to feel relevant or meaningful to someone. 观点、故事或信息打动听众、与之产生共鸣（The message resonated with voters. 信息引起选民共鸣, resonate with personal experience 与个人经历呼应）。resonate 动词见感知章。
[例] ① The film's themes resonated with young audiences. 影片主题引发年轻观众共鸣。 ② His apology did not resonate with victims' families. 他的道歉未能打动受害者家属。
[搭] resonate with 与……共鸣/契合, resonate deeply with 深度共鸣, fail to resonate with 未能打动
[联] resonate 共鸣, connect 连接, fall flat 反响平平, audience 受众
[记] resonate with 后接 audience, voters, experience。比 agree with 更强调情感共鸣

**hinge upon**
英/美 /hɪndʒ əˈpɒn/
v. 取决于；以……为枢轴
[义] To depend entirely on something (formal). 结果、成败全系于某因素（Success hinges upon timing. 成败取决于时机, The case hinges upon one witness. 案件系于一名证人）。hinge on 见本章，upon 略正式。
[例] ① The project's fate hinged upon a single vote. 项目命运取决于一票。 ② Recovery hinges upon stable supply chains. 复苏有赖于供应链稳定。
[搭] hinge upon/on 取决于, hinge upon a factor 系于某一因素
[联] hinge on 取决于, depend on 依赖, rest on 基于, decisive 决定性的
[记] hinge upon 与 hinge on 同义，upon 多见于英式正式文体
