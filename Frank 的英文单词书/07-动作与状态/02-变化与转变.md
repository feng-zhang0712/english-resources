# 变化与转变

> 本章收跨领域的「变、成、转、替、渐/骤变」等通用动词与名物。**不**收人际行为语境的 change / adapt / grow（→ 01-05）；**不**收纯数量增减（increase / decrease → 01-05 / 06）；**不**收专章已主归属的词（convert → 06-02，turn 表转向 → 05-03，revolution 表革命 → 02-06，restore 表生态恢复 → 03-05）。

**become**
英/美 /bɪˈkʌm/
v. 变成；成为；适合
[义] To begin to be something; to come to be; to suit or look good on someone. 开始具有某种性质或身份（become a teacher 成为教师，become clear 变得清楚）；亦表「适合、相称」（That colour becomes you 那颜色衬你）。系动词，后接名词或形容词。与 turn 比，become 更强调「逐渐或自然演变成」；与 get 比，become 略正式。
[形] became / become / becoming
[例] ① She became more confident after years of public speaking. 多年公开演讲后，她变得更自信了。 ② It became obvious that the original plan would not work. 原方案行不通，这一点变得很明显。
[搭] become aware of 开始意识到, become clear/evident 变得清楚/明显, become popular 变得流行, become involved 卷入, become accustomed to 习惯于, what became of ……后来怎么了
[联] turn：常指较快的转变，亦表转向, get：口语「变得」, grow：逐渐变成，偏自然过程, remain 仍然是, stay 保持, change 改变（01-05）, transform 彻底转变, develop 逐步发展
[记] 不规则过去式 became。become of sb./sth. 问「后来怎样了」（What became of him?）。becoming 作形容词表「得体的、相称的」

**happen**
英/美 /ˈhæpən/
v. 发生；碰巧
[义] To take place or occur, especially without being planned; to do or be something by chance. 某事出现或进行，常无预谋（Accidents happen 事故难免）；亦表碰巧（I happen to know 我碰巧知道）。不及物，不可说 × happen sth.，须 happen to sb. 或 It happened that…。
[例] ① The meeting happened to fall on my day off, which was convenient. 会议碰巧落在我的休息日，很方便。 ② Whatever happens, we'll support you. 无论发生什么，我们都会支持你。
[搭] happen to sb. 发生在某人身上, happen to do 碰巧做, as it happens 碰巧, whatever happens 无论发生什么, accidents happen 事故难免, shit happens 倒霉事难免（口语）
[联] occur：较正式，常书面, take place：较中性，可指安排好的活动, arise：问题/困难「出现」, happenstance 偶然, coincidence 巧合, unexpected 意外的, planned 有计划的
[记] happen 不可接直接宾语（× happen an accident，用 an accident happened）。happen to know/see 表「碰巧」。happening 名词/old slang 表「活动/事件」

**occur**
英/美 /əˈkɜː(r)/
v. 发生；出现；想到
[义] To happen; to exist or appear in a particular place or situation; to come into someone's mind. 发生（较 happen 正式）；存在于某情境（occur in nature 天然存在）；突然想到（It occurred to me that… 我突然想到……）。不及物。
[例] ① The accident occurred at around midnight on the motorway. 事故约午夜发生在高速公路上。 ② It never occurred to her that he might be lying. 她从没想过他可能在撒谎。
[搭] occur to sb. 某人想到, it occurs to me that 我突然想到, occur in 存在于, occur frequently 频繁发生, naturally occur 天然存在
[联] happen：更口语、更通用, take place：可指安排好的活动, arise：问题、冲突「产生」, strike：突然想到（An idea struck me）, remember 想起, forget 忘记
[记] occur 不可接人作「发生」的主语误用（× sb. occurred an idea，用 an idea occurred to sb.）。occurrence 名词指事件。recur 再次发生

**take place**
英/美 /teɪk pleɪs/
v. 发生；举行
[义] To happen, especially of an organized event or a process that unfolds over time. 发生或进行，尤指已安排的活动或持续过程（The ceremony takes place on Friday 仪式周五举行, Changes take time 改变需要时间）。比 happen 更中性，可暗示「有时间、地点框架」；比 occur 口语化。
[例] ① The conference will take place in Berlin next spring. 会议将于明年春季在柏林举行。 ② Major reforms took place during the first year of the new administration. 新政府执政第一年进行了重大改革。
[搭] take place in/on 在……发生/举行, take place over 在……期间进行, take the place of 取代, give place to 让位于, in the first place 起初
[联] happen 发生, occur 发生（较正式）, hold 举行, host 主办, venue 场地, schedule 安排, cancel 取消, postpone 推迟, replace 取代
[记] take place 无被动（× be taken place）。take the place of 表「取代」，与 take place「发生」勿混。in the first place 表「起初/首先」

**undergo**
英/美 /ˌʌndəˈɡəʊ/
v. 经历；遭受；接受（治疗等）
[义] To experience or be subjected to something, especially a change or difficult process. 经历、承受某种变化或过程，常含「被动承受、过程不易」（undergo surgery 接受手术, undergo a transformation 经历转变, undergo testing 接受检测）。及物，宾语多为 change, test, treatment, shift 等。
[形] underwent / undergone / undergoing
[例] ① The company underwent major restructuring after the merger. 合并后公司经历了重大重组。 ② Patients must undergo several tests before treatment begins. 治疗开始前病人须接受多项检查。
[源] under-（在……下）+ go（走）→ 在过程中「走过一遭」→ 经历。
[族] undertaker 殡葬业者（同 under+go 另一义系，勿混）
[搭] undergo surgery 接受手术, undergo a change/transformation 经历变化/转变, undergo testing 接受检测, undergo training 接受培训, undergo review 接受审查
[联] experience 经历（可中性可主动）, suffer 遭受（多负面）, endure 忍受, go through 经历（口语）, transform 转变, recover 恢复
[记] 不规则过去式 underwent，过去分词 undergone。undergo 宾语不能是人作「被经历者」以外的误用；主语是承受变化的一方

**transform**
英/美 /trænsˈfɔːm/
v. 使转变；改造
n. 变换式（数学/技术）
[义] To change completely the character, appearance, or function of someone or something. 使性质、外观或功能彻底改观（transform a room 改造房间, transform lives 改变人生, transform data 转换数据）。比 change 程度更深，强调「质变」。及物为主。
[形] transformed / transformed / transforming
[例] ① Cheap rent and good light transformed the empty warehouse into an art studio. 低廉租金与良好采光把空仓库改造成了艺术工作室。 ② Technology has transformed how we shop, work, and stay in touch. 科技彻底改变了我们购物、工作与联系的方式。
[源] trans-（跨越）+ form（形状）→ 改换形态 → 转变。
[族] transformation 转变, transformer 变压器, transformative 变革性的
[搭] transform into 转变成, transform the way 改变……的方式, radically transform 彻底改造, transform a space 改造空间, life-transforming 改变人生的
[联] change 改变（较泛）, convert 转换（06-02）, reform 改革（02-06）, reshape 重塑, revolutionize 使革命性变化, preserve 保持原样, stagnate 停滞
[记] transform A into B 固定结构。形容词 transformative 常用于 policy, experience。名词 transformation 可数/不可数均可

**transition**
英/美 /trænˈzɪʃn/
n. 过渡；转变
v. 过渡；转型
[义] The process of changing from one state, stage, or condition to another; to make such a change. 从一种状态、阶段或条件到另一种的过渡过程（a smooth transition 平稳过渡, career transition 职业转型, gender transition 性别过渡）。可数/不可数。动词 transition 较新，多用于正式或专业语境（transition to renewable energy 向可再生能源转型）。
[例] ① The country is in a difficult transition from war to peace. 国家正处于从战争到和平的艰难过渡。 ② She transitioned into management after ten years as an engineer. 做了十年工程师后，她转型进入了管理岗位。
[源] trans-（跨越）+ it（走，*-ire*）+ -ion → 跨步而过 → 过渡。
[族] transitional 过渡期的, transit 过境, transient 短暂的
[搭] smooth transition 平稳过渡, transition period 过渡期, in transition 在过渡中, transition from A to B 从 A 到 B 的过渡, career transition 职业转型
[联] change 变化, shift 转变（02-03）, phase 阶段（03-06）, interim 临时的, gradual 逐渐的, sudden 突然的, stability 稳定, transform 转变
[记] transition from … to … 为常见搭配。transitional 形容词：transitional government 过渡政府。动词 transition 后常接 to/into

**transformation**
英/美 /ˌtrænsfəˈmeɪʃn/
n. 转变；改造
[义] A complete change in someone or something's appearance, character, or nature. 外观、性格或本质的彻底变化（digital transformation 数字化转型, personal transformation 个人蜕变, urban transformation 城市改造）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① The neighborhood has undergone a remarkable transformation in the last decade. 十年来这个街区经历了令人瞩目的转变。 ② Leadership requires a transformation in how teams communicate. 领导力要求团队沟通方式的根本改变。
[源] transform + -ation 名词后缀。
[族] transform 转变, transformative 变革性的, transformer 变压器
[搭] digital transformation 数字化转型, undergo a transformation 经历转变, remarkable transformation 显著转变, physical transformation 外形改变, economic transformation 经济转型
[联] change 变化, reform 改革, revolution 革命（02-06）, makeover 改头换面, shift 转变, renewal 更新（05-01）, stagnation 停滞
[记] 可数/不可数均可。a transformation 指一次具体转变。形容词 transformative 强调「带来质变的力量」

**revert**
英/美 /rɪˈvɜːt/
v. 恢复；回复；还原
[义] To go back to a previous state, practice, or type; to return property or rights to a former owner. 回到先前的状态或做法（revert to type 故态复萌, revert to old habits 恢复旧习惯）；法律上还原（revert to the state 归还国家）。不及物常接 to；及物 revert sth. 较少见。
[例] ① After the holiday, traffic reverted to its usual chaos. 假期过后，交通又恢复了往常的混乱。 ② Without maintenance, the garden quickly reverted to wilderness. 无人打理，花园很快又变回了野地。
[源] re-（回）+ vert（转，*vertere*）→ 转回去 → 恢复。
[族] reverse 反转, inverse 反向的, convert 转换（06-02）, diversion 转移（05-03）
[搭] revert to 恢复到, revert to type 故态复萌, revert to the mean 回归均值, revert to default 恢复默认
[联] return 返回, restore 恢复（03-05 生态义）, relapse 复发/故态复萌, undo 撤销, persist 持续, change 改变, renew 更新
[记] revert to 不可接动名词（× revert to using，口语用 go back to）。revert 在法律/地产语境指「复归原主」。reversion 名词

**invert**
英/美 /ɪnˈvɜːt/
v. 使倒转；使反转
[义] To put something upside down or in the opposite order, position, or relationship. 上下或前后颠倒；顺序或关系反转（invert the bottle 把瓶子倒过来, invert the usual pattern 颠倒常规模式）。及物。形容词 inverse 表「相反的」；名词 inversion 指倒装或反转。
[例] ① Invert the cake onto a plate while it is still warm. 蛋糕尚温时倒扣在盘子上。 ② The chart inverts the usual relationship between cost and quality. 这张图表颠倒了成本与质量之间的常规关系。
[源] in-（向内/反向）+ vert（转）→ 向内翻 → 倒转。
[族] inverse 反向的, inversion 倒装/反转, convert 转换, reverse 反转
[搭] invert the order 颠倒顺序, invert a bottle 倒置瓶子, invert sugar 转化糖（化学）, population inversion 粒子数反转（物理）
[联] reverse 反转/倒退, flip 翻转, upside down 颠倒, mirror 镜像, opposite 相反的, upright 正立的, transpose 转置
[记] 规则动词。inverse relationship 指「反向关系」（一方增另一方减）。inverted commas 英式指引号

**evolve**
英/美 /ɪˈvɒlv/
v. 进化；逐步发展
[义] To develop gradually, especially from a simple to a more complex form; to develop by a process of evolution. 逐步演变、发展（evolve over time 随时间演变, evolve into 演化成, species evolve 物种进化）。不及物为主，亦可及物 evolve a strategy 逐步形成策略。比 develop 更强调「渐进、自然筛选或长期过程」。
[形] evolved / evolved / evolving
[例] ① Language evolves constantly, even if we do not notice it day to day. 语言一直在变，尽管我们日常未必察觉。 ② The startup evolved from a student project into a global platform. 这家初创公司从学生项目逐步发展成全球平台。
[源] e-（出）+ volv（滚，*volvere*）→ 展开、演化 → 进化。
[族] evolution 进化/演变, evolutionary 进化的, involve 涉及（同 volv 系，义不同）
[搭] evolve into 演化成, evolve over time 随时间演变, evolve gradually 逐渐演变, evolve a plan/strategy 逐步形成计划/策略, culturally evolved 文化演化的
[联] develop 发展, adapt 适应（01-05）, transform 彻底转变, mutate 突变, progress 进步（01-05）, stagnate 停滞, species 物种, natural selection 自然选择
[记] evolve 不可用于「进化论」以外的生造（× evolve a instant change）。evolution 在生物学与日常「逐步变化」两义均常见

**evolution**
英/美 /ˌiːvəˈluːʃn/
n. 进化；演变；发展
[义] The gradual development of plants, animals, etc. over millions of years; the gradual development of something. 生物长期演化；亦指观念、制度、产品的逐步发展（the evolution of language 语言的演变, product evolution 产品迭代, theory of evolution 进化论）。不可数为主，可数指「一系列演变阶段」。
[例] ① Darwin's theory of evolution changed how we understand life on Earth. 达尔文的进化论改变了我们对地球生命的理解。 ② We traced the evolution of the policy from draft to final law. 我们追溯了该政策从草案到成法的演变过程。
[源] evolve + -tion；拉丁语 *evolutio*（展开）。
[族] evolve 演变, evolutionary 进化的, revolution 革命（02-06，义近反）
[搭] theory of evolution 进化论, evolution of ……的演变, gradual evolution 逐步演变, product evolution 产品演进, cultural evolution 文化演化
[联] revolution 革命（02-06）, development 发展, progress 进步, adaptation 适应, mutation 突变, transform 转变, stagnation 停滞
[记] 不可数为主。theory of evolution 专指生物进化论。evolution vs. revolution：前者渐进，后者剧变

**develop**
英/美 /dɪˈveləp/
v. 发展；开发；显影；患病
[义] To grow or cause to grow gradually; to build or improve something over time; to begin to have a disease or problem. 逐渐成长或改进（develop a skill 培养技能, develop a product 开发产品）；开始出现问题（develop a fever 发烧, develop cracks 出现裂缝）。及物/不及物均可。比 evolve 更通用、可指人为推动。
[形] developed / developed / developing
[例] ① The team developed a new method for detecting early signs of infection. 团队开发了一种检测感染早期迹象的新方法。 ② Tensions developed between the two departments after the reorganization. 重组后两个部门之间逐渐产生了紧张关系。
[源] de-（去掉/展开）+ velop（包裹，古法语 *veloper*）→  unwrap → 展开 → 发展。
[族] development 发展, developer 开发者, developing 发展中的, developed 发达的
[搭] develop a skill/habit 培养技能/习惯, develop a product 开发产品, develop into 发展成, develop countries 发展中国家, develop symptoms 出现症状
[联] evolve 逐步演变, grow 生长（01-05）, expand 扩大（01-05）, improve 改进（01-04）, stagnate 停滞, decline 衰退（01-06）, create 创造
[记] developing country 发展中国家；developed country 发达国家。develop 可接 disease/symptom/problem 表「开始出现」

**development**
英/美 /dɪˈveləpmənt/
n. 发展；开发；事态进展
[义] The process of growing, improving, or building something; a new event or stage that changes a situation. 成长、改进或建设的过程；改变局势的新进展（economic development 经济发展, child development 儿童发展, the latest developments 最新进展）。不可数/可数。
[例] ① Sustainable development balances growth with environmental protection. 可持续发展在增长与环保之间取得平衡。 ② We will keep you informed of any developments in the case. 案件有任何新进展我们都会通知您。
[搭] economic development 经济发展, child development 儿童发展, sustainable development 可持续发展, latest developments 最新进展, property development 房地产开发, research and development 研发
[联] progress 进步（01-05）, growth 增长, evolution 演变, setback 挫折, advance 推进（01-05）, stagnation 停滞, innovation 创新
[记] 不可数/可数均可。R&D = research and development 研发。development vs. event：前者强调「形成过程或新阶段」

**emerge**
英/美 /ɪˈmɜːdʒ/
v. 出现；浮现；显露
[义] To come out of or appear from somewhere; to become known or visible after being hidden. 从隐藏或不明状态中出现、显露（emerge from 从……中浮现, emerge as 崭露头角为, facts emerge 事实浮出水面）。不及物。强调「原本不可见或不明确，现在进入视野」。
[形] emerged / emerged / emerging
[例] ① New evidence emerged during the second week of the trial. 审判第二周出现了新证据。 ② She emerged as the leading candidate after the first round of voting. 第一轮投票后，她成为领先候选人。
[源] e-（出）+ merg（没入，*mergere*）→ 从淹没中露出 → 浮现。
[族] emergence 出现, emergency 紧急情况（同 merg 系，义转）, merge 合并, submerge 淹没
[搭] emerge from 从……中出现, emerge as 成为/崭露头角, emerge victorious 胜出, newly emerged 新出现的, emerging markets 新兴市场
[联] appear 出现, arise 产生, surface 浮出水面, disappear 消失, reveal 揭示, hide 隐藏, come out 出来
[记] emerge 不可接直接宾语表「使出现」（× emerge evidence，用 evidence emerged）。emerging 形容词：emerging technology 新兴技术

**emergence**
英/美 /ɪˈmɜːdʒəns/
n. 出现；浮现；兴起
[义] The fact of something becoming visible, known, or important after being hidden or absent. 事物在隐藏或不明确之后变得可见、已知或重要（the emergence of AI 人工智能的兴起, emergence of new variants 新变体的出现）。不可数为主。
[例] ① The emergence of social media changed political campaigning forever. 社交媒体的兴起永远改变了政治竞选方式。 ② Scientists study the emergence of drug-resistant bacteria. 科学家研究耐药细菌的出现机制。
[搭] the emergence of ……的兴起/出现, sudden emergence 突然出现, emergence from 从……中浮现
[联] appear 出现, rise 上升, dawn 开端, decline 衰落, disappearance 消失, trend 趋势, breakthrough 突破
[记] 不可数为主。勿与 emergency（紧急情况）混淆：emergence 强调「浮现、兴起」

**arise**
英/美 /əˈraɪz/
v. 产生；出现；起身
[义] To happen, especially of a problem, difficulty, or question; to get up from sleep or a seat (formal/literary). 问题、困难、需求等「产生、出现」（problems arise 问题出现, arise from 源于）；正式/文学义「起身」（arise from one's seat）。不及物。过去式 arose /əˈrəʊz/，过去分词 arisen /əˈrɪzn/。
[形] arose / arisen / arising
[例] ① Misunderstandings often arise when people assume rather than ask. 人们靠猜测而非询问时，误解常常产生。 ② A new challenge has arisen since we last met. 自上次见面以来出现了新挑战。
[源] a-（加强）+ rise（升起）→ 升起 → 出现。
[族] rise 上升, raise 举起（及物，勿混）, arise 与 rise 不同词
[搭] arise from/out of 源于, problems arise 问题出现, should the need arise 如有需要, questions arise 问题产生
[联] occur 发生, happen 发生, emerge 浮现, stem from 源于, result from 因……而产生, disappear 消失, settle 解决
[记] arise 不可接宾语（× arise a problem，用 a problem arose）。arise from 表原因；raise 是及物「举起/提高」。 arise from one's seat 较正式

**adjust**
英/美 /əˈdʒʌst/
v. 调整；适应

## 瓦解与衰退

## 转化与相变

**transmute**
英/美 /trænsˈmjuːteɪt/

[义] To change something into something else of a different nature, especially in a surprising or almost magical way (formal/literary). 使转变成性质不同的事物，常带「惊人、近乎魔法」色彩（transmute lead into gold 点石成金〔喻〕, transmute grief into art 把悲痛化为艺术）。及物。较正式、文学或哲学语境。
[形] transmuted / transmuted / transmuting

[搭] transmute into 转化成, transmute grief into art 化悲痛为艺术, transmutation of ……的转化

[记] transmutation 可数/不可数。transmute 比 transform 更文、更强调「本质跨类改变」。日常多用 transform

**morph**

[义] To change smoothly from one image, shape, or form to another, especially in animation or computing; (linguistics) a form of a word. 尤指动画、计算机图像中平滑变形（One scene morphs into the next 场景渐变切换）；语言学中的词形。口语与技术语境常见，源自 metamorphosis 的缩略。

[联] transform 转变, metamorphose 蜕变, transition 过渡, animation 动画, shape 形状, change 变化
[记] morph 作动词为 20 世纪末来自图形学，现已进入日常。metamorphosis 在动物章（03-01）；morph 取技术/口语「渐变变形」义

**metamorphose**
英/美 /ˌmetəˈmɔːfəʊz/
v. 变态；蜕变
[义] To change completely in form or character, especially as an insect or amphibian develops from larva to adult; to transform profoundly. 形态或性格完全改变，尤指昆虫/两栖类变态（A caterpillar metamorphoses into a butterfly 毛虫化蝶）；广义深刻转变。不及物常接 into；及物亦可。
[形] metamorphosed / metamorphosed / metamorphosing
[例] ① Over weeks, the tadpole metamorphoses into a frog. 数周内，蝌蚪变态为蛙。 ② The quiet town metamorphosed into a busy resort in ten years. 十年间，安静小镇蜕变成繁忙度假区。

v. 过渡到；转型为
[义] To change from one state, role, or system into another, usually in a planned or gradual way. 从一种状态、角色或制度过渡到另一种，通常有计划或较渐进（transition into management 转型做管理, transition into civilian life 过渡到平民生活）。不及物/及物均可。
[例] ① She transitioned into product leadership after years in engineering. 在工程岗位多年后，她转型担任产品负责人。 ② The economy is transitioning into a service-based model. 经济正过渡为以服务为主的模式。
[搭] transition into a role 转型担任角色, transition into civilian life 过渡到平民生活, transition into 过渡到, smoothly transition into 平稳过渡至
[联] transition 过渡, transform into 转变成, shift 转变（02-03）, role 角色, civilian 平民的, service-based 服务型的
[记] transition into 与 transition from A to B 互补。动词 transition 较新，正式文件与 HR 语境常见

**morph from**
英/美 /mɔːf frɒm/
v. 由……演变成
[义] To change gradually from one form or type into another. 从一种形态或类型逐渐变成另一种（morph from a blog into a business 从博客发展成事业, morph from prototype to product 从原型到产品）。不及物/及物均可。与 morph into 成对。
[例] ① The project morphed from a side experiment into the company's main revenue source. 项目从副业实验演变成公司主要收入来源。 ② Over a decade, the town morphed from a fishing village into a resort. 十年间，这座小镇从渔村变成了度假地。
[搭] morph from A into B 从 A 演变成 B, morph from 由……演变, morph from prototype 从原型演变
[联] morph into 演变成, morph 变形, evolve from 由……演化, transform 转变, prototype 原型, revenue 收入
[记] morph from A into/to B 三词框架。from 侧强调起点，into 侧强调终点

**devolve into**
英/美 /dɪˈvɒlv ˈɪntuː/
v. 蜕化为；沦为
[义] To gradually change into a worse or more chaotic state. 逐渐恶化、堕落或陷入混乱（talks devolved into shouting 谈判沦为叫骂, devolve into chaos 陷入混乱）。不及物。较正式，新闻常见。
[例] ① What began as a policy debate devolved into personal attacks. 一场政策辩论逐渐蜕化为人身攻击。 ② The protest devolved into looting after dark. 入夜后，抗议演变成了打砸抢。
[搭] devolve into chaos 陷入混乱, devolve into farce 沦为闹剧, devolve into violence 演变为暴力, devolve into 蜕化为
[联] degenerate 退化（01-01）, descend into 陷入, morph into 演变成, chaos 混乱, violence 暴力, debate 辩论
[记] devolve into 几乎总是负面结果。devolve power（下放权力）是另一义系，勿混

**blossom into**
英/美 /ˈblɒsəm ˈɪntuː/
v. 绽放成；发展成为
[义] To develop into something successful, beautiful, or fully realized in a positive way. 积极地向成功、成熟或充分发展（blossom into an artist 成长为艺术家, a friendship blossomed into love 友谊绽放为爱情）。不及物。
[例] ① The internship blossomed into a full-time job she loved. 实习绽放成她热爱的全职工作。 ② A small online group blossomed into a global community. 一个小网络群组发展成了全球社区。
[搭] blossom into love 发展为爱情, blossom into a career 成长为事业, blossom into 绽放成, friendship blossoms into 友谊发展为
[联] grow into 逐渐变成, mature into 成熟为, flourish 繁荣, friendship 友谊, career 事业, community 社区
[记] blossom into 几乎全为褒义或中性积极。与 devolve into 常成反义对照

**fall into**
英/美 /fɔːl ˈɪntuː/
v. 陷入；分成
[义] To pass into a particular state or category, often without intending to; to be divided into groups. 无意或逐渐进入某种状态（fall into a routine 陷入惯例, fall into decline 陷入衰退）；分成（fall into three categories 分成三类）。不及物。

[搭] fall into a trap 落入陷阱, fall into decline 陷入衰退, fall into place 各就各位, fall into categories 分成类别
[联] slip into 陷入, lapse into 陷入, decline 衰退, trap 陷阱, category 类别, slump 低迷
[记] fall into place 表「 suddenly make sense / 各就各位」，属固定短语。fall into decline/trap 新闻极频

## 边界与渐变

**shade into**
英/美 /ʃeɪd ˈɪntuː/

**bleed into**

[义] To spread gradually into another area, category, or period so that boundaries become unclear. 逐渐渗入另一领域或时段，使边界不清（Work bleeds into weekends 工作侵占周末, genres bleed into each other 类型互相渗透）。不及物。比喻义极频。

[搭] bleed into each other 互相渗透, bleed into weekends 侵占周末, bleed into 渗入, work bleeds into life 工作侵入生活
[联] seep into 渗入（07-05）, blur 模糊, boundary 边界, remote work 远程办公, genre 类型, activism 行动主义
[记] bleed into 无「流血」本义；纯比喻。与 seep into 比，bleed into 更强调「界线被吞没」

## 调整与累积

**wind down**
英/美 /waɪnd daʊn/
v. 逐步缩减；放松
[义] To gradually reduce activity, intensity, or operations until stopping or nearly stopping; to relax after stress. 逐步减少活动或强度直至停止（wind down production 减产, wind down a campaign 逐步结束活动）；放松身心。及物/不及物均可。过去式 wound down /waʊnd/。
[例] ① The factory wound down output before the holiday shutdown. 放假停产前，工厂逐步减产。 ② She wound down with tea and music after the deadline. 截止日期过后，她喝茶听音乐放松下来。
[搭] wind down production 逐步减产, wind down operations 逐步缩减运营, wind down for the day 结束一天的工作, wind down a project 逐步收尾项目
[联] taper off 逐渐减少, phase out 逐步淘汰（03-05）, ramp up 加快提升, relax 放松, production 生产, campaign 活动
[记] wind down vs. ramp up 常成对。wind down 亦指「身心放松」，与 business 义并存

**even out**
英/美 /ˈiːvn aʊt/
v. 趋平；拉平
[义] To become more equal, stable, or smooth over time; to remove unevenness. 随时间变得更均等、稳定或平滑（Prices evened out 价格趋稳, even out differences 拉平差异）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① Demand usually evens out after the initial rush. 最初抢购过后，需求通常会趋平。 ② The coach tried to even out playing time among the squad. 教练试图让队员上场时间更平均。
[搭] even out over time 随时间趋平, even out differences 拉平差异, even out 趋稳, even out the surface 把表面弄平
[联] level off 趋平, stabilize 稳定, balance 平衡, difference 差异, demand 需求, smooth 平滑的
[记] even out 与 level off 近义；even out 更口语。even out 亦可指物理「弄平表面」

**smooth out**
英/美 /smuːð aʊt/
v. 弄平；消除波折
[义] To remove problems, wrinkles, or irregularities; to make a process run more easily. 消除波折、皱纹或不规则处（smooth out wrinkles 抚平皱纹, smooth out logistics 理顺物流）。及物。
[例] ① Extra staff helped smooth out delays at the border. 增派人员有助于消除边境延误。 ② They smoothed out the wording before signing the contract. 签约前，他们理顺了措辞上的分歧。
[搭] smooth out wrinkles 抚平皱纹, smooth out problems 消除问题, smooth out logistics 理顺物流, smooth out differences 消除分歧
[联] iron out 消除（分歧）, even out 趋平, wrinkle 皱纹, logistics 物流, delay 延误, difference 分歧
[记] smooth out 与 iron out 常可互换指「消除分歧/问题」；smooth out 略偏「使流程顺滑」

**ratchet up**
英/美 /ˈrætʃɪt ʌp/
v. 逐步收紧；层层加码
[义] To increase something gradually in a series of steps, often so that it cannot easily be reversed. 分步骤逐步提高，且往往难以退回（ratchet up pressure 层层加压, ratchet up sanctions 逐步加码制裁）。及物。新闻与政治语境极频。
[例] ① The central bank ratcheted up interest rates three times this year. 央行今年三度加息。 ② Campaigns ratchet up rhetoric as election day nears. 选战临近，各阵营言辞层层加码。
[搭] ratchet up pressure 层层加压, ratchet up sanctions 加码制裁, ratchet up tensions 加剧紧张, ratchet up costs 成本逐级上升
[联] ramp up 加快提升, escalate 升级（02-08）, tighten 收紧, pressure 压力, sanction 制裁, rhetoric 言辞
[记] ratchet 本义「棘轮」——只能单向卡紧。ratchet up 暗含「只升不降」

**step up**
英/美 /step ʌp/
v. 加强；加紧
[义] To increase effort, intensity, or responsibility; to take on a more important role. 加大力度、强度或责任（step up efforts 加大力度, step up security 加强安保）；承担更重要角色。不及物/及物均可。
[例] ① Health agencies stepped up testing during the outbreak. 疫情期间，卫生机构加强了检测。 ② She stepped up as team lead when her manager fell ill. 经理生病时，她挺身而出担任组长。
[搭] step up efforts 加大力度, step up security 加强安保, step up to the plate 挺身而出, step up production 增产
[联] ramp up 加快提升, ratchet up 层层加码, intensify 加强（06-04）, effort 努力, security 安保, lead 负责人
[记] step up to the plate 棒球比喻「关键时刻站出来」。step up 比 ramp up 更通用、略少「单向棘轮」色彩

## 形态与质变

**curdle**
英/美 /ˈkɜːdl/
v. 凝结；变味
[义] (Of milk) to separate into solids and liquid; (figurative) to turn sour or unpleasant. （牛奶）凝结；比喻关系、情绪「变味」（Milk curdled 牛奶结块, affection curdled into resentment 感情变味成怨恨）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① Leave lemon juice in hot milk and it will curdle almost instantly. 热牛奶里加柠檬汁会几乎立刻结块。 ② Trust curdled after repeated broken promises. 一再食言后，信任变味了。
[搭] curdle milk 使牛奶凝结, curdle into resentment 变味成怨恨, curdled milk 结块的奶, curdle relations 使关系变味
[联] sour 变酸, spoil 变质, resentment 怨恨, trust 信任, milk 牛奶, separate 分离
[记] curdle into 比喻义文学/评论常见。curdled 形容词：curdled milk / curdled affection

**transfigure**
英/美 /trænsˈfɪɡə(r)/
v. 改观；使焕容
[义] To change the appearance or character of something so that it looks much more beautiful or noble; (in religious contexts) to transform spiritually. 使外观或气质大为改观，显得更美或更高尚；宗教语境「显圣/变容」。及物。较正式、文学色彩。
[形] transfigured / transfigured / transfiguring
[例] ① Morning light transfigured the ordinary street into something almost magical. 晨光把寻常街道映得近乎神奇。 ② The role transfigured her public image within a year. 这个角色一年内改写了她的公众形象。
[源] trans- + figure（形）→ 改换形貌 → 改观。
[族] transfiguration 变容/改观, figure 形象
[搭] transfigure the landscape 使风景改观, transfigured by light 被光线照亮而改观, transfigure one's image 改写形象
[联] transform 转变, illuminate 照亮, appearance 外观, magical 神奇的, image 形象, noble 高尚的
[记] Transfiguration 专指基督教「耶稣变容」。日常比喻义读「改观、焕发光彩」即可

**crystallization**
英/美 /ˌkrɪstəlaɪˈzeɪʃn/
n. 结晶；明确化
[义] The process of forming crystals; the process of ideas or plans becoming clear and definite. 形成晶体；想法或计划变得清晰明确（crystallization of ideas 思路成型, sudden crystallization 突然明确化）。不可数/可数。
[例] ① Slow crystallization produces larger, clearer salt crystals. 缓慢结晶可形成更大更透的盐晶体。 ② The report marks the crystallization of months of debate. 这份报告标志着数月辩论的思路成型。
[源] crystal（晶体）+ -ization → 结晶化。
[族] crystallize 结晶/明确化, crystal 晶体, crystalline 结晶的
[搭] crystallization of ideas 思路成型, slow crystallization 缓慢结晶, sudden crystallization 骤然明确
[联] crystallize 结晶, clarify 澄清, solidify 巩固, idea 想法, debate 辩论, crystal 晶体
[记] 比喻义 crystallization of a plan/policy 学术与政策写作常见。动词 crystallize 见本章同条

**ferment**
英/美 /fəˈment/
v. 发酵；酝酿
n. 发酵；动荡
[义] To undergo chemical change because of bacteria or yeast; (figurative) to develop in a turbulent or exciting way. 因细菌/酵母而发酵；比喻社会变革、情绪「酝酿」（Ideas fermented 想法酝酿, a period of ferment 动荡期）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① Grapes ferment naturally if yeast is present on the skin. 果皮上有酵母时，葡萄会自然发酵。 ② Dissent fermented for years before the protests broke out. 抗议爆发前，不满已酝酿多年。
[源] 拉丁语 *fervere*（沸腾）→ 发酵时冒泡 → 发酵/酝酿。
[族] fermentation 发酵, fermenting 发酵中的
[搭] ferment change 酝酿变革, a period of ferment 动荡期, ferment grapes 发酵葡萄, ferment unrest 酝酿动荡
[联] brew 酝酿, fester 化脓恶化, change 变化, unrest 动荡, yeast 酵母, protest 抗议
[记] 名词 a ferment 指「发酵过程」或「社会动荡」。ferment vs. fester：后者偏「恶化化脓」，前者偏「酝酿待发」

**transition from**
英/美 /trænˈzɪʃn frɒm/

[义] To change from one state, system, or role to another, usually in stages. 从一种状态、制度或角色过渡到另一种，通常分阶段（transition from print to digital 从纸质过渡到数字, transition from student to worker 从学生过渡到职场人）。不及物/及物均可。

[搭] transition from A to B 从 A 过渡到 B, transition from print to digital 从纸质到数字, transition from 从……过渡
[联] transition into 过渡到, transition 过渡, shift from 从……转变, digital 数字的, renewable 可再生的, staff 正式的
[记] transition from A to B 比 transition into 更常明示两端。HR 与政策文件极频

**degenerate into**

[义] To become worse in quality, morals, or order; to pass into a lower or more unpleasant state. 品质、道德或秩序变坏，沦为更差状态（debate degenerated into abuse 辩论沦为辱骂, degenerate into chaos 陷入混乱）。不及物。

[搭] degenerate into chaos 陷入混乱, degenerate into violence 沦为暴力, degenerate into farce 沦为闹剧, degenerate into 退化为
[联] devolve into 蜕化为, descend into 陷入, degenerate 退化（01-01）, chaos 混乱, violence 暴力, fraud 欺诈
[记] degenerate into 几乎总接负面结果。形容词 degenerate /dɪˈdʒenərət/ 与动词 /dɪˈdʒenəreɪt/ 重音不同

**descend into**

[联] devolve into 蜕化为, degenerate into 退化为, fall into 陷入, chaos 混乱, poverty 贫困, conflict 冲突
[记] descend into 新闻标题极频。descend 本义「下降」，比喻「局势恶化」

**burst into**

v. 降温；冷静下来
[义] To become cooler; to become calmer or less angry or excited. 温度降低；情绪或局势冷静下来（Let it cool down 让它冷却一下, tensions cooled down 紧张缓和）。及物/不及物均可。与 heat up 相对。
[例] ① Leave the pan to cool down before you wash it. 洗之前让锅先冷却。 ② Both sides agreed to cool down the rhetoric after the summit. 峰会后，双方同意收敛言辞、给局势降温。
[搭] cool down before 在……之前冷却, cool down tensions 给紧张局势降温, let sb. cool down 让某人冷静, cool down quickly 迅速降温
[联] heat up 升温, simmer down 平息, calm down 平静下来, tension 紧张, rhetoric 言辞, anger 愤怒
[记] cool down 比喻义极频。cool down vs. calm down：cool down 可指物降温，calm down 多指人

**slow down**
英/美 /sləʊ daʊn/
v. 放慢；减缓
[义] To go or make something go more slowly; to become less active or intense. 变慢或使变慢；活动或强度减弱（Slow down 慢点, the economy slowed down 经济放缓）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① Doctors told him to slow down after the heart scare. 虚惊一场后，医生让他放慢节奏。 ② Growth slowed down in the second half of the year. 下半年增长放缓。
[搭] slow down growth 放缓增长, slow down production 减产, slow down before 在……前减速, slow down dramatically 大幅放缓
[联] speed up 加快, decelerate 减速, stall 停滞, growth 增长, production 生产, economy 经济
[记] slow down vs. grind to a halt：前者仍可能继续，后者几乎完全停住

**speed up**
英/美 /spiːd ʌp/
v. 加快；提速
[义] To move or happen more quickly; to make something happen faster. 移动或发生更快；使加速（speed up the process 加快流程, time seems to speed up 时间似乎变快）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① The council speeded up approvals for emergency repairs. 议会加快了应急维修审批。 ② As the deadline neared, everything seemed to speed up. 临近截止日期，一切似乎都在加速。
[搭] speed up the process 加快流程, speed up production 加速生产, speed up dramatically 大幅提速, speed up recovery 加速恢复
[联] slow down 放慢, accelerate 加速（01-05 / 05-01）, hurry 匆忙, process 流程, production 生产, deadline 截止日期
[记] 过去式英式 speeded up，美式亦见 sped up。speed up 与 accelerate 近义，speed up 更口语

## 封顶与补全

**level out**
英/美 /ˈlevl aʊt/
v. 趋平；持平
[义] To stop rising or falling and become steady at a particular level. 停止升降并在某一水平趋于稳定（Prices levelled out 价格趋平, growth levels out 增长趋稳）。不及物。与 level off 近义，level out 英式更常见。
[例] ① After a sharp rise, inflation finally levelled out last quarter. 急涨之后，通胀上季度终于趋平。 ② Learning curves steep at first, then usually level out. 学习曲线起初很陡，随后通常会趋平。
[搭] level out at 在……水平趋平, prices level out 价格趋平, level out after 在……之后趋稳, growth levels out 增长趋稳
[联] level off 趋平, even out 趋平, stabilize 稳定, plateau 进入平台期（03-03）, inflation 通胀, growth 增长
[记] level out / level off 常可互换。level out 作不及物，无 level out sth.

**max out**
英/美 /mæks aʊt/
v. 达到上限；刷满
[义] To reach the maximum limit of something, especially a credit card, capacity, or effort (informal). 达到某事物的上限，尤指信用卡额度、容量或努力（max out a credit card 刷爆卡, max out capacity 满负荷）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① They maxed out the venue at five hundred attendees. 会场五百人上限已满。 ② She maxed out her savings on the down payment. 她把积蓄全砸在首付上了。
[搭] max out a credit card 刷爆信用卡, max out capacity 满负荷, max out at 最高达, max out effort 尽力到顶
[联] peak 达到峰值, limit 限制, capacity 容量, credit card 信用卡, savings 储蓄, effort 努力
[记] max out 极口语。maxed out 形容词：maxed-out credit cards / a maxed-out team

**round out**
英/美 /raʊnd aʊt/
v. 完善；凑整
[义] To complete or improve something by adding what is missing; to make fuller or more balanced. 通过补足所缺而使完整或更均衡（round out the team 补齐团队, round out an education 完善教育）。及物。
[例] ① An internship rounded out her degree with practical experience. 实习用实践经验完善了她的学位。 ② The final chapter rounds out the argument with case studies. 最后一章用案例收束论证。
[搭] round out the team 补齐团队, round out an education 完善教育, round out the picture 补全图景, round out with 以……收尾
[联] complete 完成, complement 补充, balance 平衡, experience 经验, team 团队, education 教育
[记] round out vs. round off：round off 多指「把棱角修圆/收尾」，round out 偏「使完整丰满」

**fill out**
英/美 /fɪl aʊt/
v. 填完；丰满起来
[义] To complete a form; to become fuller in shape or more complete in detail. 填写表格；在形状上变丰满或细节更完整（fill out a form 填表, the story filled out 故事更充实了）。及物/不及物均可。
[例] ① Please fill out the application before the interview. 面试前请填好申请表。 ② As more data arrived, the picture filled out. 随着更多数据到来，图景更完整了。
[搭] fill out a form 填表, fill out details 补全细节, fill out over time 随时间充实, fill out the picture 补全图景
[联] complete 完成, expand 扩展, form 表格, detail 细节, application 申请, data 数据
[记] fill out a form 美式；英式常 fill in a form。fill out 不及物表「变丰满/变完整」

## 关闭与失效

**close down**
英/美 /kləʊz daʊn/
v. 关闭；停业
[义] To stop operating permanently or for a long period, especially of a business or facility. 永久或长期停止运营，尤指企业或设施（close down a factory 关闭工厂, shops closed down 店铺停业）。及物/不及物均可。与 shut down 近义，close down 更偏「结业、关张」。
[例] ① Several local papers closed down during the digital shift. 数字化转型期间，几家地方报纸停刊了。 ② Authorities closed down the site for safety violations. 当局因安全违规关闭了该工地。
[搭] close down a factory 关闭工厂, close down permanently 永久关闭, close down operations 停止运营, close down for good 彻底关门
[联] shut down 关停, wind down 逐步缩减, reopen 重新开放, factory 工厂, operation 运营, permanently 永久地
[记] close down vs. shut down：close down 更常指企业「关张」，shut down 亦指系统「宕机」

**give out**
英/美 /ɡɪv aʊt/
v. 耗尽；停止运转
[义] To stop working or functioning; to become used up; to distribute. 停止运转；被用完；分发（The engine gave out 引擎坏了, supplies gave out 补给耗尽）。不及物。与 give off（散发）勿混。
[例] ① Her knees gave out halfway through the marathon. 马拉松跑到一半，她的膝盖就不行了。 ② Food supplies gave out before relief arrived. 救援到达前，食物已经耗尽。
[搭] give out under 在……下撑不住, supplies give out 补给耗尽, engine gives out 引擎坏了, give out completely 完全失效
[联] wear out 耗尽, run out 用完, break down 出故障, supply 补给, engine 引擎, marathon 马拉松
[记] give out 不及物表「失效/耗尽」。give out information 为另一义「发布信息」

**implode**
英/美 /ɪmˈpləʊd/
v. 内爆；崩溃
[义] To collapse inward violently; (figurative) to fail suddenly and completely from internal pressure. 向内猛烈 collapse；（比喻）因内部压力突然彻底崩溃（a company imploded 公司内爆, the market imploded 市场崩盘自内）。不及物/及物均可。
[例] ① The fund imploded after hidden losses came to light. 隐性损失曝光后，基金瞬间崩溃。 ② Trust imploded once the cover-up was confirmed. 掩盖被证实后，信任瞬间崩塌。
[源] im-（向内）+ plod（爆炸，*plodere*）→ 向内爆 → 内爆。
[族] implode vs. explode 外爆, implosion 内爆
[搭] implode under pressure 在压力下崩溃, company implodes 公司内爆, trust implodes 信任崩塌, market implodes 市场自内崩盘
[联] collapse 崩溃, explode 外爆, burst 爆裂, trust 信任, fund 基金, pressure 压力
[记] implode vs. explode：im- 向内，ex- 向外。比喻义新闻极频，不必先想物理内爆

## 溢出与转折

**overflow**
英/美 /ˌəʊvəˈfləʊ/
v. 溢出；满溢
n. 溢出；溢流口
[义] To flow over the edges of a container; to have more of something than can be contained or dealt with. 从容器边缘溢出；某物多到装不下或处理不了（The river overflowed 河水泛滥, overflow with emotion 情感满溢）。不及物/及物均可。
[例] ① The clinic overflowed with patients after the outbreak was announced. 疫情公布后，诊所里患者人满为患。 ② Joy overflowed when she saw her family at the airport. 在机场见到家人，她喜悦满溢。
[搭] overflow with 充满/满溢, river overflows 河水泛滥, overflow into 溢出到, overflow crowd 超员人群
[联] spill over 溢出（07-05）, flood 泛滥, boil over 沸溢, emotion 情感, river 河流, crowd 人群
[记] overflow with emotion/joy 固定搭配。overflow 名词亦指计算机「缓冲区溢出」

**turn the tide**
英/美 /tɜːn ðə taɪd/
v. 扭转局势
[义] To change a situation from one direction to the opposite, especially from losing to winning or from bad to good. 使局势从一端转向相反方向，尤指由劣转优（turn the tide of war 扭转战局, turn the tide against 使形势对……不利）。及物。
[例] ① Early victories helped turn the tide of the campaign. 早期胜利帮助扭转了选战局势。 ② Public outrage turned the tide against the proposed cuts. 公众愤怒使拟议的削减方案形势逆转。
[搭] turn the tide of war 扭转战局, turn the tide against 使形势对……不利, turn the tide in favour of 使形势有利于
[联] reverse 反转, shift 转变, turn around 扭转, campaign 选战, war 战争, victory 胜利
[记] turn the tide 不可分写。the tide 喻「大势、潮流」，非仅海水潮汐

**shift gears**
英/美 /ʃɪft ɡɪəz/
v. 换挡；转变策略
[义] To change the way you are doing something; (literally) to change to a different gear in a vehicle. 改变做事方式；（字面）汽车换挡（shift gears mid-project 项目中途改策略）。不及物/及物均可。美式拼 gears。
[例] ① After poor early results, the team shifted gears and focused on retention. 早期结果不佳后，团队转变策略，聚焦留存。 ② She shifted gears smoothly from editing to management. 她从编辑转向管理，过渡很顺。
[搭] shift gears mid-project 中途改策略, shift gears to 转向, shift gears quickly 快速换挡/转向, shift gears into 转入
[联] change tack 改变策略, pivot 转向, transition 过渡, strategy 策略, focus 聚焦, management 管理
[记] 比喻义极频。shift gears into + 动名词/名词：shift gears into growth mode

**change tack**
英/美 /tʃeɪndʒ tæk/
v. 改变策略
[义] To start using a different method or policy in order to achieve something. 改用不同方法或政策以达成目标（change tack on immigration 调整移民政策方针, change tack midstream 中途改弦更张）。不及物/及物均可。英式较常见。
[例] ① The startup changed tack when enterprise clients showed more interest than consumers. 企业客户比消费者更有兴趣后，初创公司调整了策略。 ② Critics urged the government to change tack on housing policy. 批评者敦促政府调整住房政策方向。
[搭] change tack on 在……上改策略, change tack midstream 中途改弦更张, change tack completely 彻底转向, change tack when 当……时改策略
[联] shift gears 转变策略, pivot 转向, policy 政策, strategy 策略, immigration 移民, housing 住房
[记] tack 本义航海「航向/帆角」→ 策略。change tack vs. U-turn：后者更强调「180 度急转」

## 转变短语（续八）

**mutate from**
英/美 /mjuːˈteɪt frɒm/
v. 由……突变而来
[义] To develop as a changed form from an earlier version or source, especially through mutation. 从早期版本或来源经突变/演变而来（mutate from an earlier strain 由早期毒株突变而来）。不及物/及物均可。与 mutate into 成对。
[例] ① The variant may have mutated from a strain circulating last winter. 该变体可能由去年冬季流行的毒株突变而来。 ② The feature mutated from an experimental side project. 该功能由一项实验性副业项目演变而来。
[搭] mutate from a strain 由毒株突变而来, mutate from an earlier version 由早期版本演变, mutate from 由……突变/演变
[联] mutate into 突变成, mutate 突变, evolve from 由……演化, variant 变体, strain 毒株, version 版本
[记] mutate from 强调溯源；mutate into 强调结果形态。医学与产品迭代语境均见

**solidify into**
英/美 /səˈlɪdɪfaɪ ˈɪntuː/
v. 巩固为；凝固成
[义] To become or make something firm, stable, or definite; to change into a solid form. 变得或使变得 firm、稳定或明确；凝固为固体（solidify into a habit 固化为习惯, alliances solidified into treaties 联盟巩固为条约）。及物/不及物均可。与 solidify 词条互补。
[例] ① Informal cooperation solidified into a formal partnership. 非正式合作巩固为正式合伙关系。 ② Lava solidifies into rock as it cools. 岩浆冷却时凝固为岩石。
[搭] solidify into a habit 固化为习惯, solidify into 巩固为, solidify into rock 凝固为岩石, solidify into law 固化为法律
[联] solidify 巩固, crystallize 明确化, harden 变硬, habit 习惯, partnership 合伙, treaty 条约
[记] solidify into 比喻义偏「由软到硬、由虚到实」。物理义「凝固为 rock」亦常见

## 突发开始

**break out**
英/美 /breɪk aʊt/
v. 爆发；突然发生
[义] To start suddenly, especially of war, disease, fire, or strong emotion; to escape from a place. 突然开始，尤指战争、疾病、火灾或强烈情绪（War broke out 战争爆发, break out in a rash 突然起疹）；逃脱。不及物。
[例] ① Fighting broke out along the border overnight. 边境一夜之间爆发冲突。 ② She broke out in a cold sweat when she heard the news. 听到消息，她冒出一身冷汗。
[搭] break out in war 爆发战争, break out in a rash/sweat 起疹/冒冷汗, break out of 逃出, fire breaks out 起火
[联] erupt into 爆发为, flare up 突然加剧, outbreak 爆发（名词）, war 战争, fighting 冲突, rash 皮疹
[记] break out 不可接宾语（× break out a war，用 war broke out）。break out of 另义「逃出」
