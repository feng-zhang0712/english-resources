# 因果与影响

> 本章收跨领域的因果框架词：因—果名物与动词、影响、引发、必然推出等。**不**收人际礼仪行为链（apologize, persuade → 行为与动作章）；**不**收纯统计/逻辑专论（correlation, entailment 作逻辑术语 → 认知与思维章）；**不**收阻断类动词详编（prevent, hinder → 行为与动作章已有）。*cause* / *effect* / *affect* 主轴词归本章。

**cause**
英/美 /kɔːz/
n. 原因；事业
v. 引起；导致
[义] As a noun: something that makes an event or situation happen; also a principle or organization one supports (a good cause 正义事业). As a verb: to make something happen, especially something bad or unwanted (cause damage 造成损害, cause sb. to do sth. 致使某人做某事). 因果链上的「因」——比 reason 更绑客观发生，比 because 为名词/动词而非连词。与 correlate 不同：相关不等于因果。
[形] causes / caused / caused / causing
[例] ① Heavy rain was the main cause of the flooding. 暴雨是洪水的主要原因。 ② The new policy may cause more harm than good. 新政策可能弊大于利。 ③ She devoted her life to a cause she believed in. 她将一生奉献于所信仰的事业。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *causa*（原因、案件、诉讼）→ 法语 *cause* → 英语。连词 because 同根（by + cause）。
[族] because 因为, causal 因果的, causation 因果关系
[搭] the cause of ……的原因, cause damage 造成损害, cause sb. to do 导致某人做, root cause 根本原因, cause for concern 令人担忧的原因, cause and effect 因果, panic buying caused shortages 恐慌性抢购造成短缺
[联] reason 理由（偏解释）：effect 结果, consequence 后果, trigger 触发, lead to 导致, result in 导致, correlate 相关（≠因果）, prevent 阻止, affect /əˈfekt/：动词「影响」，拼写易混
[记] cause of + 名词；cause sb. to do（不用 cause sb. do）。cause for concern 极常用。区分 cause（因）与 correlation（相关）：A correlates with B 不必是 A causes B

**effect**
英/美 /ɪˈfekt/
n. 效果；影响；效应
v. 实现；使发生（正式）
[义] As a noun: a change that is a result of an action or other cause (the effect of ……的影响, have an effect on 对……产生影响). 可数指具体效果（side effects 副作用）；不可数可表效力（in effect 实际上, take effect 生效）。动词 effect change 表「使变革生效」，书面正式。与 cause 成对；与 affect /əˈfekt/（动词，影响）拼写易混。
[形] effects（复数）, effecting, effected
[例] ① The new law had an immediate effect on housing prices. 新法对房价立竿见影。 ② The medicine takes effect within thirty minutes. 药在三十分钟内起效。 ③ The board voted to effect major reforms. 董事会投票决定实施重大改革。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *efficere*（做出、完成），ex-（出）+ facere（做）→ 做出来 → 效果。与 affect, efficient, defect 同 facere 词根。
[族] affect 影响（动词）, effective 有效的, efficiency 效率
[搭] the effect of/on ……的影响, side effects 副作用, in effect 实际上, take effect 生效, effect change 实现变革, cause and effect 因果
[联] impact 影响（较正式）：result 结果, outcome 结果, consequence 后果, cause 原因, affect /əˈfekt/：动词「影响」，勿与 effect 混淆, ineffective 无效的
[记] effect on + 对象；take effect 表「生效」。名词 effect vs. 动词 affect：The drought affected crops; the effect was severe. 区分 in effect（实际上）与 in affect（几乎不用）

**affect**
英/美 /əˈfekt/
v. 影响；打动；（疾病）侵袭
[义] To produce a change in someone or something; to move someone emotionally; (of a disease) to attack. 使某人或某物产生变化（affect the economy 影响经济）；打动情感（deeply affected 深受触动）；疾病侵袭（affect the lungs 累及肺部）。动词，重音在第二音节。与 effect /ɪˈfekt/（名词，效果）为经典易混对；与 infect 不同，affect 不强调病原体传播。
[形] affects / affected / affected / affecting；形容词 affected 做作的
[例] ① Rising interest rates will affect millions of homeowners. 加息将影响数百万房主。 ② The documentary affected her so much that she decided to volunteer. 纪录片深深打动了她，她决定去当志愿者。 ③ The condition can affect people of any age. 这种病任何年龄的人都可能得。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *afficere*（作用于），ad-（向）+ facere（做）→ 对……做出作用 → 影响。与 effect, infect, perfect 同 facere 词根。
[族] effect 效果, affection 喜爱, affectation 做作
[搭] affect the economy 影响经济, deeply affected 深受触动, adversely affect 不利影响, affect sb.'s decision 影响某人的决定, be affected by 受……影响
[联] effect /ɪˈfekt/：名词「效果/结果」，拼写易混, influence 影响（较温和、可指人）, impact 冲击性影响, alter 改变, infect 感染, move 打动, unaffected 未受影响的
[记] affect 作动词重音 /əˈfekt/；名词 affect /ˈæfekt/ 指「情感」（心理学），本章不展开。常用被动 be affected by。区分 affect（影响）与 effect（作动词时表「实现」，极正式）

**because**
英/美 /bɪˈkɒz/ 美亦 /bɪˈkɑːz/
conj. 因为
[义] For the reason that; due to the fact that. 引出原因从句（because it was raining 因为在下雨, because of the rain 因为下雨）。口语极常见；比 since / as 更直接点明因果。because of + 名词；because + 从句。
[例] ① We stayed home because it was raining. 因为下雨，我们待在了家。 ② Because of the strike, buses were cancelled. 因罢工，公交停运了。
[搭] because of 因为（后接名词）, just because 仅仅因为, because I said so 因为我这么说的（口语）, not because … but because 不是因为……而是因为
[联] since 既然/因为（略书面）, as 由于, due to 由于, owing to 由于, therefore 因此, so 所以, cause 原因（名词）
[记] because of + 名词；because + 主谓从句。just because … doesn't mean … 口语反驳结构。because 不可接名词（用 because of）

**causal**
英/美 /ˈkɔːzl/
adj. 因果的；原因的
[义] Relating to or acting as a cause; involving cause and effect. 与原因或因果关系有关的（causal link 因果联系, causal relationship 因果关系, causal factor 成因）。学术、新闻、研究语境常见。名词 causality 表「因果性」。
[例] ① Researchers found a causal link between smoking and lung disease. 研究者发现吸烟与肺病之间存在因果联系。 ② Correlation does not prove a causal relationship. 相关并不能证明存在因果关系。
[源] 拉丁语 *causa*（原因）+ -al → causal「与原因有关的」。
[族] cause 原因, causation 因果关系, causality 因果性, because 因为
[搭] causal link 因果联系, causal relationship 因果关系, causal factor 成因, causal connection 因果关联, establish causal 确立因果
[联] correlation 相关（≠因果）, consequential 作为结果的, resulting 由此产生的, underlying 根本的, direct 直接的, indirect 间接的
[记] causal vs. casual /ˈkæʒuəl/（随便的）发音相近，拼写勿混。causal relationship 为学术固定搭配

**result**
英/美 /rɪˈzʌlt/
n. 结果；成果
v. 导致；起因于
[义] Something that happens because of something else; the final score or outcome of a contest. 因他事而发生的情况（the result of ……的结果）；比赛最终比分。动词 result in 导致（后接结果），result from 起因于（后接原因）——主动语态主语为「因」或「果」依介词而变。比 consequence 更中性，比 outcome 可指具体得分。
[形] results, resulted, resulted, resulting
[例] ① The accident was the result of careless driving. 事故是粗心驾驶造成的。 ② The policy resulted in lower emissions. 该政策导致了排放下降。 ③ Disputes often result from poor communication. 纠纷常因沟通不畅而起。
[源] 拉丁语 *resultare*（跳回、弹回），re-（回）+ *saltare*（跳）→ 回落 → 结果。
[族] resultant 由此产生的（正式）
[搭] the result of ……的结果, result in 导致, result from 起因于, as a result 因此, end result 最终结果, end in a result 以比分结束（体育）
[联] outcome 结果（偏最终）, consequence 后果（常含评价）, effect 效果, cause 原因, lead to 导致, stem from 源于, yield 产生
[记] result in + 结果（主语是原因）；result from + 原因（主语是结果）。as a result 后常接逗号接完整句。result vs. consequence：result 中性；consequence 常暗示好坏评价

**consequence**
英/美 /ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/ 美 /ˈkɑːnsəkwens/
n. 后果；结果；重要性
[义] A result of an action or condition, often one that is unwelcome or unpleasant; importance (of little consequence 无关紧要). 行动或状况的结果，常含负面或评价色彩（face the consequences 承担后果, serious consequences 严重后果）；亦表「重要性」。可数。比 result 更强调「随之而来的（尤指负面）结局」。
[例] ① He did not consider the consequences of quitting without another job. 他没考虑没找下家就辞职的后果。 ② The decision is of great consequence for the whole industry. 这一决定对全行业至关重要。
[源] 拉丁语 *consequentia*（跟随），*consequi*（跟随）→ 紧随其后 → 后果。
[族] consequent 随之发生的, consequently 因此, consequential 重要的
[搭] face the consequences 承担后果, serious consequences 严重后果, as a consequence 因此, of little consequence 无关紧要, logical consequence 逻辑后承
[联] result 结果（更中性）, outcome 结局, repercussion 余波, aftermath 后果, effect 效果, punishment 惩罚, inevitable 不可避免的
[记] 可数：a consequence。consequence of + 名词/动名词。as a consequence / in consequence 表「因此」，偏书面

**outcome**
英/美 /ˈaʊtkʌm/
n. 结果；结局
[义] The way a situation, process, or event ends; the final result. 局势、过程或事件的最终结局（positive outcome 好结果, uncertain outcome 结局未定, election outcome 选举结果）。可数。比 result 更指「过程结束时的最终状态」，比 consequence 更中性、少负面预设。
[例] ① We won't know the outcome of the trial until next week. 审判结果要到下周才知道。 ② Early treatment improves the outcome for most patients. 早期治疗改善大多数患者的预后。
[搭] positive outcome 好结果, final outcome 最终结果, uncertain outcome 结局未定, election outcome 选举结果, likely outcome 可能的结果
[联] result 结果, consequence 后果, effect 效果, ending 结局, resolution 解决, prognosis 预后, verdict 裁决
[记] 可数：an outcome。outcome of + 事件/过程。outcome vs. result：outcome 强调「最终怎样了」；result 可指中间产物或比赛比分

**impact**
英/美 /ˈɪmpækt/
n. 影响；冲击
v. 冲击；对……产生影响
[义] The powerful effect that something has on a situation or person; to have an effect on. 对局势或人的强烈作用（have an impact on 对……产生影响, environmental impact 环境影响）。名词重音在第一音节 /ˈɪmpækt/；动词重音常在第二音节 /ɪmˈpækt/，但名词义亦常见。比 influence 更强调「力度、冲击感」。
[形] impacts, impacted, impacted, impacting
[例] ① The pandemic had a huge impact on small businesses. 疫情对小微企业冲击巨大。 ② New tariffs will impact exports to Europe. 新关税将影响对欧出口。
[源] 拉丁语 *impingere*（撞上），im-（进入）+ * pangere*（固定）→ 撞入 → 冲击。
[族] impinge 冲击（正式）
[搭] have an impact on 对……产生影响, environmental impact 环境影响, significant impact 显著影响, impact assessment 影响评估, impact negatively 产生负面影响
[联] influence 影响（较温和）, effect 效果, affect 影响, consequence 后果, shock 冲击, ripple effect 连锁反应, collateral damage 连带损害
[记] impact on 极常用；impact 作动词在美式英语中已很常见。impact vs. influence：impact 强调冲击力度；influence 可指长期、温和的作用

**influence**
英/美 /ˈɪnfluəns/
n. 影响；影响力
v. 影响；左右
[义] The capacity to have an effect on someone's character, opinions, or behaviour; a person or thing with such capacity. 影响他人性格、观点或行为的能力；具有这种能力的人或事（under the influence of 在……影响下, influence on 对……的影响）。比 impact 更温和、可指长期潜移默化；可褒可中性。
[形] influences, influenced, influenced, influencing
[例] ① Parents have a strong influence on their children's values. 父母对子女的价值观影响深远。 ② Social media can influence how people vote. 社交媒体能影响人们的投票方式。
[源] 拉丁语 *influere*（流入），in-（进入）+ * fluere*（流）→ 星力「流入」→ 影响（旧天文学义）。
[族] influential 有影响力的, influencer 网红（新义）
[搭] have an influence on 对……有影响, under the influence of 在……影响下, influence sb.'s decision 影响某人的决定, exert influence 施加影响, sphere of influence 势力范围
[联] impact 冲击性影响, affect 影响, sway 左右, persuade 说服, pressure 施压, role model 榜样, peer pressure 同辈压力
[记] influence on/over + 对象；influence sb. to do 较少，常用 influence sb.'s choice。under the influence 法律语境指「酒驾」

**produce**
英/美 /prəˈdjuːs/ 美 /prəˈduːs/
v. 生产；引起；拿出
n. 农产品（/ˈprɒdjuːs/）
[义] To cause something to happen or exist; to make or grow something. 使某事发生或存在（produce results 产生结果, produce evidence 拿出证据）；生产、制造。名词 produce 指「农产品」，重音在第一音节。因果义强调「产出、使出现」。
[形] produces / produced / produced / producing；名词 producer 生产者
[例] ① The talks failed to produce an agreement. 会谈未能达成协议。 ② Stress can produce physical symptoms such as headaches. 压力可引发头痛等躯体症状。
[源] 拉丁语 * producere*（向前引出），pro-（向前）+ ducere（引导）→ 引出 → 生产。
[族] product 产品, production 生产, productive 多产的, producer 生产者
[搭] produce results 产生结果, produce evidence 拿出证据, mass produce 批量生产, produce an effect 产生效果, farm produce 农产品
[联] generate 产生, create 创造, cause 导致, yield 产出, trigger 触发, manufacture 制造, outcome 结果
[记] produce 作动词重音 /prəˈdjuːs/；作名词「农产品」重音 /ˈprɒdjuːs/。produce results/evidence 为高频因果搭配

**generate**
英/美 /ˈdʒenəreɪt/
v. 产生；引起；发电
[义] To cause something to exist or begin; to produce energy. 使某物存在或开始（generate interest 引起兴趣, generate revenue 创造收入, generate electricity 发电）。比 produce 略正式，常指「从无到有地产生」抽象事物或能量。
[形] generates / generated / generated / generating
[例] ① The campaign generated a lot of public debate. 这场运动引发了大量公共讨论。 ② Solar panels generate power during daylight hours. 太阳能电池板白天发电。
[源] 拉丁语 *generare*（生育），* genus*（种类、出生）→ 使产生 → 生成。
[族] generation 一代, generator 发电机, generic 通用的, generous 慷慨的
[搭] generate interest 引起兴趣, generate revenue 创造收入, generate electricity 发电, generate heat 产生热量, generate publicity 制造舆论
[联] produce 生产/产生, create 创造, spark 引发, trigger 触发, yield 产出, stimulate 刺激
[记] generate + 抽象名词（interest, revenue, debate）极常见。generate vs. produce：generate 多指「引发、产生（抽象物或电）」；produce 可指实物生产

**induce**
英/美 /ɪnˈdjuːs/ 美 /ɪnˈduːs/
v. 引起；诱导；催生
[义] To cause something to happen; to persuade or bring about (often in medical or formal contexts). 导致某事发生（induce labour 引产, drug-induced 药物所致）；说服、诱使（induce sb. to do 诱使某人做）。比 cause 更正式，医学、科学语境常见。
[形] induces / induced / induced / inducing
[例] ① The medication can induce drowsiness. 该药可致嗜睡。 ② Nothing could induce her to change her mind. 什么都不能让她改变主意。
[源] 拉丁语 * inducere*（引入），in-（进入）+ ducere（引导）→ 引入 → 引起。
[族] induction 归纳/入职, inductive 归纳的
[搭] induce labour 引产, induce sleep 催眠, drug-induced 药物所致, induce sb. to do 诱使某人做, stress-induced 压力所致
[联] cause 导致, trigger 触发, provoke 激起, persuade 说服, bring about 带来, side effect 副作用
[记] induce 医学义「引产、诱发」极固定。induce sb. to do 正式；口语多用 persuade / convince

**contribute**
英/美 /kənˈtrɪbjuːt/
v. 贡献；促成；投稿
[义] To help to cause something to happen; to give money, help, or ideas. 促成某事发生（contribute to 有助于/促成）；贡献金钱、帮助或观点。强调「作为因素之一参与因果」，未必是唯一原因。
[形] contributes / contributed / contributed / contributing
[例] ① Several factors contributed to the delay. 若干因素导致了延误。 ② She contributes regularly to an online magazine. 她定期给一家在线杂志投稿。
[源] 拉丁语 *contribuere*（共同给予），con-（共同）+ * tribuere*（分配）→ 共同出一份 → 贡献。
[族] contribution 贡献, contributor 贡献者, contributory 促成的
[搭] contribute to 有助于/促成, contribute money 捐款, contributing factor 促成因素, contribute to a fund 向基金捐款
[联] lead to 导致, cause 导致, factor 因素, play a role 起作用, donate 捐赠, add to 增加
[记] contribute to + 名词/动名词；主语为「促成因素之一」。contribute to a magazine 指「投稿」，与「促成」义同形

**entail**
英/美 /ɪnˈteɪl/
v. 必然包含；使必要
[义] To involve something as a necessary or inevitable part or consequence. 必然包含或导致（entail costs 必然带来成本, entail doing sth. 意味着必须做某事）。比 cause 更绑「逻辑上或结构上不可避免」，不强调意外。逻辑学 entailment 表「蕴含」。
[形] entails / entailed / entailed / entailing
[例] ① The project will entail considerable expense. 该项目将必然产生可观费用。 ② Running a restaurant entails long hours and hard work. 开餐馆意味着长时间辛苦劳作。
[源] 拉丁语 *intailare*（切分标记），in- + * talea*（枝条）→ 限定继承 → 必然附带。
[族] entailment 蕴含/必然结果
[搭] entail costs 必然带来成本, entail doing sth. 意味着必须做, entail risks 必然涉及风险, logically entail 逻辑上蕴含
[联] involve 涉及, require 需要, imply 隐含, cause 导致, consequence 后果, necessary 必要的
[记] entail + 名词/动名词；无 entail sb. to do。entail vs. cause：entail 强调「必然包含」；cause 可指偶然引发

**precipitate**
英/美 /prɪˈsɪpɪteɪt/
v. 促成；使突然发生
adj. 仓促的，鲁莽的
[义] To cause an event to happen suddenly or sooner than expected; (adj.) done hastily without careful consideration. 使事件突然或提前发生（precipitate a crisis 酿成危机, precipitate change 加速变革）；形容词表「仓促的」（a precipitate decision 草率的决定）。正式、新闻与学术语体。
[形] precipitates / precipitated / precipitated / precipitating
[例] ① The assassination precipitated a civil war. 暗杀事件引发了一场内战。 ② We should avoid a precipitate withdrawal of troops. 我们应避免仓促撤军。
[源] 拉丁语 * praecipitare*（头朝下扔），* praeceps*（头向前）→ 推落 → 加速坠落 → 使突然发生。

v. 预示；是……的好兆头
n. 占卜者
[义] To be a sign of especially good or bad things to come; (historical) a Roman religious official who interpreted omens. 预示吉凶（augur well/ill 是好/坏兆头）；古罗马占卜官。及物/名词，正式。与 bode well/ill 结构极近，augur 更书面。
[形] augurs / augured / augured / auguring
[例] ① The strong turnout augurs well for democracy. 高投票率对民主是好兆头。 ② Continued delays augur ill for the project timeline. 持续延误对项目时间表不利。
[源] 拉丁语 * augur*（占卜者），* avis*（鸟）+ * garrire*（说话）→ 观鸟以占卜 → 预示。
[族] augury 预兆
[搭] augur well 是好兆头, augur ill 是坏兆头, augur well for 对……是好兆头, augury of 的预兆
[联] bode 预示, omen 预兆, portend 预示, sign 迹象, well 好, ill 坏
[记] augur well/ill (for) + 对象。augury 名词：an augury of change 变革之兆。augur vs. bode：极近；augur 更古典、正式

**attributable**
英/美 /əˈtrɪbjətəbl/
adj. 可归因于……的
[义] Able to be regarded as being caused by something. 可被视为由某因素造成（deaths attributable to pollution 可归因于污染的死亡, attributable to 归因于）。形容词，正式/报告语体。表「统计或判断上，果与因可建立对应关系」。
[例] ① Half of the decline is attributable to lower prices. 半数降幅可归因于降价。 ② The error is attributable to a software bug. 该错误可归因于软件缺陷。
[源] attribute（归因）+ -able → 可归因的。
[族] attribute 归因, attribution 归因, attributable to 归因于
[搭] attributable to 归因于, deaths attributable to 可归因于……的死亡, partly attributable 部分可归因, directly attributable 直接可归因
[联] account for 解释, blame 归咎, cause 原因, due to 由于, responsible 负责的, pollution 污染
[记] attributable to + 原因（固定结构）。主语为「果」（deaths, decline, error）。attributable vs. due to：attributable 作形容词修饰名词

**in consequence**
英/美 /ɪn ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/ 美 /ɪn ˈkɑːnsəkwens/
adv. 因此；结果
[义] As a result; consequently (formal). 因此、结果，偏正式（He failed the exam and was, in consequence, denied entry 他考试不及格，因而被拒入学）。短语。与 as a result、consequently 同组，语气更书面。
[例] ① The firm collapsed; in consequence, hundreds lost jobs. 公司倒闭，结果数百人失业。 ② She resigned, and in consequence lost her pension rights. 她辞职了，因而失去养老金权益。
[搭] in consequence of 由于（后接名词）, and in consequence 因而, as a consequence 因此
[联] consequently 因此, therefore 因此, as a result 因此, in consequence of 由于, result 结果
[记] in consequence 单独表「因此」；in consequence of + 名词表「由于」。in consequence vs. in consequence of：前者接果，后者接因

**thereby**
英/美 /ˌðeəˈbaɪ/
adv. 从而；借此
[义] By that means; as a result of that. 从而、由此（lowered rates, thereby boosting demand 降低利率，从而刺激需求）。副词，正式。连接「手段/前步行动 → 直接后续效应」，常接动名词。
[例] ① The law simplified procedures, thereby reducing delays. 法律简化了程序，从而减少延误。 ② She resigned, thereby avoiding a public scandal. 她辞职了，从而避免公开丑闻。
[源] there + by → thereby「借此 → 从而」。
[搭] thereby reducing 从而减少, thereby avoiding 从而避免, thereby making 从而使, thereby boosting 从而刺激
[联] thus 因此, consequently 因此, hence 因此, reduce 减少, boost 刺激
[记] thereby + 动名词（reducing, avoiding, making）极固定。thereby vs. therefore：thereby 绑「同一主语之手段→效应」

**so that**
英/美 /səʊ ðæt/
conj. 以便；以至于
[义] In order that; with the result that. 以便（表目的：I left early so that I would not be late 我早走以免迟到）；以至于（表结果：It rained so hard that streets flooded 雨下得太大，街道淹了）。后接从句。表「意图之因 → 期望之果」或「程度之因 → 结果之果」。
[例] ① She spoke slowly so that everyone could understand. 她说得很慢，以便大家都能听懂。 ② The box was so heavy that I couldn't lift it. 箱子太重，我搬不动。
[搭] so that 以便/以至于, so … that 如此……以至于, in order that 以便（更正式）
[联] in order to 为了, so as to 以便, therefore 因此, purpose 目的, result 结果, understand 理解
[记] so that + 从句（目的/结果）。so … that … 表程度结果。so that vs. in order to：so that 接从句；in order to 接动词原形

**in order to**
英/美 /ɪn ˈɔːdə(r) tuː/
prep. 为了；以便
[义] With the purpose of; so as to. 为了（做某事）（in order to save time 为节省时间, in order to prevent accidents 为防止事故）。后接动词原形。表「行为目的作为因 → 预期结果/避免之果」。
[例] ① They merged departments in order to cut costs. 他们合并部门以削减成本。 ② She recorded the meeting in order to review it later. 她录了会议以便日后回顾。
[搭] in order to save time 为节省时间, in order to prevent 为防止, in order not to 为了不, so as to 以便（近义）
[联] so that 以便, so as to 以便, for the sake of 为了, purpose 目的, prevent 防止, save 节省
[记] in order to + 动词原形；in order not to + 动词原形表「为了不」。in order to vs. so as to：近义；in order to 略更正式
