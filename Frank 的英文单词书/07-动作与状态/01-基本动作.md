# 基本动作

> 本章收跨领域通用动作动词：位移、取放、施力、制造与破坏等物理层面的「做」。**不**收人际/礼仪行为（apologize, promise → `01-05`）；**不**收路径名物与方向介副词（through, route → `05-03`）；**不**收专指工具、家居设备的 operate/handle（→ 各物章）。
>
> 一词一条：*give, take, move, walk, run, open, close, leave, arrive* 等已在 `01-05` 行为与动作卷成条，本章**不重复**；以下补足该卷未收的通用动词轴心。

## 日耳曼核心：去来

**go**
英 /ɡəʊ/ 美 /ɡoʊ/
v. 去；走；进行；变成
[义] To move or travel from one place to another; to leave; to function or proceed (The clock goes tick-tock.); to become (go bad 变质, go silent 安静下来). 英语最基础的位移动词之一，义域极宽：物理移动（go home）、进行（go well 进展顺利）、变化（go red 变红）。与 come 方向相反：go 离说话者或参照点而去，come 朝其而来。
[形] go / went / gone / going
[例] ① What time do you usually go to bed? 你通常几点睡觉？ ② Something went wrong with the printer again. 打印机又出问题了。
[搭] go home 回家, go to 去, go on 继续, go back 回去, go out 外出, go wrong 出问题, go bad 变质, let go 松手；放手
[联] come 来（方向反义）, leave 离开, arrive 到达, move 移动, stay 留下, return 返回, walk 走, run 跑
[记] go + 动名词表「去做某类活动」：go shopping, go swimming。go 作连系动词 + 形容词表变化：go mad, go bankrupt。过去分词 gone 表「不在了」：He's gone. 他走了/人不在了

**come**
英/美 /kʌm/
v. 来；到达；发生；变成
[义] To move toward or arrive at a place; to happen or occur (Spring comes early this year.); to reach a state (come true 成真, come loose 松脱). 与 go 相对：come 朝说话者或参照点而来。come from 表来源；come across 偶然遇见或给人印象。
[形] come / came / come / coming
[例] ① Can you come to the meeting at three? 你能三点来开会吗？ ② The news came as a shock to everyone. 消息让所有人震惊。
[搭] come from 来自, come back 回来, come in 进来, come across 偶然遇见；给人……印象, come true 成真, come up with 想出, come to an end 结束
[联] go 去, arrive 到达, reach 到达, approach 接近, leave 离开, return 回来, happen 发生
[记] come 与 go 构成最基础位移对。come + 不定式表「最终做到」（She came to realize… 她逐渐意识到……）。How come…? 口语「怎么会……？」

## 取放与传递

**get**
英/美 /ɡet/
v. 得到；拿到；变得；到达
[义] To obtain, receive, or fetch something; to reach a place (get home); to become (get tired 累了, get dark 天黑). 超高频「取得/到达/变化」动词：物理取得（get a ticket）、到达（get to work）、连系变化（get better）。比 take 更宽，不强调「从某处拿走」的方向性。
[形] get / got / got 或 gotten / getting
[例] ① I need to get some milk on my way home. 我回家路上得买点牛奶。 ② It gets cold here at night, even in summer. 这里夜里会冷，就算在夏天。
[搭] get sth. 拿到/得到, get to 到达, get home 到家, get ready 准备好, get better/worse 好转/恶化, get along 相处, get rid of 摆脱
[联] obtain 获得（较正式）, receive 收到, fetch 去取, take 拿, give 给, become 变成, reach 到达
[记] 美式过去分词 gotten 更常见（I've gotten used to it）；英式多用 got。get + 形容词表变化：get angry, get married。get sb. to do 使某人做某事

**put**
英/美 /pʊt/
v. 放；置；写；表达
[义] To move something to a particular place or position; to write or express (put your name on the form, put it simply 简而言之). 强调「使某物处于某位置」，与 take 方向相反。不规则：put – put – put。
[形] put / put / put / putting
[例] ① Put your coat on the hook by the door. 把外套挂在门边的钩子上。 ② She put the keys in her bag and left. 她把钥匙放进包里走了。
[搭] put sth. on/in/under 把……放在……上/里/下, put away 收好, put down 放下, put off 推迟, put up with 忍受, put forward 提出
[联] place 放置（较正式）, set 放置, lay 平放, take 拿, remove 移走, drop 丢下, hold 拿着
[记] put 三态同形。put on 穿衣服；put off 推迟（勿与 take off 混淆）。put sb. down 还可指「让某人下车/贬低某人」，依语境

**bring**
英/美 /brɪŋ/
v. 带来；拿来；导致
[义] To carry or move something toward the speaker or a destination (bring a book, bring sb. to tears 使人流泪). 方向义明确：朝说话者或目标位置「带来」。bring about 导致；bring up 抚养/提出（话题）。
[形] bring / brought / brought / bringing
[例] ① Don't forget to bring your ID tomorrow. 明天别忘了带身份证。 ② The policy brought about major changes in the industry. 这项政策给行业带来重大变化。
[搭] bring sth. 带来, bring sb. sth. 给某人带来, bring about 导致, bring up 抚养；提出, bring back 带回, bring in 引进；赚得
[联] take 带走（方向反义）, carry 搬运, fetch 去取来, deliver 递送, send 寄送, cause 导致
[记] bring/take 方向：bring 朝「来处/此处」，take 朝「去处/彼处」。Bring me the file. / Take this to her. 不可说 × bring to there，用 take

**carry**
英/美 /ˈkæri/
v. 携带；搬运；支撑；刊登
[义] To support and move someone or something from one place to another; to have something with you; to publish or broadcast (The paper carried the story.). 强调「随身带着或搬运」，不一定有明确方向（bring/take 有方向）。carry on 继续；carry out 执行。
[形] carry / carried / carried / carrying
[例] ① She carries a laptop in her backpack every day. 她每天背包里带着笔记本电脑。 ② The bridge is strong enough to carry heavy trucks. 这座桥足以承载重型卡车。
[搭] carry sth. 携带/搬运, carry on 继续, carry out 执行, carry a bag 拎包, carry weight 有分量（喻）, carry a tune 不跑调
[联] bring 带来, take 带走, hold 拿着, transport 运输, lift 举起, drop 放下, load 装载
[记] carry 可指「随身携带」（状态），bring/take 多指一次位移动作。carry out a plan 执行计划；carry on working 继续工作

**hold**
英/美 /hoʊld/
v. 拿着；握住；容纳；举行
[义] To carry or support in your hands or arms; to contain (The bottle holds a liter.); to organize (hold a meeting); to keep a position (hold the door). 强调「握持、保持某状态或容纳」。hold on 等一下；hold back 抑制。
[形] hold / held / held / holding
[例] ① Hold the railing when you go down the stairs. 下楼梯时请扶好扶手。 ② The stadium holds about fifty thousand people. 这座体育场可容纳约五万人。
[搭] hold sth. 拿着/握住, hold on 等一下, hold back 抑制, hold a meeting 开会, hold your breath 屏息, get hold of 联系到/拿到
[联] grab 抓, grasp 握紧, carry 携带, release 释放, let go 松手, contain 容纳, keep 保持
[记] hold 与 keep 近义：hold 多指「握/持/容」，keep 多指「保留、持续拥有」。hold on 电话用语「请稍等」。hold sb. responsible 追究责任

**send**
英/美 /send/
v. 发送；寄；派遣
[义] To cause something to go to another place or person (send a letter, send an email); to cause someone to go somewhere (send troops). 强调「使某物/某人离开发送者而去」。send for 派人去请；send off 送行。
[形] send / sent / sent / sending
[例] ① I'll send you the file by email this afternoon. 我今天下午用邮件把文件发给你。 ② They sent a technician to fix the heating system. 他们派了一名技师来修供暖系统。
[搭] send sth. to sb. 把……寄/发给, send an email 发邮件, send for 派人去请, send off 送行, send back 退回, send out 发出
[联] receive 收到, deliver 递送, mail 邮寄, dispatch 派遣, fetch 去取, forward 转发, return 退回
[记] send 与 bring 方向相反：send 使离开发送者而去。send sb. doing 使某人去做（send him shopping）。send for a doctor 派人请医生

**fetch**
英/美 /fetʃ/
v. 去取来；接来
[义] To go to a place and bring someone or something back. 含「去 + 回」双程：离开再去把东西或人带回来（fetch the kids from school, fetch a chair）。英式比美式更常用；美式常改用 get。
[形] fetch / fetched / fetched / fetching
[例] ① Could you fetch me a glass of water from the kitchen? 你能去厨房帮我拿杯水来吗？ ② The dog fetched the ball and dropped it at his feet. 狗把球叼回来放在他脚边。
[搭] fetch sth. 去取来, fetch sb. 去接某人, fetch and carry 跑腿打杂, fetch up 最终到达（英式）
[联] bring 带来, get 拿到, retrieve 取回, collect 取, deliver 送达, carry 搬运
[记] fetch 强调「去取回」完整动作；bring 只强调「带来」方向。英式 Could you fetch…? 极自然；美式更常说 Can you get…? 勿与 catch（接住）混淆

## 施力与操控

**push**
英/美 /pʊʃ/
v. 推；按；推动；敦促
[义] To use force to move someone or something away from you; to press a button; to encourage or urge (push for reform). 施力方向「离身而去」。push 还可指「推销、推广」（push a product）。
[形] push / pushed / pushed / pushing
[例] ① She pushed the door open and walked in. 她推开门走了进去。 ② Don't push yourself too hard—you need rest. 别逼自己太紧——你需要休息。
[搭] push the door 推门, push a button 按按钮, push for 推动/争取, push sb. to do 敦促, push away 推开, push through 挤过；强行通过
[联] pull 拉, press 按, shove 猛推, drag 拖, move 移动, force 强迫, pull：方向相反
[记] push 与 pull 成对记忆。push open/ shut 推开门/关。push sb. around 欺负/摆布某人（口语）。push 作名词：give sb. a push 推一把

**pull**
英/美 /pʊl/
v. 拉；拖；拔；吸引
[义] To use force to move something toward you or in the direction you are moving; to remove by pulling (pull a tooth); to attract (pull a crowd). 施力方向「向身而来」。pull over 靠边停车；pull out 拔出/退出。
[形] pull / pulled / pulled / pulling
[例] ① He pulled the drawer open and found the missing keys. 他拉开抽屉，找到了丢的钥匙。 ② The sale pulled in more customers than we expected. 促销吸引来的顾客比预期多。
[搭] pull the door 拉门, pull out 拔出；退出, pull over 靠边停车, pull through 渡过难关, pull together 齐心协力, pull a muscle 拉伤肌肉
[联] push 推, drag 拖, tug 拽, draw 拉, attract 吸引, release 松开, push：方向相反
[记] pull 与 push 方向相反。pull over 驾车靠边。pull strings 走后门（喻）。pull one's weight 尽本分（喻）

**throw**
英 /θrəʊ/ 美 /θroʊ/
v. 扔；抛；投；使突然陷入
[义] To send something through the air with force; to move your body quickly (throw oneself on the bed); to cause suddenly (throw sb. into panic). 强调「离手而出、经空中」。throw away 扔掉；throw up 呕吐（口语）。
[形] throw / threw / thrown / throwing
[例] ① He threw the ball to the catcher. 他把球传给接球手。 ② The scandal threw the company into crisis. 丑闻使公司陷入危机。
[搭] throw sth. at/to 朝……扔/扔给, throw away 扔掉, throw out 扔掉；赶走, throw up 呕吐, throw a party 办派对, throw light on 阐明
[联] catch 接住, toss 轻抛, hurl 猛掷, drop 丢下, pitch 投, throw：与 catch 动作对
[记] throw 不规则：threw / thrown。throw at 含「朝人扔（可能攻击）」；throw to 传给对方。throw away 浪费（throw away a chance）

**catch**
英/美 /kætʃ/
v. 接住；抓住；赶上；感染
[义] To grab something moving through the air; to capture (catch a fish); to reach in time (catch the bus); to become ill with (catch a cold). 与 throw 相对，强调「截获、接住」。catch up 赶上；catch on 流行/理解。
[形] catch / caught / caught / catching
[例] ① She caught the vase before it hit the floor. 她在花瓶落地前接住了它。 ② I hope we can catch the last train tonight. 希望今晚能赶上末班火车。
[搭] catch sth. 接住/抓住, catch the bus/train 赶车, catch a cold 感冒, catch up with 赶上, catch on 理解；流行, catch sight of 瞥见
[联] throw 扔, grab 抓, seize 抓住, miss 没接住, drop 掉落, trap 设陷阱, release 释放
[记] catch 不规则：caught / caught。catch 可表「听清/理解」（Sorry, I didn't catch that.）。catch fire 着火；catch someone's eye 引起注意

**drop**
英 /drɒp/ 美 /drɑːp/
v. 落下；掉；放下；放弃
[义] To fall or let fall; to put down briefly (drop your bags); to stop or abandon (drop the subject, drop a course). 可指意外掉落或主动松手放下。drop off 放下/睡着；drop out 退出。
[形] drop / dropped / dropped / dropping
[例] ① Be careful—the plate might drop. 小心——盘子可能会掉。 ② Just drop the package at the front desk. 把包裹放在前台就行。
[搭] drop sth. 掉落/放下, drop off 放下；睡着, drop out 退出, drop by 顺访, drop in 顺便来访, drop the ball 搞砸（喻）
[联] fall 落下, throw 扔, pick up 捡起, lift 举起, release 释放, spill 洒出, catch 接住
[记] drop 与 fall：drop 可及物（drop the pen）；fall 多不及物（The pen fell.）。drop by/in 顺便拜访。名词 a drop 指「一滴；小量下降」

**grab**
英/美 /ɡræb/
v. 抓；抢；匆匆拿
[义] To take hold of something suddenly and roughly; to get quickly (grab a bite, grab a seat). 比 take 更急、更猛，常带「一把抓住或抢得」色彩。grab at 试图抓住。
[形] grab / grabbed / grabbed / grabbing
[例] ① He grabbed his coat and ran out the door. 他抓起外套冲出门去。 ② Grab a seat—we're about to start. 赶紧找座——我们要开始了。
[搭] grab sth. 抓住/抢, grab at 试图抓, grab a bite 随便吃点, grab attention 吸引注意, grab and go 拿了就走
[联] seize 抓住, snatch 一把夺, grasp 握紧, hold 拿着, release 放开, take 拿
[记] grab 偏口语、动作急。grab sb's arm 抓住胳膊。grab 作名词：make a grab for 试图抢夺。比 take 更强调「快、猛、粗」

**pick**
英/美 /pɪk/
v. 采；挑；捡；接
[义] To choose from a group (pick a card); to remove small things with your fingers (pick fruit); to collect from the ground (pick up a coin). pick up 极常用：捡起、接人、学会、好转。与 choose 比，pick 更口语、更随意。
[形] pick / picked / picked / picking
[例] ① She picked up the phone on the second ring. 铃响第二声她就接了电话。 ② Pick whichever seat you like—there's plenty of room. 随便选座——地方够大。
[搭] pick up 捡起；接人；学会, pick out 挑出, pick at 挑剔地吃, pick on 找茬, pick a fight 寻衅
[联] choose 选择, select 挑选（较正式）, collect 收集, drop 放下, grab 抓, gather 聚集
[记] pick up 义极多：pick up the kids 接孩子, pick up Spanish 学会西班牙语, business is picking up 生意好转。pick 与 choose：pick 更随意（pick a color）

## 制造、破坏与保持

**make**
英/美 /meɪk/
v. 做；制造；使；成为
[义] To create or produce by combining parts or materials; to cause to be or become (make sb. happy, make a decision); to earn (make money). 超高频「使成/制造」动词：物理制作（make a cake）、使役（make me laugh）、形成（make friends）。与 do 比，make 强调「产出结果或使某物存在」。
[形] make / made / made / making
[例] ① She made a cake for his birthday. 她为他生日做了个蛋糕。 ② The news made everyone nervous. 消息让大家都紧张起来。
[搭] make sth. 做/制造, make sb. do 使某人做, make friends 交朋友, make a decision 做决定, make sure 确保, make up 组成；化妆；和解
[联] create 创造, produce 生产, build 建造, do 做, break 打破, cause 导致, force 迫使
[记] make 与 do：make 强调产出（make coffee）；do 强调执行活动（do homework）。make sb. do 不带 to；make 表「成为」：make a good leader。make it 成功赶到/做到

**break**
英/美 /breɪk/
v. 打破；弄坏；中断；违反
[义] To separate into pieces by force; to damage so it no longer works; to interrupt (break the silence); to fail to obey (break the law). 与 make 相对，强调「完整性被破坏」。break down 故障/崩溃；break up 分手/解散。
[形] break / broke / broken / breaking
[例] ① Be careful not to break the glass. 小心别打碎玻璃。 ② The talks broke down after both sides refused to compromise. 双方拒绝妥协后，谈判破裂了。
[搭] break sth. 打破/弄坏, break down 故障；崩溃, break up 分手；解散, break the law 违法, break a record 破纪录, break even 收支平衡
[联] smash 砸碎, crack 裂而未碎, fix 修理, repair 修复, make 制造, damage 损坏, mend 修补
[记] break 不规则：broke / broken。break 作名词：take a break 休息一下。broken 作形容词：a broken arm 骨折。break vs. damage：break 强调「碎/断/不工作」

**keep**
英/美 /kiːp/
v. 保持；保留；继续；饲养
[义] To continue to have something and not give it away; to stay in a particular state (keep quiet, keep warm); to continue doing (keep trying); to store or look after (keep pets). 强调「持续拥有或维持状态」。keep on 继续做；keep up 保持/跟上。
[形] keep / kept / kept / keeping
[例] ① Keep the receipt in case you need to return it. 留着收据，万一要退。 ② Keep trying—you're getting closer. 继续试——你离成功更近了。
[搭] keep sth. 保留, keep doing 继续做, keep quiet 保持安静, keep in touch 保持联系, keep up with 跟上, keep an eye on 照看
[联] hold 拿着, retain 保留（较正式）, maintain 维持, store 储存, lose 丢失, give away 赠送, stop 停止
[记] keep 与 hold：keep 强调「持续拥有/保持状态」；hold 强调「握持」。keep sb. waiting 让某人等。keep fit 保持健康。keep from doing 忍住不做

**let**
英/美 /let/
v. 让；允许；出租
[义] To allow someone to do something or something to happen (let me go, let the water run); to rent out (let a flat). 使役动词：let sb. do sth.（不带 to）。let go 松手/放手；let down 使失望。
[形] let / let / let / letting
[例] ① Let me know if you need any help. 需要帮忙就告诉我。 ② They won't let us leave until the paperwork is done. 文书办完之前他们不让我们走。
[搭] let sb. do 让某人做, let go 松手；放手, let in 让进, let out 放出；出租, let down 使失望, let alone 更不用说
[联] allow 允许（较正式）, permit 许可, make 使, prevent 阻止, forbid 禁止, enable 使能够
[记] let sb. do 不带 to（Let him go. 不是 × let him to go）。let's = let us，提建议。let 表「出租」：let a room 英式。let go of 松开

## 放置、装载与使用

**use**
英/美 /juːz/
v. 使用；用；运用
n. 使用；用途
[义] To take, employ, or consume something for a purpose (use a pen, use your phone, use force). 为达目的而取用、运用某物或方法；use up 用完。名词 use 不可数表「使用」，可数表「用途」（It's no use 没用）。
[形] use / used / used / using
[例] ① May I use your charger for a few minutes? 我可以用你的充电器几分钟吗？ ② She used to live in Shanghai before moving here. 搬来之前她住在上海。
[搭] use sth. for 用……做, use up 用完, make use of 利用, in use 在使用中, out of use 已停用, used to do 过去常常
[联] employ 使用（较正式）, apply 应用, operate 操作, utilize 利用, waste 浪费, reuse 再利用
[记] use 与 used to：used to do 表过去习惯（I used to smoke）；be used to doing 表习惯于。useful/useless 形容词。used 作形容词表「二手的」（a used car）

**deliver**
英/美 /dɪˈlɪvər/
v. 递送；交付；发表
[义] To take goods, letters, or people to a place or person; to give a speech or service (deliver a package, deliver a baby, deliver a speech). 把物品或人送到目的地；亦指接生、发表演说。比 send 更强调「送达终点、交到手中」。
[形] deliver / delivered / delivered / delivering
[例] ① The package was delivered to the wrong address. 包裹被送到了错误地址。 ② She delivered an excellent presentation on climate policy. 她就气候政策做了一场精彩报告。
[搭] deliver sth. to sb. 把……送到, deliver a speech 发表演说, deliver a baby 接生, home delivery 送货上门, deliver on a promise 履行承诺
[联] send 寄送, carry 搬运, transport 运输, receive 收到, fetch 去取, mail 邮寄, distribute 分发
[记] deliver 强调「送到、交到」。delivery 名词：next-day delivery 次日达。deliver on 表「兑现（承诺）」。deliver 与 send：send 发出即可；deliver 须送达

**remove**
英/美 /rɪˈmuːv/
v. 移走；去掉；脱下
[义] To take something away from a place or position; to get rid of (remove your shoes, remove a stain, remove sb. from office). 从原处拿开或使消失；比 take away 更正式、更强调「脱离原位置」。
[形] remove / removed / removed / removing
[例] ① Please remove your laptop from the bag at security. 安检时请把笔记本电脑从包里取出。 ② The surgeon removed the damaged tissue. 外科医生切除了受损组织。
[源] re-（再/离开）+ move（移动）→ 移开 → 移除。
[族] removal 移除, removable 可拆卸的
[搭] remove sth. from 从……移走, remove your shoes 脱鞋, remove a stain 去污渍, remove from office 免职, far removed from 与……相去甚远
[联] take 拿, put 放, delete 删除, add 添加, insert 插入, detach 分离, eliminate 消除
[记] remove 及物，须接宾语。remove vs. move：move 可指「移动到新位置」；remove 强调「从原处去掉」。removable 指「可拆卸的」（removable battery）

**insert**
英/美 /ɪnˈsɜːrt/
v. 插入；嵌入
[义] To put something into something else (insert a key, insert a SIM card, insert a paragraph). 把某物放进另一物内部或中间；与 remove 相对。insert 偏正式、技术文档常见。
[形] insert / inserted / inserted / inserting
[例] ① Insert the USB drive into the port on the left. 把 U 盘插入左侧接口。 ② He inserted a brief comment between the two sections. 他在两节之间插入了一句简短评论。
[源] in-（进入）+ *serere*（连接）→ 连入 → 插入。
[族] insertion 插入, inset 插页
[搭] insert into 插入到, insert a card 插卡, insert a comment 插入评论, insert mode 插入模式
[联] remove 移走, add 添加, attach 附着, embed 嵌入, extract 取出, plug in 插上
[记] insert 及物：insert A into B。insert vs. put：insert 强调「插入缝/槽/页间」。insert 作名词 /ˈɪnsɜːrt/ 指「插页、插件」

**fill**
英/美 /fɪl/
v. 装满；填满；担任
[义] To make something full; to occupy a space or role (fill a glass, fill a form, fill a position). 使容器或空间充满；填表；担任（职位）。fill in 填写/临时顶替；fill up 加满。
[形] fill / filled / filled / filling
[例] ① Could you fill this bottle with water? 你能把这瓶子装满水吗？ ② She filled in for the manager while he was on leave. 经理休假期间她临时代理。
[搭] fill with 用……装满, fill in 填写；顶替, fill out 填写（表格）, fill up 加满, fill a role 担任角色
[联] empty 倒空, pour 倒, spill 洒出, load 装载, complete 填完, occupy 占据
[记] fill 与 empty 相对。fill in a form（英式）/ fill out a form（美式）填表。be filled with 充满（be filled with joy）。fill 作名词：eat your fill 吃个够

**lay**
英/美 /leɪ/
v. 平放；铺设；下（蛋）
[义] To put something down carefully in a flat position; to produce eggs (lay the table, lay carpet, a hen lays eggs). 小心地平放或铺设；母鸡下蛋。不规则：lay – laid – laid；与 lie（躺）易混：lay 及物（lay the book down），lie 不及物（lie down）。
[形] lay / laid / laid / laying
[例] ① She laid the documents on the desk before leaving. 离开前她把文件平放在桌上。 ② Workers are laying new pipes under the street. 工人正在街道下铺设新管道。
[搭] lay the table 摆餐具, lay down 放下；制定, lay off 解雇, lay out 摆开；设计, lay eggs 下蛋
[联] put 放, place 放置, set 放置, lie 躺（易混）, spread 铺开, stack 堆叠
[记] lay/lie 易混：lay–laid–laid 及物「放平」；lie–lay–lain 不及物「躺」（He lay on the bed 他躺在床上）。lay 表「下蛋」：Hens lay eggs

**load**
英/美 /loʊd/
v. 装载；加载
n. 负荷；一批
[义] To put a large quantity of things into a vehicle, container, or device (load the truck, load a webpage). 把大量物品装入运输工具、容器或设备；计算机「加载」。与 unload 相对。
[形] load / loaded / loaded / loading
[例] ① It took an hour to load all the boxes onto the truck. 把所有箱子装上车花了一小时。 ② The page is slow to load on mobile networks. 这页面在移动网络上加载很慢。
[搭] load with 装满, load into/onto 装入, download 下载, upload 上传, a truckload 一卡车（喻）
[联] unload 卸货, pack 打包, carry 搬运, fill 装满, download 下载, upload 上传
[记] load 与 download/upload 同场。loaded 口语可表「很有钱的/加料的」。load 作名词：a heavy load 重负（喻）。take a load off 歇歇脚（口语）

**unload**
英/美 /ʌnˈloʊd/
v. 卸货；卸载；倾诉
[义] To remove goods from a vehicle or items from a container; to get rid of something unwanted (unload the van, unload your worries). 从车或容器中卸下货物；口语 unload 可指「倾诉、倒苦水」。
[形] unload / unloaded / unloaded / unloading
[例] ① We unloaded the furniture as soon as the truck arrived. 卡车一到我们就卸了家具。 ② She unloaded all her frustrations on me after work. 下班后她把一肚子委屈都倒给了我。
[搭] unload from 从……卸下, unload cargo 卸货, unload on sb. 向某人倾诉（口语）
[联] load 装载, remove 移走, dump 倾倒, deliver 送达, pack 打包, download 下载
[记] unload 与 load 成对。unload on sb. 口语「向某人发泄/倾诉」。unload 计算机义：unload a module 卸载模块

## 拖拽、滑动与抛掷

**drag**
英/美 /dræɡ/
v. 拖；拽；拖延
[义] To pull something heavy along the ground; to move slowly or with effort (drag a suitcase, drag your feet 磨蹭). 沿地面拉拽重物；引申「拖延、磨蹭」。drag on 拖太久。
[形] drag / dragged / dragged / dragging
[例] ① He dragged the heavy bag across the floor. 他把沉重的袋子拖过地板。 ② The meeting dragged on for another hour. 会议又拖了一个小时。
[搭] drag along 拖着走, drag on 拖延, drag sb. into 把某人卷入, drag and drop 拖放（计算机）, drag your feet 磨蹭
[联] pull 拉, tug 拽, push 推, haul 拖运, carry 搬运, rush 赶紧
[记] drag 强调「费力地拖」。drag and drop 计算机拖放操作。drag 作名词：a drag 累赘/无聊事（口语）。drag sb. down 拖累某人

**slide**
英/美 /slaɪd/
v. 滑动；悄悄移动
n. 幻灯片；滑梯
[义] To move smoothly along a surface while touching it; to move quietly into a position (slide the door open, slide into a seat). 沿接触面平滑移动；悄悄溜进位置。不规则：slide – slid – slid。
[形] slide / slid / slid / sliding
[例] ① She slid the window open to let in some air. 她滑开窗户透气。 ② Prices slid sharply after the announcement. 公告发布后价格急滑。
[搭] slide open/closed 滑开/关上, slide into 滑入；悄悄进入, slide show 幻灯放映, let slide 放任不管
[联] slip 滑倒, glide 滑翔, roll 滚动, drag 拖, push 推, slip：多指意外滑倒
[记] slide vs. slip：slide 强调「平滑滑动」；slip 多指「意外滑倒/滑落」。slide 作名词：on the slide 在下滑（英式）。slide 不规则：slid / slid

**roll**
英/美 /roʊl/
v. 滚动；卷；擀
n. 卷；名单
[义] To turn over and over or move on wheels; to form into a cylinder (roll a ball, roll dough, roll your eyes). 翻滚或轮式移动；卷成筒状；翻白眼。roll out 推出；roll up 卷起。
[形] roll / rolled / rolled / rolling
[例] ① The ball rolled under the sofa and disappeared. 球滚到沙发底下不见了。 ② Roll up your sleeves and let's get started. 挽起袖子，咱们开始吧。
[搭] roll over 翻身, roll up 卷起, roll out 推出, roll call 点名, on a roll 连战连捷（口语）, roll your eyes 翻白眼
[联] slide 滑动, spin 旋转, turn 转动, wrap 包裹, unfold 展开, rock 摇晃
[记] roll 作名词极多：bread roll 小面包, payroll 工资单。rock and roll 摇滚。roll vs. slide：roll 强调「转/滚」；slide 强调「滑」

**shake**
英/美 /ʃeɪk/
v. 摇；抖；握手
n. 摇动；奶昔
[义] To move quickly up and down or from side to side (shake a bottle, shake hands, shake with fear). 快速来回或上下移动；握手；因恐惧而发抖。不规则：shake – shook – shaken。
[形] shake / shook / shaken / shaking
[例] ① Shake the bottle well before you open it. 打开前请充分摇晃瓶子。 ② They shook hands and agreed to work together. 他们握手同意合作。
[搭] shake hands 握手, shake off 甩掉, shake up 整顿, milkshake 奶昔, shake with 因……颤抖
[联] tremble 颤抖, shiver 打战, nod 点头, wave 挥手, stir 搅动, vibrate 振动
[记] shake 不规则：shook / shaken。shake hands with sb. 与某人握手。shake 作名词：fair shake 公平待遇（美式口语）。shaken 形容词：shaken confidence 动摇的信心

**shove**
英/美 /ʃʌv/
v. 猛推；乱放
n. 推
[义] To push someone or something roughly or with force; to put something carelessly in a place (shove sb. aside, shove sth. in a drawer). 粗鲁或用力推；随便塞放。比 push 更粗、更带怒气或草率。
[形] shove / shoved / shoved / shoving
[例] ① He shoved his way through the crowd to reach the exit. 他挤开人群朝出口挤去。 ② Just shove those papers in the drawer for now. 那些纸先随便塞抽屉里吧。
[搭] shove aside 推到一边, shove in 塞入, give sb. a shove 推一把
[联] push 推, thrust 猛推, bump 碰撞, pull 拉, gentle 温和地
[记] shove 偏口语、带粗鲁感。push 中性；shove 含「蛮力/不耐烦」。shove off 走开（英式口语，略粗鲁）

**tug**
英/美 /tʌɡ/
v. 拽；拉
n. 猛拉
[义] To pull something hard, often repeatedly or with short jerks (tug at a rope, tug someone's sleeve). 用力拉，常指反复或短促地拽。比 pull 更强调「猛拽、拖扯」。
[形] tug / tugged / tugged / tugging
[例] ① The dog tugged at the leash, eager to run. 狗拽着牵引绳想跑。 ② She tugged his sleeve to get his attention. 她拽了拽他的袖子引起注意。
[搭] tug at 拽, tug of war 拔河, tugboat 拖船
[联] pull 拉, drag 拖, yank 猛拉, push 推, rope 绳子, grab 抓
[记] tug at 固定搭配：tug at heartstrings 触动心弦（喻）。tug of war 拔河。tug 与 yank：tug 可反复；yank 常一下猛拉

**toss**
英/美 /tɔːs/
v. 抛；扔；掷
n. 抛；掷币
[义] To throw something lightly or carelessly; to move restlessly (toss a salad, toss a coin, toss and turn). 轻抛或随意扔；翻来覆去睡不着。比 throw 力度轻、更随意。
[形] toss / tossed / tossed / tossing
[例] ① Toss the vegetables with a little olive oil before roasting. 烤前用少许橄榄油拌一下蔬菜。 ② He tossed the keys onto the table and sat down. 他把钥匙扔到桌上坐下了。
[搭] toss and turn 辗转反侧, toss a coin 掷硬币, toss aside 扔到一边, toss out 扔掉
[联] throw 扔, hurl 猛掷, catch 接住, flip 快速翻转, drop 丢下
[记] toss 比 throw 轻、随意。toss a salad 拌沙拉。toss and turn 固定：辗转难眠。toss up 英式「掷币决定」

## 击打与破坏

**hit**
英/美 /hɪt/
v. 打；击；撞；达到
n. 打击；热门
[义] To bring your hand or an object against something with force; to reach a level (hit the target, hit a record high). 用手或物体击打；碰撞；达到（高点/目标）。不规则：hit – hit – hit。
[形] hit / hit / hit / hitting
[例] ① The ball hit the window and cracked the glass. 球砸中窗户，玻璃裂了。 ② Sales hit a new high last quarter. 上季度销售创下新高。

[联] strike 击打, beat 连续击打, kick 踢, miss 未击中, punch 用拳猛击, bump 撞
[记] hit 三态同形。hit 可指「碰撞」（The car hit a tree）或「达到」（hit bottom 触底）。hit 作名词：a hit song 热门歌曲。hit on sb. 口语「搭讪」

**kick**
英/美 /kɪk/

n. 踢；快感
[义] To hit someone or something with your foot (kick the ball, kick the door open). 用脚踢；kick off 开始（会议/赛事）；kick out 踢出/开除。
[形] kick / kicked / kicked / kicking
[例] ① The striker kicked the ball into the top corner. 前锋把球踢进死角。 ② She kicked off her shoes and collapsed onto the sofa. 她踢掉鞋瘫在沙发上。
[搭] kick the ball 踢球, kick off 开始, kick out 开除, kick a habit 戒除习惯, kick in 生效
[联] hit 打, punch 用拳猛击, slap 掌掴, throw 扔, score 得分, foul 犯规
[记] kick 专指「用脚」。kick the bucket 俚语「死」（慎用）。kick 作名词：get a kick out of 从……中得到乐趣（口语）

**knock**
英/美 /nɑːk/
v. 敲；撞；击倒
n. 敲击声
[义] To hit a door with your knuckles to attract attention; to collide with force (knock on the door, knock over a vase). 敲门；碰撞弄倒。knock out 击昏；knock down 击倒/拆毁。
[形] knock / knocked / knocked / knocking
[例] ① Someone knocked on the door just as we were sitting down to dinner. 我们刚坐下吃饭就有人敲门。 ② He knocked over his coffee cup while reaching for the phone. 他伸手拿电话时打翻了咖啡杯。
[搭] knock on/at the door 敲门, knock over 碰倒, knock out 击昏, knock down 击倒, knock off 下班（英式口语）
[联] tap 轻敲, bang 猛关, hit 打, bump 碰撞, ring 按门铃, push 推
[记] knock on/at the door 敲门。knock over 碰倒。knock 作名词：a knock at the door 敲门声。Fortune knocks once 机会不常来（谚）

**rip**
英/美 /rɪp/
v. 撕；扯
n. 裂口
[义] To tear something quickly and forcefully (rip a letter open, rip your jeans). 快速用力撕开——日常撕纸、撕衣服首选 rip；同拼写 tear 读 /teə/ 表撕扯，读 /tɪə/ 表眼泪，音义皆不同。
[形] rip / ripped / ripped / ripping
[例] ① She ripped the envelope open and read the letter. 她撕开信封读信。 ② Be careful—you might rip the fabric if you pull too hard. 小心——太用力会把布料扯破。
[搭] rip open 撕开, rip off 敲竹杠, rip apart 撕碎, rip up 撕毁, let rip 放开（口语）
[联] tear /teə/ 撕, cut 切, shred 撕碎, mend 修补, patch 补丁, split 裂开
[记] rip 日常撕扯首选。rip off 口语「敲竹杠/山寨」。ripped 形容词口语「肌肉发达的」。rip vs. tear /teə/：同义；rip 更口语

## 传递、覆盖与操作

**return**
英/美 /rɪˈtɜːrn/
v. 返回；归还；回报
n. 返回；退货
[义] To go or come back to a place; to give or send something back (return home, return a book, return a call). 回到原处；归还；回电话。与 give back 近义；return 更正式。
[形] return / returned / returned / returning
[例] ① She returned the library books before the due date. 她在到期日前还了图书馆的书。 ② I'll return your call as soon as I'm free. 我有空就回你电话。
[搭] return to 回到, return sth. to sb. 把……还给, return home 回家, in return 作为回报, tax return 报税单
[联] come back 回来, go back 回去, give back 还回, send back 退回, leave 离开, borrow 借
[记] return 及物/不及物均可。return 作名词：many happy returns 生日快乐（固定）。return ticket 英式「往返票」

**pass**
英/美 /pɑːs/
v. 传递；经过；通过
n. 通行证；及格
[义] To hand something to someone (pass the salt); to go past (pass the station); to succeed in a test (pass an exam). 递给；经过；通过。与 hand 比，pass 强调「经手递过」；亦含通过考试（pass an exam），此处收传递与经过义。
[形] pass / passed / passed / passing
[例] ① Could you pass me the remote, please? 请把遥控器递给我好吗？ ② We passed several villages on the way to the coast. 去海边的路上经过了好几个村子。
[搭] pass sth. to sb. 把……递给, pass by 经过, pass through 穿过, pass on 传递；去世（委婉）, pass the exam 通过考试
[联] hand 递给, give 给, throw 扔, cross 穿过, fail 未通过, deliver 送达
[记] pass 义极多，靠语境：pass time 消磨时间, pass judgment 下判断。pass away 委婉「去世」。pass on 传递信息或指「过世」

**cover**
英/美 /ˈkʌvər/
v. 覆盖；包括；报道
n. 盖子；封面
