# 社交与人际关系

**friend**
英/美 /frend/
n. 朋友
[义] A person you know well and like, but who is usually not a member of your family. 彼此熟悉、有感情、非亲属的同伴（a close friend 密友，make friends 交朋友）。比 acquaintance 亲近；与 friendship（友谊关系）并读：friend 指人，friendship 指关系状态。社交场的核心词之一。
[例] ① She met her best friend in kindergarten and they are still close. 她幼儿园就认识了最好的朋友，至今亲密。 ② He's not just a friend—he's family to us. 他不仅是朋友——对我们来说像家人。
[搭] best friend 最好的朋友, make friends 交朋友, close friend 密友, friend request 好友申请（社交媒体）, be friends with 与……是朋友
[联] friendship 友谊, acquaintance 熟人, colleague 同事, neighbor 邻居, companion 伙伴, trust 信任, bond 纽带, relationship 关系, loyalty 忠诚, stranger 陌生人, enemy 敌人, buddy：朋友，口语
[记] 可数：a friend / friends。be friends with sb.（不说 × friend with 省略 be）。Facebook 时代 friend 亦作动词「加好友」

**neighbor**
英 /ˈneɪbə(r)/ 美 /ˈneɪbər/
n. 邻居
[义] A person who lives near you. 指住得近的人（next-door neighbor 隔壁邻居）。美式拼写 neighbor，英式 neighbour，义同。社区生活、邻里纠纷、互助语境极高频（help a neighbor 帮邻居）。
[例] ① Our neighbors watered the plants while we were away. 我们外出时邻居帮忙浇了花。 ② She complained about noise from the neighbors upstairs. 她抱怨楼上邻居的噪音。
[搭] next-door neighbor 隔壁邻居, friendly neighbor 友好的邻居, neighborhood 街区, neighbor dispute 邻里纠纷
[联] neighborhood 社区/街区, community 社区, friend 朋友, stranger 陌生人, house 房屋, fence 围栏, noise 噪音, help 帮助, local 本地的, resident 居民, neighbourhood：英式拼写
[记] 可数：a neighbor。美式 neighbor，英式 neighbour。love thy neighbor 源自《圣经》，指「爱人如己」的延伸

**colleague**
英/美 /ˈkɒliːɡ/
n. 同事
[义] A person you work with, especially in a profession or business. 一起工作、共事的人（a senior colleague 资深同事）。比 coworker 略正式；不限办公室——医生、教师、议员均可用 colleague。职场社交的核心词。
[例] ① A colleague covered her shift when she was sick. 她生病时一位同事替她值班。 ② He discussed the idea with trusted colleagues before the meeting. 会前他和信得过的同事讨论了想法。
[搭] work colleague 同事, senior colleague 资深同事, former colleague 前同事, colleague from another department 外部门同事
[联] coworker 同事（较口语）, boss 老板, workplace 职场, team 团队, professional 专业人士, office 办公室, peer 同辈, friend 朋友, acquaintance 熟人, collaboration 合作
[记] 可数：a colleague。colleague 可指同级或不同级，只强调共事。不要与 college（大学）拼写混淆

**relationship**
英/美 /rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/
n. 关系
[义] The way two or more people or groups feel and behave toward each other. 人与人、人与组织之间的互动纽带（a close relationship 亲密关系，working relationship 工作关系，romantic relationship 恋爱关系）。极宽义词——语境决定是友情、爱情、亲子还是商业关系。
[形] 复数 relationships
[例] ① They have maintained a good relationship with their neighbors for years. 多年来他们与邻居保持着良好的关系。 ② Trust is the foundation of any healthy relationship. 信任是任何健康关系的基础。
[搭] close relationship 亲密关系, working relationship 工作关系, romantic relationship 恋爱关系, build a relationship 建立关系, relationship with 与……的关系
[联] friend 朋友, family 家庭, bond 纽带, trust 信任, love 爱, partner 伴侣, colleague 同事, connection 联系, intimacy 亲密, conflict 冲突, connection：联系，较中性
[记] 可数：a relationship。in a relationship 常指「有恋爱对象」。relationship to 可指血缘（relationship to the victim 与受害者的关系）

**socialize**
英/美 /ˈsəʊʃəlaɪz/
v. 社交；交往
[义] To meet and spend time with people in a friendly way, especially for pleasure. 与人交往、参加社交活动（socialize with colleagues 与同事社交，don't feel like socializing 不想应酬）。亦指「使社会化」（socialize children 让孩子融入社会）——较次要义。
[形] socialize / socialized / socialized / socializing
[例] ① After moving to a new city, she joined a club to socialize. 搬到新城市后，她加入了俱乐部去社交。 ② He prefers to socialize in small groups rather than at big parties. 他更喜欢小圈子交往，不爱大型派对。
[搭] socialize with 与……交往, socialize at parties 在派对上社交, socializing 社交活动（名词化）, socialize children 使孩子社会化
[联] social 社会的, society 社会, friend 朋友, mingle 交际, party 派对, network 建立人脉, outgoing 外向的, introvert 内向者, gathering 聚会, community 社区, isolate：孤立，反义
[记] 英式拼写 socialise。socialize with 后接人/群体。don't feel like socializing 是常见口语

**society**
英/美 /səˈsaɪəti/
n. 社会；社团；上流社会
[义] ① 全社会、公共生活（modern society 现代社会，a role in society 社会角色）；② 为共同兴趣组成的组织（a historical society 历史学会）；③ 旧时指「上流社会」（high society 上流社会）。与 social / socialize 同根，强调人与人结成的整体结构。
[形] 复数 societies
[例] ① Education plays a key role in any democratic society. 教育在任何民主社会中都至关重要。 ② She joined the local birdwatching society. 她加入了当地的观鸟协会。
[源] 源自拉丁语 societas（同伴关系），socius（同伴）→ 社会即「人们结伴而行的整体」。
[搭] modern society 现代社会, civil society 公民社会, high society 上流社会, society at large 整个社会, historical society 历史学会
[联] social 社会的, community 社区, culture 文化, citizen 公民, public 公众, organization 组织, class 阶层, norm 规范, institution 制度, member 成员, civilization 文明
[记] 可数/不可数依义。the society 可指特定社团；society 泛指社会时不可数。societal 形容词（societal change 社会变迁）

**guest**
英/美 /ɡest/
n. 客人；宾客
[义] A person who is invited to visit someone's home or attend an event. 被邀请到访或出席的人（house guest 住客，wedding guest 婚礼宾客）。与 host 相对：guest 是被接待方。酒店语境中 guest 亦指「住客」（hotel guest 酒店客人）。
[形] 复数 guests
[例] ① We have guests coming for dinner tonight. 今晚有客人来吃晚饭。 ② Each guest received a small gift at the conference. 会上每位来宾都收到了小礼物。
[搭] house guest 住客, wedding guest 婚礼宾客, guest room 客房, guest list 宾客名单, be my guest 请便（口语）
[联] host 主人, hospitality 好客, invitation 邀请, welcome 欢迎, visitor 访客, stranger 陌生人, party 派对, dinner 晚餐, hotel 酒店, attendee 出席者, invitee 被邀请者
[记] 可数：a guest。be my guest 口语表「请自便、不用客气」。guest 作形容词（guest speaker 客座演讲人）

**host**
英/美 /həʊst/
n. 主人；主持人
v. 主办；主持
[义] n. ① 接待客人的人（a gracious host 好客的主人）；② 节目或活动的主持人（TV host 电视主持人）。v. 主办、承办（host a party 办派对，host the Olympics 主办奥运）。与 guest 成对。
[例] ① Our host showed us around the city before dinner. 主人晚饭前带我们逛了城市。 ② The city will host the international film festival next year. 该市明年将主办国际电影节。
[搭] host a party 办派对, host country 东道国, TV host 电视主持人, gracious host 好客的主人, play host to 招待
[联] guest 客人, hospitality 好客, welcome 欢迎, invitation 邀请, event 活动, party 派对, entertain 招待, audience 观众, presenter 主持人（英式）, visitor 访客
[记] 可数：a host。host 亦可指「一大群」（a host of problems 许多问题）——另一义。hostname 为计算机术语

**hospitality**
英/美 /ˌhɒspɪˈtæləti/
n. 好客；款待
[义] Friendly and generous treatment of guests or strangers. 指热情接待客人的传统与行为（warm hospitality 盛情款待，the hospitality industry 酒店餐饮业）。形容词 hospitable（好客的）。西方文化中 declining hospitality 需礼貌说明理由。
[例] ① We were overwhelmed by the hospitality of the local family. 当地家庭的好客让我们受宠若惊。 ② She works in hospitality, managing a boutique hotel. 她从事酒店业，管理一家精品酒店。
[源] 源自拉丁语 hospes（主人、客人、陌生人），与 host、hotel 同根——本义「接待旅人」。
[搭] warm hospitality 盛情款待, hospitality industry 酒店餐饮业, show hospitality 展现好客, hospitable 好客的（形容词）
[联] host 主人, guest 客人, welcome 欢迎, generous 慷慨的, hotel 酒店, restaurant 餐厅, courtesy 礼貌, warmth 热情, invite 邀请, reception 接待
[记] 不可数为主。形容词 hospitable（hospitable culture 好客文化）。hospital 同根但义已分化，勿混淆

**invitation**
英/美 /ˌɪnvɪˈteɪʃn/
n. 邀请；请柬
[义] A written or spoken request to come to an event; the act of inviting. 邀请这一行为或请柬本身（accept an invitation 接受邀请，wedding invitation 婚礼请柬）。社交礼仪中回复 RSVP（请回复是否出席）常与 invitation 并现。
[形] 复数 invitations；动词 invite / invited / invited / inviting
[例] ① She received an invitation to speak at the conference. 她收到了在大会上发言的邀请。 ② Thank you for the invitation, but I already have plans. 谢谢邀请，但我已有安排。
[搭] accept/decline an invitation 接受/谢绝邀请, wedding invitation 婚礼请柬, invitation to ……的邀请, open invitation 随时欢迎的邀请
[联] invite 邀请, guest 客人, host 主人, RSVP 请回复, party 派对, welcome 欢迎, event 活动, card 请柬, attend 出席, decline 谢绝
[记] 可数：an invitation。invitation to + 活动；invitation for sb. 较少用。inviting 形容词亦可表「诱人的」（inviting smell 诱人的香味）——另一义

**invite**
英/美 /ɪnˈvaɪt/
v. 邀请
n. 邀请（口语）
[义] v. To ask someone to come to an event or visit you. 口头或书面请人参加（invite friends over 请朋友来家里，invite sb. to a wedding 邀某人参加婚礼）。n. 口语指「邀请」本身（send an invite 发邀请）——非正式。invite 亦可表「招致、引来」（invite trouble 自找麻烦）。
[形] invite / invited / invited / inviting
[例] ① They invited only close friends and family. 他们只邀请了密友和家人。 ② Don't invite trouble by arguing with the manager. 别跟经理吵，自找麻烦。
[搭] invite sb. to 邀请某人参加, invite over 请人来家里, invite trouble 招惹麻烦, invite comments 征集意见
[联] invitation 邀请, guest 客人, host 主人, welcome 欢迎, party 派对, attend 出席, RSVP 请回复, include 纳入, exclude 排除, request 请求
[记] 动词 invite sb. to sth.；invite sb. over 指请来家里。名词 invite 口语化，正式写作用 invitation

**greet**
英/美 /ɡriːt/
v. 问候；迎接
[义] To say hello to someone or welcome them. 见面时打招呼或迎接（greet guests at the door 在门口迎接客人，greet someone with a smile 微笑着问候）。greeting 为名词（exchange greetings 互致问候）。正式场合 greet 比 say hi 更得体。
[形] greet / greeted / greeted / greeting
[例] ① She greeted each guest as they arrived. 每位客人到达时她都问候致意。 ② The news was greeted with applause. 这一消息赢得了掌声（受到热烈欢迎）。
[搭] greet sb. with a smile 微笑着问候, greet guests 迎接客人, greet the morning 迎接清晨（文学）, exchange greetings 互致问候
[联] welcome 欢迎, hello 你好, greeting 问候, host 主人, guest 客人, meet 见面, introduce 介绍, farewell 告别, wave 挥手, hug 拥抱, ignore 忽视
[记] 及物动词 greet sb.。被动 be greeted with 指「受到某种反应」。greeting card 贺卡

**welcome**
英/美 /ˈwelkəm/
v. 欢迎
n. 欢迎
adj. 受欢迎的
[义] v. To greet someone in a friendly way when they arrive. 友好地迎接来者（welcome visitors 欢迎访客，You're welcome 不客气——回应感谢）。adj. 受欢迎的（a welcome change 可喜的变化）。n. 迎接（give sb. a warm welcome 热烈欢迎）。
[形] welcome / welcomed / welcomed / welcoming
[例] ① The host welcomed us into her home. 主人把我们迎进家门。 ② Any help would be welcome. 任何帮助都欢迎。
[搭] welcome to 欢迎来到, You're welcome 不客气, warm welcome 热烈欢迎, welcome party 欢迎会, welcome change 可喜的变化
[联] greet 问候, host 主人, guest 客人, hospitality 好客, invitation 邀请, arrival 到达, reception 接待, stranger 陌生人, reject 拒绝, unwelcome 不受欢迎的
[记] You're welcome 回应 Thank you。welcome to + 地点（Welcome to London!）。形容词 welcome 后置或前置均可

**farewell**
英/美 /ˌfeəˈwel/
n. 告别
int. 再见
[义] An act of leaving or saying goodbye. 离别、道别（a fond farewell 深情告别，farewell party 欢送会）。比 goodbye 略正式或文学化。bid farewell to 为固定搭配（向……告别）。
[例] ① Colleagues gathered for a farewell lunch on his last day. 他最后一天同事们聚在一起吃了送别午餐。 ② She waved farewell from the train window. 她在火车窗外挥手告别。
[搭] bid farewell to 向……告别, farewell party 欢送会, fond farewell 深情告别, farewell speech 告别演说
[联] goodbye 再见, leave 离开, departure 出发, greet 问候, welcome 欢迎, reunion 重聚, parting 离别, wave 挥手, speech 演说, emotion 情感
[记] 可数：a farewell。farewell 比 goodbye 正式。美式 farewell 亦作感叹词，日常更常说 bye / goodbye

**stranger**
英/美 /ˈstreɪndʒə(r)/
n. 陌生人
[义] A person you do not know. 不认识的人（don't talk to strangers 别和陌生人说话，a complete stranger 完全陌生的人）。stranger 亦作形容词比较级 strange 的派生——「陌生」社会化边界的核心词。与 friend 构成光谱两端。
[例] ① A stranger helped her carry the stroller up the stairs. 一位陌生人帮她把婴儿车抬上楼梯。 ② It's easier to open up to a stranger than to family sometimes. 有时对陌生人反而比家人更容易敞开心扉。
[搭] complete stranger 完全陌生的人, don't talk to strangers 别和陌生人说话, stranger danger 陌生人危险（儿童教育）, perfect stranger 完全不认识的人
[联] friend 朋友, acquaintance 熟人, neighbor 邻居, trust 信任, danger 危险, safety 安全, unknown 未知的, familiar 熟悉的, guest 客人, privacy 隐私
[记] 可数：a stranger。stranger to 可指「对……陌生」（a stranger to fear 不知恐惧为何物）。strange 奇怪的/陌生的

**peer**
英/美 /pɪə(r)/
n. 同辈；同龄人；同等地位的人
v. 凝视
[义] n. A person who is the same age or has the same social position as you. 年龄、地位相近的人（peer pressure 同辈压力，peer group 同龄群体）。学术语境 peer review 指「同行评审」。v. 眯眼细看（peer at the screen 盯着屏幕看）——另一义，靠语境区分。
[例] ① Teenagers are often influenced by peer pressure. 青少年常受同辈压力影响。 ② She discussed the paper with peers in her field. 她与领域内的同行讨论了这篇论文。
[搭] peer pressure 同辈压力, peer group 同龄群体, peer review 同行评审, peer at 凝视, among one's peers 在同辈中
[联] colleague 同事, friend 朋友, age 年龄, group 群体, pressure 压力, equal 平等的, classmate 同学, competition 竞争, respect 尊重, influence 影响, peer（n. 同辈）vs. peer（v. 凝视）：同形异义
[记] 可数：a peer。peer pressure 为教育、心理高频词。英式贵族 peer 亦指「贵族议员」——另一义

**companion**
英/美 /kəmˈpæniən/
n. 同伴；伴侣
[义] A person you spend a lot of time with, especially because you enjoy their company. 长期相伴、共度时光的人，可指朋友、旅伴、伴侣（a travelling companion 旅伴，a faithful companion 忠实的伙伴）。比 friend 略正式或文学；可指宠物（a canine companion 犬类伙伴）。
[例] ① Her dog has been her constant companion for twelve years. 她的狗十二年来一直是她忠实的伙伴。 ② He missed his companion on the long walk home. 长途走回家时，他想念同行的伙伴。
[源] 源自拉丁语 companio，com-（共同）+ panis（面包）→ 同吃一块面包的人 → 同伴。
[搭] travelling companion 旅伴, faithful companion 忠实的伙伴, constant companion 常伴左右的人, companion animal 伴侣动物
[联] friend 朋友, partner 伴侣, company 陪伴, accompany 陪伴, together 一起, loneliness 孤独, bond 纽带, relationship 关系, sidekick 搭档（口语）
[记] 可数：a companion。companion 不必然指恋爱关系；romantic partner 需另说。company 同源，指「陪伴」或「公司」

**bond**
英/美 /bɒnd/
n. 纽带；联系；债券
v. 建立感情；黏合
[义] n. ① 人与人之间的情感联系（a strong bond 牢固的纽带，family bond 家庭纽带）；② 金融「债券」。v. 建立亲密关系（bond with a child 与孩子建立感情）。社交与家庭语境取第一义。
[例] ① Shared experiences created a lasting bond between the two friends. 共同经历让两位朋友建立了持久的纽带。 ② Parents need time to bond with a newborn. 父母需要时间与新生的孩子建立亲情。
[搭] strong bond 牢固纽带, bond with 与……建立感情, family bond 家庭纽带, bond over 因……而亲近, social bond 社会纽带
[联] relationship 关系, friendship 友谊, trust 信任, connection 联系, tie 纽带, family 家庭, love 爱, intimacy 亲密, link 链接, attachment 依恋
[记] 可数：a bond。bond with sb. 为常用搭配。bond 亦指化学键、债券——多义词

**gossip**
英/美 /ˈɡɒsɪp/
n. 流言蜚语；爱说闲话的人
v. 说闲话
[义] Casual talk about other people's private lives, often including details that may not be true. 议论他人私事，常含未经核实成分（office gossip 办公室八卦，gossip about celebrities 议论名人）。动词 gossip about 指「说……的闲话」。社交中负面但极常见。
[形] gossip / gossiped / gossiped / gossiping
[例] ① She tries not to listen to office gossip. 她尽量不听办公室八卦。 ② Neighbors gossiped about the couple's sudden divorce. 邻居们议论那对夫妻突然离婚的事。
[搭] spread gossip 传播流言, office gossip 办公室八卦, gossip about 议论, gossip column 八卦专栏
[联] rumor 谣言, talk 谈论, privacy 隐私, reputation 名声, scandal 丑闻, whisper 低声说, news 消息, truth 真相, colleague 同事, neighbor 邻居
[记] 不可数/可数：some gossip / a piece of gossip。gossip 含贬义，形容人 a gossip 指「长舌妇/八卦的人」。动词 gossip with/about

**rumor**
英 /ˈruːmə(r)/ 美 /ˈruːmər/
n. 谣言；传闻
v. 谣传
[义] Information or a story that is passed from person to person but has not been proven true. 未经证实的传闻（spread a rumor 散布谣言，rumor has it that ……据传闻……）。比 gossip 更强调真假未明；美式 rumor，英式 rumour。政治、职场、社交媒体语境极高频。
