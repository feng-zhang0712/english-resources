# 教育与学习

**education**
英/美 /ˌedʒuˈkeɪʃn/
n. 教育
[义] The process of teaching and learning, especially in schools and universities. 在学校等机构中传授知识、培养能力的系统过程（higher education 高等教育，public education 公共教育）。比 schooling 更宽，可指理念与制度层面。
[例] ① Education is often seen as a path to better opportunities. 教育常被视为通往更好机会的途径。 ② Parents care deeply about their children's education. 父母非常关心子女的教育。
[搭] higher education 高等教育, public education 公共教育, education system 教育体系, quality education 优质教育, value of education 教育的价值
[联] school 学校, student 学生, teacher 老师, learn 学习, study 学习, degree 学位, literacy 读写能力, classroom 教室, university 大学, training 培训
[记] 不可数为主。educational 形容词。

**student**
英/美 /ˈstjuːdnt/
n. 学生
[义] A person who is studying at a school, college, or university. 在学校或大学就读、接受教育的人（college student 大学生，international student 留学生）。教育场最核心角色词之一。
[例] ① The students handed in their homework before the deadline. 学生在截止日前交了作业。 ② She works part-time as a student. 她读书期间兼职打工。
[搭] college student 大学生, international student 留学生, graduate student 研究生, student loan 学生贷款, student life 学生生活
[联] teacher 老师, pupil 小学生, classroom 教室, school 学校, campus 校园, course 课程, exam 考试, learn 学习, degree 学位, classmate 同学
[记] 可数：a student。student 亦可指「研究者」（a student of history 历史研究者）

**teacher**
英/美 /ˈtiːtʃə(r)/
n. 教师；老师
[义] A person whose job is to teach in a school. 以教书为职业的人（primary school teacher 小学教师，English teacher 英语老师）。比 professor 更泛指中小学及一般教学岗位。
[例] ① The teacher explained the grammar clearly. 老师把语法讲得很清楚。 ② Teachers often buy classroom supplies with their own money. 教师常自掏腰包买教具。
[搭] school teacher 学校教师, English teacher 英语老师, teacher training 师资培训, substitute teacher 代课老师, teacher's pet 老师宠儿（口语）
[联] student 学生, classroom 教室, lesson 课程, teach 教, education 教育, principal 校长, professor 教授, tutor 家教, pupil 小学生, homework 作业
[记] 可数：a teacher。

**professor**
英/美 /prəˈfesə(r)/
n. 教授
[义] A senior teacher at a college or university, especially one with the highest rank. 大学里的高级教师，尤指最高职级者（professor of economics 经济学教授，full professor 正教授）。比 teacher 更专指高等教育。
[例] ① The professor published a new research paper. 这位教授发表了一篇新研究论文。 ② She became a professor at thirty-five. 她三十五岁成为教授。
[搭] full professor 正教授, associate professor 副教授, professor of ……教授, guest professor 客座教授, professor emeritus 荣誉退休教授
[联] university 大学, lecturer 讲师, faculty 院系教师, student 学生, research 研究, campus 校园, degree 学位, academic 学术的, dean 院长, tenure 终身教职
[记] 可数：a professor。缩写 Prof.。professor 不限理科，人文社科同样使用

**pupil**
英/美 /ˈpjuːpl/
n. 小学生；弟子
[义] A child who goes to school, especially at primary level. 就读学校的孩子，尤指小学阶段（school pupil 在校生）。英式常用；美式更常说 student。亦指「门生、弟子」。
[例] ① The pupils lined up before morning assembly. 小学生在晨会前排队。 ② Class sizes affect how much attention each pupil gets. 班级规模影响每个学生能得到的关注。
[搭] school pupil 在校生, primary pupil 小学生, pupil performance 学生表现, pupil-teacher ratio 师生比
[联] student 学生, teacher 老师, school 学校, classroom 教室, primary school 小学, child 孩子, learn 学习, education 教育
[记] 可数：a pupil。英式 pupil 常指中小学；美式多用 student。pupil 亦指「瞳孔」——另一义

**tutor**
英/美 /ˈtjuːtə(r)/
n. 家教；导师
v. 辅导
[义] A private teacher who helps one student or a small group outside regular classes. 课外一对一或小班辅导者（private tutor 私人家教，math tutor 数学家教）。大学语境亦指「导师」（personal tutor 个人导师）。
[例] ① She hired a tutor to help with chemistry. 她请了家教补化学。 ② He tutors high school students on weekends. 他周末给高中生做辅导。
[搭] private tutor 私人家教, math tutor 数学家教, tutor session 辅导课, personal tutor 个人导师, tutor sb. in 辅导某人……
[联] teacher 老师, student 学生, homework 作业, lesson 课程, tutorial 辅导课, study 学习, coach 教练, mentor 导师, exam 考试
[记] 可数：a tutor。tutorial 名词/形容词。tutor 动词：tutor sb. in math

**faculty**
英/美 /ˈfæklti/
n. 院系；全体教员
[义] ① 大学里的学院或系（faculty of law 法学院）；② 全体教师（faculty members 教职员工）。美式常用；英式 faculty 多指院系，staff 指教职员。
[例] ① She joined the faculty of engineering in 2020. 她 2020 年进入工程学院任教。 ② The faculty voted on the new curriculum. 全体教员对新课程投了票。
[搭] faculty member 教职员工, faculty of law 法学院, faculty meeting 教职工会议, faculty advisor 学业导师
[联] professor 教授, university 大学, dean 院长, department 系, lecturer 讲师, campus 校园, academic 学术的, staff 员工, college 学院
[记] 可数/集合名词。faculty 亦指「能力、官能」——多义。美式 the faculty = 全体教师

**principal**
英/美 /ˈprɪnsəpl/
n. 校长
adj. 主要的
[义] n. The person in charge of a school. 中小学校的负责人（school principal 校长）。adj. 主要的——与 principle（原则）同音不同义，勿混。
[例] ① The principal announced a new attendance policy. 校长宣布了新的出勤规定。 ② Parents met with the principal about bullying. 家长就霸凌问题与校长面谈。
[搭] school principal 校长, principal's office 校长室, vice principal 副校长, meet the principal 见校长
[联] headteacher 校长, teacher 老师, school 学校, student 学生, discipline 纪律, parent 家长, administration 管理, campus 校园
[记] 可数：a principal。principal vs. principle：校长 vs. 原则，拼写易混

**headteacher**
英 /ˌhedˈtiːtʃə(r)/ 美 /ˌhedˈtiːtʃər/
n. 校长
[义] The teacher who is in charge of a school. 负责管理学校的教师，即校长（英式常用，与 principal 同义）。美式更常说 principal。
[例] ① The headteacher welcomed new pupils on the first day. 校长在开学第一天欢迎新生。 ② She has been headteacher for twelve years. 她当校长十二年了。
[搭] deputy headteacher 副校长, headteacher's office 校长室, meet the headteacher 见校长
[联] principal 校长, teacher 老师, school 学校, pupil 小学生, staff 教职工, parent 家长, leadership 领导
[记] 可数：a headteacher。英式；美式 principal。亦说 headmaster/headmistress（较传统）

**dean**
英/美 /diːn/
n. 院长；系主任
[义] A person in a university who is in charge of a faculty or department. 大学中负责某学院或系的行政领导（dean of students 学生事务主任，dean's list 院长荣誉名单）。
[例] ① The dean approved her research proposal. 院长批准了她的研究计划。 ② He made the dean's list three semesters in a row. 他连续三个学期上了院长荣誉名单。
[搭] dean of students 学生事务主任, dean's list 院长荣誉名单, faculty dean 学院院长, meet the dean 见院长
[联] faculty 院系, professor 教授, university 大学, student 学生, academic 学术的, administration 管理, department 系
[记] 可数：a dean。dean's list 美式荣誉制度，表学业优秀

**classroom**
英/美 /ˈklɑːsruːm/
n. 教室
[义] A room where students are taught. 学生上课的房间（classroom management 课堂管理，empty classroom 空教室）。校园日常极高频。
[例] ① The students returned to the classroom after recess. 课间休息后学生回到教室。 ② Each classroom has a smart board now. 每间教室现在都有电子白板。
[搭] classroom management 课堂管理, classroom activity 课堂活动, empty classroom 空教室, classroom rules 课堂规则
[联] teacher 老师, student 学生, school 学校, lesson 课程, desk 课桌, board 黑板, campus 校园, learn 学习
[记] 可数：a classroom。class + room 复合词

**campus**
英/美 /ˈkæmpəs/
n. 校园
[义] The land and buildings of a university or college. 大学或学院的场地与建筑群（on campus 在校内，campus life 校园生活）。可数；亦可指大公司园区（corporate campus）。
[例] ① She lived on campus during her first year. 她第一年住在校内。 ② The campus library stays open until midnight. 校园图书馆开到午夜。
[搭] on campus 在校内, off campus 在校外, campus life 校园生活, campus tour 校园参观, university campus 大学校园
[联] university 大学, college 学院, student 学生, dormitory 宿舍, library 图书馆, school 学校, lecture 讲座, graduation 毕业
[记] 可数：a campus。on/off campus 固定搭配

**library**
英/美 /ˈlaɪbreri/
n. 图书馆
[义] A building or room containing books and resources for reading and study. 收藏书籍与资料、供阅读学习的场所（school library 学校图书馆，borrow from the library 从图书馆借书）。
[例] ① She spent hours in the library preparing for exams. 她在图书馆花了数小时备考。 ② The library offers quiet study rooms. 图书馆提供安静自习室。
[搭] school library 学校图书馆, public library 公共图书馆, library card 借书证, borrow from the library 从图书馆借书
[联] book 书, textbook 课本, study 学习, student 学生, campus 校园, research 研究, read 阅读, quiet 安静的
[记] 可数：a library。librarian 图书管理员。library 亦指「程序库」——计算机义

**dormitory**
英/美 /ˈdɔːmətri/
n. 宿舍
[义] A building at a college or boarding school where students live. 大学或寄宿学校里学生居住的楼房（live in a dormitory 住宿舍）。口语常缩写 dorm。
[例] ① Freshmen usually stay in a dormitory on campus. 大一新生通常住校内宿舍。 ② The dorm was noisy on Friday nights. 周五晚上宿舍很吵。
[搭] live in a dormitory 住宿舍, dorm room 宿舍房间, dorm life 宿舍生活, on-campus dormitory 校内宿舍
[联] campus 校园, student 学生, roommate 室友, college 大学, boarding school 寄宿学校, housing 住房, rent 租金
[记] 可数：a dormitory / dorm。英式 hall of residence 同义

**lecture hall**
英/美 /ˈlektʃə hɔːl/
n. 阶梯教室；大讲堂
[义] A large room with tiered seating where lectures are given. 设有阶梯座位、用于上大课的房间（packed lecture hall 座无虚席的大讲堂）。大学常见设施。
[例] ① Over three hundred students filled the lecture hall. 三百多名学生坐满了阶梯教室。 ② She lost her notes somewhere in the lecture hall. 她把笔记落在大讲堂某处了。
[搭] packed lecture hall 满座的大讲堂, lecture hall seating 阶梯座位, enter the lecture hall 进入阶梯教室
[联] lecture 讲座, professor 教授, student 学生, campus 校园, seminar 研讨课, university 大学, auditorium 礼堂
[记] 可数：a lecture hall。

**laboratory**
英/美 /ləˈbɒrətri/
n. 实验室
[义] A room or building equipped for scientific experiments or teaching practical skills. 配备仪器、用于实验或实践教学的房间（science laboratory 科学实验室，language lab 语音室）。口语常缩写 lab。
[例] ① Students must wear goggles in the chemistry laboratory. 学生在化学实验室必须戴护目镜。 ② The lab report is due on Friday. 实验报告周五截止。
[搭] science laboratory 科学实验室, language lab 语音室, lab report 实验报告, lab equipment 实验设备, computer lab 计算机机房
[联] experiment 实验, science 科学, research 研究, student 学生, professor 教授, campus 校园, test 测试
[记] 可数：a laboratory / lab。美式常读 /ˈlæbrətɔːri/

**cafeteria**
英/美 /ˌkæfəˈtɪəriə/
n. 自助餐厅；食堂
[义] A restaurant, especially in a school or workplace, where you choose food and pay at a counter. 自选食物、柜台结账的餐厅，多设于学校或单位（school cafeteria 学校食堂）。校园生活高频词。
[例] ① They met for lunch in the cafeteria. 他们在食堂吃午饭。 ② The cafeteria serves vegetarian options every day. 食堂每天提供素食。
[搭] school cafeteria 学校食堂, cafeteria food 食堂饭菜, cafeteria line 打饭队伍, eat in the cafeteria 在食堂吃
[联] lunch 午餐, student 学生, campus 校园, food 食物, meal 餐, school 学校, dining hall 餐厅（英式）
[记] 可数：a cafeteria。亦拼 canteen（英式学校食堂）

**auditorium**
英/美 /ˌɔːdɪˈtɔːriəm/
n. 礼堂；观众席
[义] A large hall for audiences, especially for performances, ceremonies, or assemblies. 供演出、典礼或集会使用的大厅（school auditorium 学校礼堂）。graduation 典礼常在此举行。
[例] ① The whole school gathered in the auditorium. 全校师生聚集在礼堂。 ② Parents filled the auditorium for the spring concert. 家长们坐满了礼堂观看春季音乐会。
[搭] school auditorium 学校礼堂, auditorium seating 观众席, fill the auditorium 座无虚席
[联] graduation 毕业典礼, ceremony 典礼, performance 演出, stage 舞台, audience 观众, school 学校, concert 音乐会
[记] 可数：an auditorium。复数 auditoriums / auditoria

**elementary school**
英/美 /ˌelɪˈmentri skuːl/
n. 小学
[义] A school for young children, usually ages about six to eleven. 面向幼童的基础教育学校，通常约六至十一岁（美式常用）。英式更常说 primary school。
[例] ① She taught elementary school for twenty years. 她教了二十年小学。 ② Elementary school students learn reading and basic math. 小学生学习阅读和基础数学。
[搭] elementary school teacher 小学教师, elementary school student 小学生, attend elementary school 上小学
[联] primary school 小学, middle school 中学, high school 高中, pupil 小学生, teacher 老师, kindergarten 幼儿园, education 教育
[记] 美式说法。英式 primary school。elementary 亦指「基础的、初级的」

**primary school**
英/美 /ˈpraɪməri skuːl/
n. 小学
[义] A school for children between about five and eleven years old. 约五至十一岁儿童就读的学校（英式常用）。与 elementary school 同义，地域偏好不同。
[例] ① He walked his daughter to primary school every morning. 他每天早上送女儿上小学。 ② Primary school builds the foundation for later learning. 小学为后续学习打基础。
[搭] primary school teacher 小学教师, primary school pupil 小学生, local primary school 当地小学
[联] elementary school 小学, secondary school 中学, pupil 小学生, kindergarten 幼儿园, education 教育, child 孩子
[记] 英式常用；美式 elementary school。primary education 小学教育阶段

**middle school**
英/美 /ˈmɪdl skuːl/
n. 初中；中学
[义] A school for children between elementary and high school, typically ages about eleven to fourteen. 介于小学与高中之间的学校，通常约十一至十四岁（美式初中）。英式可能称 lower secondary。
[例] ① Middle school can be a challenging time socially. 初中在社交上可能是艰难时期。 ② She started playing in the middle school band. 她加入了初中乐队。
[搭] middle school student 初中生, middle school teacher 初中教师, attend middle school 上初中
[联] high school 高中, elementary school 小学, teenager 青少年, student 学生, adolescence 青春期, subject 学科
[记] 美式教育体系：elementary → middle → high school。英式结构不同

**high school**
英/美 /ˈhaɪ skuːl/
n. 高中
[义] A school for students typically aged about fourteen to eighteen. 通常十四至十八岁学生就读的中学（high school diploma 高中文凭，high school senior 高三学生）。美式核心教育阶段词。
[例] ① He played basketball all four years of high school. 他高中四年都打篮球。 ② High school graduation is a major milestone. 高中毕业是重要里程碑。
[搭] high school student 高中生, high school diploma 高中文凭, high school senior 高三学生, after high school 高中毕业后
[联] middle school 初中, college 大学, student 学生, diploma 文凭, graduation 毕业, exam 考试, senior 高年级生, teenager 青少年
[记] 可数/不可数依语境。英式 secondary school 更宽。high school 在美国特指 9–12 年级

**secondary school**
英/美 /ˈsekəndri skuːl/
n. 中学
[义] A school for students between primary school and higher education. 小学之后、高等教育之前的学校（英式常用，涵盖初中与高中）。比 high school 更宽、更正式。
[例] ① She teaches science at a secondary school in Manchester. 她在曼彻斯特一所中学教科学。 ② Secondary school education is compulsory in many countries. 许多国家中学教育为义务教育。
[搭] secondary school teacher 中学教师, secondary school student 中学生, secondary education 中学教育
[联] primary school 小学, high school 高中, student 学生, education 教育, exam 考试, subject 学科, graduate 毕业
[记] 英式常用。secondary education 中学教育阶段。美式 high school 更具体

**academy**
英/美 /əˈkædəmi/
n. 学院；专科院校
[义] A school or college that specializes in a particular subject or skill. 专攻某学科或技能的院校（military academy 军校，academy of music 音乐学院）。亦指「学会、研究院」。
[例] ① He graduated from a naval academy. 他毕业于海军学院。 ② The film academy announced this year's nominees. 电影学院公布了今年提名名单。
[搭] military academy 军校, academy of music 音乐学院, police academy 警校, academy award 学院奖
[联] school 学校, college 大学, training 培训, student 学生, education 教育, institute 学院, vocational 职业的
[记] 可数：an academy。the Academy 常特指奥斯卡主办方

**boarding school**
英/美 /ˈbɔːdɪŋ skuːl/
n. 寄宿学校
[义] A school where students live during the term. 学期内学生住校就读的学校（attend boarding school 上寄宿学校）。与 day school 相对。
[例] ① He spent his teenage years at a boarding school in Switzerland. 他青少年时期在瑞士一所寄宿学校就读。 ② Boarding school students return home for holidays. 寄宿生假期回家。
[搭] attend boarding school 上寄宿学校, boarding school student 寄宿生, elite boarding school 精英寄宿学校
[联] dormitory 宿舍, school 学校, pupil 学生, private school 私立学校, homeschooled 在家受教的, parent 父母
[记] 可数：a boarding school。board 此处指「膳宿」，非「木板」

**vocational school**
英/美 /vəʊˈkeɪʃənl skuːl/
n. 职业学校
[义] A school that teaches skills needed for a particular job or trade. 教授特定职业或行业所需技能的学校（vocational training 职业培训）。与 academic track 相对。
[例] ① She learned welding at a vocational school. 她在职业学校学了焊接。 ② Vocational schools prepare students for skilled trades. 职校为学生进入熟练工种做准备。
[搭] attend vocational school 上职校, vocational training 职业培训, vocational education 职业教育, vocational course 职业课程
[联] apprentice 学徒, trade 行业, skill 技能, certificate 证书, job 工作, career 职业, college 学院
[记] 可数：a vocational school。vocational 形容词。亦说 trade school

**nursery school**
英/美 /ˈnɪsəri skuːl/
