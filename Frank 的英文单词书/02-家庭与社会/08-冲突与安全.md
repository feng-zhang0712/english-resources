# 冲突与安全

**conflict**
英/美 /ˈkɒnflɪkt/
n. 冲突；矛盾
v. 冲突；抵触
[义] Serious disagreement or fighting between people, groups, or countries. 人与人、群体或国家之间的严重分歧或武装对抗（armed conflict 武装冲突，conflict zone 冲突地区，conflict of interest 利益冲突）。可数/不可数。动词义指时间、利益等相互抵触。
[例] ① The conflict displaced thousands of civilians. 冲突使数千平民流离失所。 ② Their schedules conflict, so the meeting was moved. 日程冲突，会议改期。
[搭] armed conflict 武装冲突, conflict zone 冲突地区, conflict of interest 利益冲突, resolve a conflict 解决冲突, internal conflict 内部冲突
[联] war 战争, violence 暴力, battle 战斗, peace 和平, dispute 争端, tension 紧张, fight 打斗, attack 攻击
[记] 可数/不可数。conflict with 与……冲突（动词）。本章侧重武装冲突与安全问题

**war**
英/美 /wɔː(r)/
n. 战争
[义] Large-scale fighting between countries or organized groups. 国家或组织之间的大规模武装对抗（declare war 宣战，civil war 内战，war zone 战区）。不可数/可数。
[例] ① The war forced millions to flee their homes. 战争迫使数百万人逃离家园。 ② Leaders warned that war would bring only suffering. 领导人警告战争只会带来苦难。
[搭] declare war 宣战, civil war 内战, war zone 战区, go to war 开战, war crime 战争罪
[联] peace 和平, battle 战斗, military 军事的, soldier 士兵, conflict 冲突, weapon 武器, ceasefire 停火, warfare 战争
[记] 不可数/可数。war on + 抽象名（war on terror 反恐战争）

**peace**
英/美 /piːs/
n. 和平；安宁
[义] A period when there is no war; freedom from violence or disturbance. 无战争的状态；免于暴力或骚乱（at peace 处于和平状态，peace treaty 和平条约，keep the peace 维持秩序）。不可数。
[例] ① Both sides agreed to work toward peace. 双方同意致力于和平。 ② The city enjoyed years of peace after the ceasefire. 停火后该市享有多年和平。
[搭] at peace 处于和平状态, peace treaty 和平条约, keep the peace 维持秩序, peace talks 和谈, world peace 世界和平
[联] war 战争, ceasefire 停火, treaty 条约, peaceful 和平的, peacekeeping 维和, conflict 冲突
[记] 不可数。make peace 和解。peaceful 形容词

**violence**
英/美 /ˈvaɪələns/
n. 暴力；猛烈
[义] Behaviour that is intended to hurt or kill people; physical force used to harm. 意在伤害或杀害他人的行为；以武力加害（domestic violence 家庭暴力，act of violence 暴力行为，violence against civilians 针对平民的暴力）。不可数。
[例] ① The report documented violence against protesters. 报告记录了针对抗议者的暴力。 ② Violence broke out after the riot. 骚乱后爆发暴力。
[搭] domestic violence 家庭暴力, act of violence 暴力行为, violence against civilians 针对平民的暴力, resort to violence 诉诸暴力, gun violence 枪支暴力
[联] attack 攻击, assault 袭击, weapon 武器, conflict 冲突, war 战争, peaceful 和平的
[记] 不可数。violent 形容词。domestic violence 为法律与新闻高频固定搭配

**military**
英/美 /ˈmɪlətri/
adj. 军事的；军队的
n. 军队；军方
[义] Relating to armed forces or war; the armed forces as an organization. 与武装部队或战争相关的；武装部队整体（military base 军事基地，military intervention 军事干预，join the military 参军）。形容词/名词集合。
[例] ① Military troops secured the border. 军事部队控制边境。 ② The military denied targeting civilians. 军方否认以平民为目标。
[搭] military base 军事基地, military intervention 军事干预, join the military 参军, military operation 军事行动, military coup 军事政变
[联] army 陆军, navy 海军, air force 空军, soldier 士兵, war 战争, combat 战斗, security forces 安全部队
[记] 通常 the military 指武装部队整体

**army**
英/美 /ˈɑːmi/

[例] ① He served in the army for five years. 他在陆军服役五年。 ② The army deployed troops to the frontline. 陆军向前线部署部队。
[搭] join the army 参军, army base 陆军基地, army officer 陆军军官, standing army 常备军, army veteran 陆军老兵
[联] military 军事的, navy 海军, air force 空军, soldier 士兵, troops 部队, combat 战斗
[记] 可数：an army。the Army 大写指具体国家陆军。army of + 名词 比喻「大批……」

**troops**
英/美 /truːps/
n. 部队；士兵
[义] Soldiers, especially in large numbers; military units. 士兵，尤指大批；军事单位（deploy troops 部署部队，withdraw troops 撤军，peacekeeping troops 维和部队）。复数。
[例] ① Troops were sent to secure the evacuation route. 部队被派去保障撤离路线。 ② Foreign troops began to pull out after the ceasefire. 停火后外国部队开始撤出。

[记] 通常复数。a troop of 可指「一群」（a troop of scouts 一队童子军）。troop 单数指「部队单位」

**combat**
英/美 /ˈkɒmbæt/
n. 战斗；格斗
v. 与……战斗；打击
[义] Fighting between armed forces; to take action to stop something harmful. 武装部队之间的交战；采取行动制止有害事物（combat zone 作战区，hand-to-hand combat 肉搏，combat terrorism 打击恐怖主义）。不可数/动词。
