# 体育与休闲

**sport**
英/美 /spɔːt/
n. 体育运动
v. 穿戴；炫耀
[义] Physical activity done for exercise or competition. 为锻炼或竞赛而进行的体育活动（team sport 团队运动，do sport 做运动，watch sport 看比赛）。可数/不可数。英式常用 sports 复数。
[例] ① Sport brings communities together on weekends. 体育让社区在周末聚在一起。 ② She sports a new team jersey at every match. 每场比赛她都穿新队服。
[搭] team sport 团队运动, do sport 做运动, watch sport 看比赛, sport event 体育赛事
[联] athlete 运动员, game 比赛, match 比赛, team 队伍, stadium 体育场, league 联赛, fitness 健身
[记] 可数/不可数。sport vs. game：sport 指项目类别，game 指具体一场。

**athletics**
英/美 /æθˈletɪks/
n. 田径运动；体育运动
[义] Track-and-field sports, or sports and physical exercise in general. 田径项目，或广义的体育运动（school athletics 学校体育，athletics club 田径俱乐部）。不可数/复数。
[例] ① She competed in athletics at university. 她在大学参加田径比赛。 ② Athletics coaches focus on speed and strength. 田径教练侧重速度与力量。
[搭] school athletics 学校体育, athletics club 田径俱乐部, athletics meet 田径运动会, athletics track 田径跑道
[联] sport 体育, athlete 运动员, track 跑道, marathon 马拉松, stadium 体育场, championship 锦标赛
[记] 通常复数或不可数。

**athlete**
英/美 /ˈæθliːt/
n. 运动员
[义] A person who is trained in or good at sports and physical exercise. 受训或擅长体育运动的人（professional athlete 职业运动员，student athlete 学生运动员，elite athlete 顶尖运动员）。可数。
[例] ① The athlete broke a world record in the final. 运动员在决赛中打破世界纪录。 ② Athletes need rest between seasons. 运动员在赛季之间需要休息。
[搭] professional athlete 职业运动员, student athlete 学生运动员, elite athlete 顶尖运动员, Olympic athlete 奥运选手
[联] sport 体育, team 队伍, coach 教练, training 训练, championship 锦标赛, fitness 体能
[记] 可数：an athlete。

**team**
英/美 /tiːm/
n. 队伍；团队
[义] A group of players who play a sport together against another group. 一起参赛、对抗另一组的球员集体（national team 国家队，home team 主队，team captain 队长）。可数。
[例] ① The team won the championship after overtime. 队伍在加时赛后赢得冠军。 ② Team spirit matters as much as skill. 团队精神与技术同样重要。
[搭] national team 国家队, home team 主队, team captain 队长, team sport 团队运动
[联] player 球员, match 比赛, coach 教练, league 联赛, stadium 体育场, victory 胜利
[记] 可数：a team。

**player**
英/美 /ˈpleɪə(r)/
n. 运动员；选手
[义] A person who takes part in a sport or game. 参与某项运动或比赛的人（key player 关键球员，team player 团队型球员，star player 明星球员）。可数。
[例] ① The star player scored the winning goal. 明星球员攻入制胜球。 ② Players warmed up on the pitch before kickoff. 开球前球员在球场热身。
[搭] key player 关键球员, team player 团队型球员, star player 明星球员, substitute player 替补球员
[联] team 队伍, athlete 运动员, coach 教练, game 比赛, bench 替补席, roster 名单
[记] 可数：a player。亦指「游戏玩家」——语境区分

**match**
英/美 /mætʃ/
n. 比赛
v. 与……匹敌
[义] A sports contest between two people or teams. 两人或两队之间的体育竞赛（football match 足球比赛，win a match 赢得比赛，friendly match 友谊赛）。可数。
[例] ① The match ended in a draw. 比赛以平局结束。 ② Fans packed the stadium for the match. 球迷挤满体育场观看比赛。
[搭] football match 足球比赛, win a match 赢得比赛, friendly match 友谊赛, live match 现场比赛
[联] game 比赛, team 队伍, score 得分, tournament 锦标赛, final 决赛, spectator 观众
[记] 可数：a match。match vs. game：英式常用 match（足球），美式 game 更常见（篮球）

**game**
英/美 /ɡeɪm/
n. 比赛；游戏
[义] A single occasion of sport played according to rules. 按规则进行的一次体育竞赛（home game 主场比赛，play a game 打一场比赛，video game 电子游戏）。可数。
[例] ① They lost the game in the last minute. 他们在最后一分钟输掉比赛。 ② Board games are popular family pastimes. 桌游是受欢迎的家庭消遣。
[搭] home game 主场比赛, play a game 打一场比赛, video game 电子游戏, board game 桌游
[联] match 比赛, sport 体育, player 球员, score 得分, team 队伍, leisure 休闲
[记] 可数：a game。

**score**
英/美 /skɔː(r)/
n. 得分；比分
v. 得分
[义] The number of points each team has in a game; to win points. 比赛中各队得分；取得分数（final score 最终比分，score a goal 进球，keep score 记分）。可数/动词。
[例] ① The score was tied at two-all. 比分二比二平。 ② She scored in the final minute. 她在最后一分钟得分。
[搭] final score 最终比分, score a goal 进球, keep score 记分, high score 高分
[联] goal 进球, win 赢, lose 输, match 比赛, team 队伍, championship 冠军赛
[记] score / scored / scoring。

**championship**

[搭] world championship 世界锦标赛, win the championship 赢得冠军, championship game 冠军赛, championship title 冠军头衔
[联] champion 冠军, tournament 锦标赛, league 联赛, final 决赛, season 赛季, medal 奖牌
[记] 可数：a championship。

**champion**
英/美 /ˈtʃæmpiən/
n. 冠军
v. 捍卫；拥护
[义] The winner of a competition; a person who fights for a cause. 竞赛获胜者；为某事业奋斗的人（world champion 世界冠军，defending champion 卫冕冠军，reigning champion 现任冠军）。可数。
[例] ① The champion lifted the trophy after the final. 冠军在决赛后举起奖杯。 ② She championed fair play in sport. 她在体育中倡导公平竞赛。

[联] championship 锦标赛, medal 奖牌, victory 胜利, athlete 运动员, tournament 锦标赛, title 头衔
[记] 可数：a champion。

**tournament**
英/美 /ˈtʊənəmənt/
n. 锦标赛
[义] A series of games or matches to decide a winner. 决出胜者的一系列比赛（tennis tournament 网球锦标赛，knockout tournament 淘汰赛，round-robin tournament 循环赛）。可数。
[例] ① She entered a tournament in Madrid. 她参加了马德里锦标赛。 ② The tournament bracket filled with underdogs. 锦标赛对阵表满是冷门选手。

[联] championship 锦标赛, match 比赛, final 决赛, seed 种子选手, league 联赛, medal 奖牌
[记] 可数：a tournament。

**league**
英/美 /liːɡ/
n. 联赛
[义] A group of sports teams that play each other over a season. 在赛季中相互比赛的球队组织（Premier League 英超，league table 积分榜，top of the league 联赛榜首）。可数。
[例] ① The club was promoted to a higher league. 俱乐部升入更高级联赛。 ② League leaders lost a key match. 联赛领头羊输掉关键比赛。

[联] season 赛季, team 队伍, championship 冠军, match 比赛, promotion 升级, relegation 降级
[记] 可数：a league。

**season**
英/美 /ˈsiːzn/
n. 赛季
[义] The period when a sport is regularly played. 某项运动常规进行的时期（regular season 常规赛，off-season 休赛期，season opener 赛季揭幕战）。可数。亦指「季节」——另一义。
[例] ① The season starts in September. 赛季九月开始。 ② Injuries ended his season early. 伤病使他赛季提前结束。
[搭] regular season 常规赛, off-season 休赛期, season opener 赛季揭幕战, mid-season 赛季中期
[联] league 联赛, playoff 季后赛, team 队伍, championship 冠军, match 比赛, training 训练
[记] 可数：a season。季节义靠语境

**playoff**
英/美 /ˈpleɪɒf/
n. 季后赛
[义] A series of games after the regular season to decide a champion. 常规赛结束后决出冠军的系列赛（make the playoffs 进入季后赛，playoff game 季后赛比赛）。可数。常用复数 playoffs。
[例] ① The team clinched a playoff spot. 队伍锁定季后赛席位。 ② Playoffs often produce dramatic upsets. 季后赛常出现戏剧性冷门。
[搭] make the playoffs 进入季后赛, playoff game 季后赛比赛, playoff race 季后赛争夺, playoff berth 季后赛名额
[联] season 赛季, championship 冠军, final 决赛, league 联赛, team 队伍, semifinal 半决赛
[记] 可数：a playoff。常用复数 the playoffs

**fan**
英/美 /fæn/
n. 球迷；粉丝

[义] A person who enthusiastically supports a team or athlete. 热情支持某队或运动员的人（football fan 球迷，die-hard fan 铁杆粉丝，fan club 球迷俱乐部）。可数。

**stadium**
英/美 /ˈsteɪdiəm/

**American football**

[义] A player or team allowed into a tournament without qualifying through normal ranking. 未经常规排名晋级、获特许入赛的选手或队伍（wildcard entry 外卡名额，receive a wildcard 获得外卡）。可数。亦拼 wild card（两词）。
[例] ① She received a wildcard into the main draw. 她获外卡进入正赛签表。 ② Wildcards often upset higher seeds early. 外卡选手常在早期爆冷击败高种子。
[搭] wildcard entry 外卡名额, receive a wildcard 获得外卡, wildcard spot 外卡席位, wildcard winner 外卡选手夺冠
[联] tournament 锦标赛, seed 种子选手, qualify 晋级, draw 签表, upset 爆冷, tennis 网球
[记] 可数：a wildcard。IT 领域 wildcard 亦指通配符——多义
