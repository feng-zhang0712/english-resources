# 法律与司法

**law**
英/美 /lɔː/
n. 法律；法学
[义] The system of rules that a country or community recognizes as regulating the actions of its members. 国家或社会用以规范成员行为的规则体系（break the law 违法，law and order 治安）。不可数为主；可数指「某项具体法律」（a new law 新法）。比 rule 更绑国家强制力与司法程序。
[例] ① It is against the law to drive without a licence. 无证驾驶违法。 ② Congress passed a law requiring safer workplaces. 国会通过了一项要求更安全工作场所的法律。
[搭] break the law 违法, obey the law 守法, law and order 治安, pass a law 通过法律, against the law 违法的
[联] legal 法律的, illegal 非法的, justice 正义, court 法庭, rule 规则, regulation 规章, lawyer 律师, crime 犯罪, government 政府, constitution 宪法
[记] 不可数为主。the law 亦可指「执法机关」（call the law 报警）。law school 法学院。

**legal**
英/美 /ˈliːɡl/
adj. 法律的；合法的
[义] Connected with the law; allowed by law. 与法律相关，或经法律允许（legal advice 法律意见，legal action 法律诉讼）。与 illegal 反义。比 lawful 更常用于日常与新闻（legal rights 合法权利）。
[例] ① You should seek legal advice before signing the contract. 签合同前应寻求法律意见。 ② Is it legal to record conversations without consent? 未经同意录音合法吗？
[搭] legal advice 法律意见, legal action 法律诉讼, legal rights 合法权利, legal system 法律体系, legal document 法律文件
[联] law 法律, illegal 非法的, lawyer 律师, court 法庭, justice 正义, legitimate 合法的, lawful 合法的, contract 合同, regulation 规章
[记] legally adv.。legal 亦可指「法定的」（legal age 法定年龄）。与 legitimate 近义，legitimate 更强调「正当、合乎情理」

**illegal**
英/美 /ɪˈliːɡl/
adj. 非法的；违法的
[义] Not allowed by law. 法律所禁止的（illegal immigration 非法移民，illegal drugs 违禁药品）。新闻与日常极高频。名词 illegality 较正式。
[例] ① It is illegal to sell alcohol to minors. 向未成年人售酒违法。 ② The police broke up an illegal gambling ring. 警方捣毁了一个非法赌博团伙。
[搭] illegal activity 非法活动, illegal immigration 非法移民, illegal drugs 违禁药品, illegal parking 违章停车, make sth. illegal 将某事定为违法
[联] legal 合法的, law 法律, crime 犯罪, arrest 逮捕, punish 惩罚, violate 违反, prohibition 禁令, lawful 合法的
[记] illegally adv.。illegal immigrant 非法移民（新闻常用）。勿与 illegible（字迹难辨）混淆

**lawful**
英/美 /ˈlɔːfl/
adj. 合法的；法定的
[义] Allowed or recognized by law; not illegal. 经法律允许或承认的（lawful owner 合法所有人，lawful arrest 依法逮捕）。比 legal 略正式，常强调「合乎法定程序」。
[例] ① The officer made a lawful arrest after obtaining a warrant. 警官取得搜查令后依法逮捕。 ② She is the lawful heir to the estate. 她是该遗产的法定继承人。
[搭] lawful owner 合法所有人, lawful arrest 依法逮捕, lawful authority 法定权力, lawful purpose 合法目的
[联] legal 法律的, illegal 非法的, law 法律, legitimate 正当的, valid 有效的, rightful 正当的, authority 权力
[记] lawfully adv.。lawful 比 legal 更强调「符合法律条文与程序」。日常口语多用 legal/illegal

**justice**
英/美 /ˈdʒʌstɪs/
n. 正义；公正；司法
[义] Fair behaviour or treatment; the system of courts and judges. 公平对待，或法院与法官构成的司法体系（social justice 社会正义，bring sb. to justice 将某人绳之以法）。不可数为主；可数指「司法官员头衔」（Justice Smith 史密斯法官）。
[例] ① Victims deserve justice, not empty promises. 受害者应得到公正，而非空头承诺。 ② The family waited years for justice to be done. 这家人等了多年才讨回公道。
[搭] social justice 社会正义, bring sb. to justice 将某人绳之以法, justice system 司法体系, miscarriage of justice 冤案, do justice to 公正对待
[联] law 法律, court 法庭, fair 公平的, judge 法官, crime 犯罪, innocent 无辜的, verdict 裁决, injustice 不公正, equality 平等
[记] 不可数为主。Department of Justice 司法部（美）。do justice to 另有义「充分展现」。injustice 反义

**injustice**
英/美 /ɪnˈdʒʌstɪs/
n. 不公正；冤屈
[义] Lack of fairness; an unfair action or situation. 缺乏公平，或不公平的对待与处境（fight against injustice 反抗不公，social injustice 社会不公）。可数/不可数均可。
[例] ① Protesters marched against racial injustice. 抗议者游行反对种族不公。 ② He could not accept the injustice of the verdict. 他无法接受裁决的不公。
[搭] fight against injustice 反抗不公, social injustice 社会不公, gross injustice 严重不公, suffer injustice 遭受冤屈
[联] justice 正义, unfair 不公平的, discrimination 歧视, protest 抗议, wrong 错误, inequality 不平等, outrage 愤慨
[记] 可数/不可数。do sb. an injustice 冤枉某人（另一义）。in- 表否定

**right**
英/美 /raɪt/
n. 权利
adj. 正确的；右边的
[义] n. A moral or legal entitlement（human rights 人权，the right to vote 投票权）。道德或法律上的正当资格。adj. 正确——多义，法律语境看 rights。复数 rights 极常见。
[例] ① Everyone has the right to a fair trial. 人人有权获得公正审判。 ② The court upheld the workers' rights. 法庭维护了工人的权利。
[搭] human rights 人权, civil rights 公民权利, the right to ……的权利, legal rights 合法权利, violate rights 侵犯权利
[联] law 法律, freedom 自由, justice 正义, constitution 宪法, duty 义务, equality 平等, democracy 民主, protection 保护
[记] 权利义多用复数 rights。right vs. privilege：right 为应得资格，privilege 为额外授予。

**human rights**
英/美 /ˌhjuːmən ˈraɪts/
n. 人权
[义] Basic rights and freedoms that belong to every person. 属于每个人的基本权利与自由（human rights violation 侵犯人权，human rights activist 人权活动家）。常复数。国际新闻与政治讨论高频。
[例] ① The report documented human rights abuses in the region. 报告记录了该地区的人权侵害。 ② Human rights groups called for an independent inquiry. 人权组织呼吁独立调查。
[搭] human rights violation 侵犯人权, human rights activist 人权活动家, human rights law 人权法, universal human rights 普遍人权
[联] rights 权利, civil rights 公民权利, freedom 自由, justice 正义, law 法律, dignity 尊严, equality 平等, activist 活动家
[记] 通常复数。Human Rights Watch 等为常见机构名。

**civil rights**
英/美 /ˌsɪvl ˈraɪts/
n. 公民权利
[义] The rights of citizens to political and social freedom and equality. 公民在政治与社会方面的自由与平等权利（civil rights movement 民权运动，civil rights law 民权法）。美国历史中特指反种族隔离斗争。
[例] ① The civil rights movement changed American society in the 1960s. 民权运动在二十世纪六十年代改变了美国社会。 ② Civil rights laws protect people from discrimination at work. 民权法保护人们在职场上免受歧视。
[搭] civil rights movement 民权运动, civil rights law 民权法, civil rights activist 民权活动家, protect civil rights 保护公民权利
[联] human rights 人权, rights 权利, discrimination 歧视, equality 平等, freedom 自由, protest 抗议, law 法律, justice 正义
[记] 通常复数。civil 此处指「公民的」，非「民事的」。civil law 民事法律——另一组搭配

**rule**
英/美 /ruːl/
n. 规则；规定
v. 统治；裁定
[义] n. An official instruction that says how things must be done. 规定应如何行事的具体条文（follow the rules 遵守规则，school rules 校规）。比 law 范围更窄、强制力可更弱。v. 裁定——法庭语境（the judge ruled that … 法官裁定……）。
[例] ① Rules require passengers to wear seat belts. 规定要求乘客系安全带。 ② The judge ruled that the evidence was inadmissible. 法官裁定该证据不予采纳。
[搭] follow the rules 遵守规则, break a rule 违反规定, as a rule 通常, rule of law 法治, rule on 对……作出裁决
[联] law 法律, regulation 规章, order 秩序, policy 政策, court 法庭, judge 法官, govern 治理, standard 标准
[记] 可数：a rule。rule of law 法治（不可数概念）。动词 rule 法庭义：rule that 从句 / rule on a motion

**regulation**
英/美 /ˌreɡjuˈleɪʃn/
n. 规章；法规
[义] An official rule made by a government or authority. 政府或主管机构制定的正式规则（safety regulations 安全规章，government regulations 政府法规）。比 rule 更正式、常指行政细则。
[例] ① New regulations limit how banks use customer data. 新法规限制银行如何使用客户数据。 ② The factory failed to meet environmental regulations. 该厂未能达到环保规定。
[搭] government regulations 政府法规, safety regulations 安全规章, comply with regulations 遵守规章, regulation of 对……的规管
[联] law 法律, rule 规则, policy 政策, standard 标准, comply 遵守, authority 当局, legislate 立法
[记] 可数/不可数。regulate 动词；regulatory 形容词（regulatory body 监管机构）

**legislation**
英/美 /ˌledʒɪsˈleɪʃn/
n. 立法；法律
[义] Laws considered collectively; the process of making laws. 法律的总称，或制定法律的过程（pass legislation 通过立法，new legislation 新法规）。不可数为主；可数指「某项立法」。
[例] ① Parliament debated legislation on data privacy. 议会就数据隐私立法展开辩论。 ② Tougher legislation was introduced after the scandal. 丑闻后出台了更严厉的法规。
[搭] pass legislation 通过立法, new legislation 新法规, draft legislation 起草法案, environmental legislation 环境立法
[联] law 法律, legislate 立法, parliament 议会, congress 国会, statute 法令, bill 法案, government 政府
[记] 不可数为主。legislate 动词；legislative 形容词（legislative body 立法机关）。

**constitution**
英/美 /ˌkɒnstɪˈtjuːʃn/
n. 宪法；章程
[义] The basic set of laws and principles by which a country or organization is governed. 国家或组织治理所依的基本法律与原则（the U.S. Constitution 美国宪法，constitutional rights 宪法权利）。可数。
[例] ① The Constitution guarantees freedom of speech. 宪法保障言论自由。 ② The court ruled that the law was unconstitutional. 法院裁定该法违宪。
[搭] the Constitution 宪法, constitutional rights 宪法权利, constitutional amendment 宪法修正案, violate the constitution 违宪
[联] law 法律, amendment 修正案, rights 权利, government 政府, democracy 民主, supreme court 最高法院, charter 宪章
[记] 可数：a constitution。constitutional 形容词；unconstitutional 违宪的。constitute 动词义不同——「构成」

**statute**
英/美 /ˈstætʃuːt/
n. 法令；成文法
[义] A written law passed by a legislative body. 立法机关通过的书面法律（federal statute 联邦法令，statute of limitations 诉讼时效）。比 law 更正式、法律文本色彩。
[例] ① The statute requires companies to report large donations. 该法令要求公司申报巨额捐赠。 ② Prosecutors cited a statute banning insider trading. 检察官援引禁止内幕交易的成文法。
[搭] federal statute 联邦法令, statute of limitations 诉讼时效, under statute 依法令, violate a statute 违反法令
[联] law 法律, legislation 立法, regulation 规章, code 法典, court 法庭, legal 法律的, ordinance 地方法令
[记] 可数：a statute。statutory 形容词（statutory requirement 法定要求）。statute of limitations 诉讼时效——法律高频短语

**amendment**
英/美 /əˈmendmənt/
n. 修正案；修改
[义] A change or addition to a law or constitution. 对法律或宪法的修改或增补（constitutional amendment 宪法修正案，First Amendment 第一修正案）。可数。
[例] ① The amendment passed with a two-thirds majority. 该修正案以三分之二多数通过。 ② Free speech is protected by the First Amendment. 言论自由受第一修正案保护。
[搭] constitutional amendment 宪法修正案, propose an amendment 提出修正案, pass an amendment 通过修正案, First Amendment 第一修正案
[联] constitution 宪法, law 法律, legislation 立法, rights 权利, bill 法案, revise 修订, reform 改革
[记] 可数：an amendment。amend 动词（修正）。美国宪法前十条修正案称 Bill of Rights 权利法案

**court**
英/美 /kɔːt/
n. 法庭；法院
[义] A place where legal cases are heard and decided. 审理并裁决案件的司法场所（go to court 上法庭，supreme court 最高法院，family court 家事法庭）。可数。亦指「球场」——另一义。
[例] ① The case will be heard in court next month. 该案将于下月在法庭审理。 ② She decided to take the matter to court. 她决定把此事告上法庭。
[搭] go to court 上法庭, supreme court 最高法院, family court 家事法庭, court case 法庭案件, in court 在庭上
[联] judge 法官, lawyer 律师, trial 审判, jury 陪审团, lawsuit 诉讼, verdict 裁决, justice 正义, defendant 被告
[记] 可数：a court。in court 在法庭；on the court 在球场上。courtroom 法庭房间；courthouse 法院大楼

**courtroom**
英/美 /ˈkɔːtruːm/
n. 法庭；审判庭
[义] A room in which trials and legal hearings take place. 进行审判与法律听证的房间（packed courtroom 座无虚席的法庭，empty courtroom 空法庭）。新闻与律政剧高频。
[例] ① The courtroom fell silent when the verdict was read. 宣读裁决时法庭鸦雀无声。 ② Cameras are not allowed in the courtroom. 审判庭内不允许摄像。
[搭] packed courtroom 满座的法庭, courtroom drama 法庭剧, enter the courtroom 进入法庭, courtroom procedure 庭审程序
[联] court 法院, judge 法官, jury 陪审团, trial 审判, witness 证人, defendant 被告, lawyer 律师, verdict 裁决
[记] 可数：a courtroom。court + room 复合词。gallery 指旁听席

**courthouse**
英/美 /ˈkɔːthaʊs/
n. 法院大楼
[义] A building containing law courts and related offices. 设有法庭及相关办公机构的建筑（county courthouse 县法院大楼，historic courthouse 历史法院建筑）。美式常用。
[例] ① Protesters gathered outside the courthouse. 抗议者聚集在法院大楼外。 ② The courthouse was renovated after the earthquake. 地震后法院大楼进行了翻新。
[搭] county courthouse 县法院, federal courthouse 联邦法院大楼, outside the courthouse 在法院外, courthouse steps 法院台阶（新闻常用）
[联] court 法院, courtroom 法庭, judge 法官, trial 审判, jury 陪审团, lawyer 律师, government 政府
[记] 可数：a courthouse。英式更常说 court building 或 law courts

**supreme court**
英/美 /suːˈpriːm kɔːt/
n. 最高法院
[义] The highest court in a country's judicial system. 一国司法体系中的最高审判机关（U.S. Supreme Court 美国最高法院，Supreme Court ruling 最高法院裁决）。可数；常大写。
[例] ① The Supreme Court overturned the lower court's decision. 最高法院推翻了下级法院的判决。 ② Supreme Court justices serve for life in the U.S. 美国最高法院大法官终身任职。
[搭] U.S. Supreme Court 美国最高法院, Supreme Court ruling 最高法院裁决, Supreme Court justice 最高法院大法官, appeal to the Supreme Court 向最高法院上诉
[联] court 法院, judge 法官, appeal 上诉, constitution 宪法, ruling 裁决, justice 正义, federal 联邦的
[记] 可数。各国名称不同：UK 为 Supreme Court（2009 年后）；美国为 the Supreme Court。

**judiciary**
英/美 /dʒuˈdɪʃəri/
n. 司法部门；法官（统称）
[义] The judges of a country considered as a group; the branch of government dealing with courts. 一国法官的整体，或负责法院的政府分支（independent judiciary 独立司法，judiciary system 司法体系）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① An independent judiciary is essential to democracy. 独立司法对民主至关重要。 ② The judiciary must not be influenced by politics. 司法部门不应受政治影响。
[搭] independent judiciary 独立司法, judiciary system 司法体系, federal judiciary 联邦司法系统, respect for the judiciary 尊重司法
[联] judge 法官, court 法院, justice 正义, legal 法律的, judicial 司法的, government 政府, branch 分支
[记] 集合名词。judicial 形容词。

**judicial**
英/美 /dʒuˈdɪʃl/
adj. 司法的；法庭的
[义] Relating to courts, judges, or the administration of justice. 与法院、法官或司法行政相关的（judicial review 司法审查，judicial process 司法程序）。比 legal 更专指「司法体系内」。
[例] ① The reform aims to speed up the judicial process. 改革旨在加快司法程序。 ② Judicial independence protects citizens from abuse of power. 司法独立保护公民免遭权力滥用。
[搭] judicial review 司法审查, judicial process 司法程序, judicial system 司法制度, judicial decision 司法裁决
[联] judge 法官, court 法院, judiciary 司法部门, justice 正义, legal 法律的, ruling 裁决, independent 独立的
[记] judicially adv.。

**case**
英/美 /keɪs/
n. 案件；案子
[义] A matter that is being officially investigated or decided in court. 在法庭上正式调查或裁决的事项（criminal case 刑事案件，civil case 民事案件，court case 法庭案件）。可数。亦指「情况、箱子」——多义。
[例] ① The case against him was dismissed for lack of evidence. 因证据不足，针对他的案件被驳回。 ② Lawyers on both sides prepared for a long case. 双方律师为一场持久诉讼做准备。
[搭] criminal case 刑事案件, civil case 民事案件, court case 法庭案件, win a case 胜诉, drop a case 撤案
[联] court 法庭, trial 审判, lawsuit 诉讼, defendant 被告, lawyer 律师, evidence 证据, verdict 裁决, judge 法官
[记] 可数：a case。make the case for 另有义「论证……」。；本章侧重法律案件义

**lawsuit**
英/美 /ˈlɔːsuːt/
n. 诉讼；官司
[义] A claim or dispute brought to a court of law for judgment. 提交法院裁决的索赔或争议（file a lawsuit 提起诉讼，class-action lawsuit 集体诉讼）。可数。比 case 更强调「主动起诉」行为。
[例] ① She filed a lawsuit against her former employer. 她对前雇主提起诉讼。 ② The lawsuit was settled out of court. 这起官司庭外和解了。
[搭] file a lawsuit 提起诉讼, class-action lawsuit 集体诉讼, settle a lawsuit 和解诉讼, win a lawsuit 胜诉
[联] sue 起诉, court 法庭, plaintiff 原告, defendant 被告, lawyer 律师, case 案件, settlement 和解, damages 损害赔偿
[记] 可数：a lawsuit。suit 本义「诉求」；lawsuit = law + suit。out of court 庭外解决

**sue**
英/美 /suː/
v. 起诉；控告
[义] To take legal action against a person or organization. 对某人或机构提起法律诉讼（sue sb. for damages 起诉某人索赔，sue a company 起诉公司）。及物动词；常接 for + 名词。
[例] ① The tenant sued the landlord for unsafe conditions. 租户因居住不安全起诉房东。 ② Several customers sued the bank over hidden fees. 多名客户因隐性收费控告银行。
[搭] sue sb. for damages 起诉索赔, sue a company 起诉公司, sue for divorce 起诉离婚, right to sue 诉权
[联] lawsuit 诉讼, plaintiff 原告, court 法庭, lawyer 律师, damages 损害赔偿, defendant 被告, claim 索赔
[记] sue / sued / sued / suing。sue for 亦可指「请求」（sue for peace 求和）——另一义

**plaintiff**
英/美 /ˈpleɪntɪf/
n. 原告
