# 程度与强度

**very**
英/美 /ˈveri/
adv. 很，非常
[义] Used to emphasize an adjective, adverb, or noun to show a high degree. 置于形容词、副词或名词前，表示程度很高（very good 很好，very much 非常，the very idea 这念头本身）。日常最通用的强化副词，语气低于 extremely / incredibly，高于 quite / fairly。
[例] ① The test was very difficult, but most students passed. 考试很难，但大多数学生还是通过了。 ② Thank you very much for your help. 非常感谢你的帮助。
[搭] very much 非常, very good 很好, very well 很好, very little 很少, the very best 最好的, at the very least 至少
[联] extremely：程度更高，偏书面或强调, quite：相当，语气较温和, fairly：还算，偏中性, too：过度，含「太多」意味, so：如此，常带结果（so tired that…）, really 真的, highly 高度地
[记] very 不可修饰比较级（× very better，用 much better）。the very + 名词表「正是那个」（the very person I need）

**so**
英/美 /soʊ/
adv. 如此，这么；因此
conj. 所以
[义] To such a great degree; used before an adjective or adverb with that or a result clause. 表示「到这种程度」，常接 that 从句或引出结果（so tired that she fell asleep 累得睡着了；so fast 这么快）。口语极常见，比 very 更带「超出预期、引发后果」的色彩。
[例] ① The room was so noisy that I couldn't concentrate. 房间太吵，我没法集中精神。 ② I've never seen so many people at a concert before. 我从没在演唱会上见过这么多人。
[搭] so much 这么多, so many 这么多, so far 到目前为止, so that 以便, so as to 为了, ever so 非常（口语）
[联] very：单纯强化，较少接 that 表结果, too：含「过分、不该如此」的负面色彩, such：后接名词（such a day）, therefore 因此, thus 因而, extremely 极其
[记] so…that 表「如此……以至于」。so 作连词表「所以」（I was tired, so I left.）。not so much A as B 与其说是 A 不如说是 B

**too**
英/美 /tuː/
adv. 太，过于；也
[义] More than is needed, wanted, or acceptable; also. 超过所需、所愿或可接受的程度（too expensive 太贵，too late 太晚）；亦表「也」（me too 我也是）。含轻微负面或遗憾——比 very 多一层「过了头」。
[例] ① The coffee is too hot to drink right now. 咖啡太烫，现在喝不了。 ② She arrived too late to catch the opening speech. 她到得太晚，没赶上开幕致辞。
[搭] too much 太多, too many 太多, too late 太晚, too expensive 太贵, me too 我也是, all too 实在太
[联] very：单纯「很」，无「过分」意味, so：强调程度，未必含负面, enough：刚好够, excessively 过度地, overly 过度地, also 也, either 也（否定句）
[记] too…to 表「太……而不能」。too 与 very 勿混：very good 很好，too good 好到有问题/难以置信。enough 在形容词后（good enough）

**quite**
英/美 /kwaɪt/
adv. 相当，颇；完全
[义] To a fairly high degree; completely (especially British). 程度「相当高」或「颇」（quite good 相当好，quite difficult 挺难）；英式口语亦可表「完全」（quite sure 完全确定，quite finished 全做完了）。比 very 弱，比 fairly 略强或相当。
[例] ① The hotel was quite comfortable, though not luxurious. 酒店相当舒适，虽谈不上豪华。 ② Are you quite sure about the meeting time? 你完全确定会议时间吗？
[搭] quite good 相当好, quite a lot 相当多, quite sure 完全确定, quite different 截然不同, quite right 完全对, not quite 不完全是
[联] fairly：还算，更中性、更弱, rather：相当，常带「出乎意料」或略负面, very：很，语气更强, pretty：相当（口语）, completely 完全, somewhat 有点
[记] quite a + 可数名词表「相当不错的」（quite a surprise 真是意外）。not quite 极常用，表「差一点、不完全是」。英式 quite 表「完全」时接近 entirely

**fairly**
英/美 /ˈfeəli/
adv. 相当，还算
[义] To a moderate degree; more than a little but less than very. 中等偏上程度——比 a little 强，比 very 弱（fairly easy 还算容易，fairly common 相当常见）。语气中性、客观，少带感情色彩。
[例] ① The instructions are fairly straightforward once you read them carefully. 仔细读过之后，说明还算直白。 ② Prices here are fairly reasonable compared with downtown. 跟市中心比，这里的价格还算合理。
[搭] fairly easy 还算容易, fairly common 相当常见, fairly well 还算好, fairly quickly 相当快, fairly sure 比较有把握
[联] quite：相当，略强或相当, rather：相当，常带意外或负面, pretty：相当（口语，略非正式）, moderately 适度地, somewhat 有点, very 很
[记] fairly 与 fairly 作「公平地」同形（judge fairly 公正裁判），依语境区分。fairly 强化程度时不可修饰比较级（用 somewhat / a bit）

**rather**
英/美 /ˈrɑːðə(r)/
adv. 相当，颇；宁愿
[义] To a fairly great degree, often unexpectedly or with slight negative tone; preferably. 程度相当高，常含「比预想的更……」或略带负面（rather difficult 相当难，rather expensive 偏贵）；亦表「宁愿」（would rather 宁愿）。比 fairly 更有「出乎预料」的色彩。
[例] ① The exam turned out to be rather harder than we expected. 考试结果比我们预想的难不少。 ② I'd rather stay home tonight than go out in the rain. 今晚我宁愿待在家，不想冒雨出去。
[搭] rather than 而不是, would rather 宁愿, rather difficult 相当难, rather like 颇像, rather good 相当好
[联] fairly：还算，更中性, quite：相当，英式可表「完全」, pretty：相当（口语）, instead 反而, prefer 更喜欢, somewhat 有点
[记] would rather do 宁愿做（后接动词原形）。rather than + 名词/动名词表「而非」。rather 表程度时常暗示「有点过头或意外」

**pretty**
英/美 /ˈprɪti/
adv. 相当，挺
adj. 漂亮的
[义] To a fairly large degree (informal, especially spoken). 口语中作程度副词，表「相当、挺」（pretty good 挺好，pretty sure 挺确定）。比 fairly / quite 略非正式；形容词义「漂亮的」见其他章节。
[例] ① The restaurant was pretty crowded when we arrived. 我们到的时候餐厅挺挤的。 ② I'm pretty sure I left my keys on the desk. 我挺确定把钥匙落在桌上了。
[搭] pretty good 挺好, pretty sure 挺确定, pretty much 差不多, pretty well 相当好, pretty soon 很快
[联] fairly：还算，更中性, quite：相当, rather：相当，常带意外, very：很, somewhat 有点, beautiful 漂亮的（形容词义）
[记] 程度副词 pretty 略口语；正式写作多用 fairly / quite。pretty much 表「差不多、基本上」。形容词 pretty 与副词同形，依后接词类区分

**extremely**
英/美 /ɪkˈstriːmli/
adv. 极其，非常
[义] To a very great degree; used to emphasize adjectives and adverbs. 程度极高，比 very 强得多（extremely important 极其重要，extremely cold 极冷）。偏书面或正式强调，口语 also 常用。
[例] ① The new policy is extremely important for small businesses. 新政策对小微企业极其重要。 ② She was extremely careful not to make the same mistake twice. 她极其小心，避免再犯同样的错。
[源] 源自 extreme（极端）+ -ly；拉丁语 *extremus*（最外面的）→ 到极点 → 极其。
[族] extreme 极端的, extremity 末端/极端
[搭] extremely important 极其重要, extremely difficult 极难, extremely useful 极有用, extremely rare 极罕见, extremely likely 极有可能
[联] very：很，语气较弱, incredibly：难以置信地，口语极常见, immensely 巨大地, exceedingly 极度地, exceptionally 异常地, slightly 稍微
[记] extremely 为 extreme 的副词形式。与 very 相比更强调「接近极限」。extremely 后可接 glad / grateful 等，语气正式

**incredibly**
英/美 /ɪnˈkredəbli/
adv. 难以置信地，极其
[义] To a very great degree; in a way that is hard to believe. 程度极高，常带「好到/坏到令人难以相信」（incredibly fast 快得惊人，incredibly lucky 幸运极了）。口语极常见，语气比 extremely 略活、略夸张。
[例] ① The team played incredibly well in the second half. 球队下半场打得好得惊人。 ② Incredibly, no one was hurt in the accident. 难以置信的是，事故中无人受伤。
[源] in-（不）+ credible（可相信的）+ -ly → 难以置信的 → 极其。
[族] incredible 难以置信的, credibility 可信度, credible 可信的
[搭] incredibly fast 快得惊人, incredibly lucky 幸运极了, incredibly difficult 难极了, incredibly useful 极有用, incredibly popular 极受欢迎
[联] extremely 极其, remarkably 显著地, exceptionally 异常地, unbelievably 难以置信地, very 很, incredibly vs. incredibly：句首表「令人惊讶的是」
[记] 句首 incredibly 可表「令人惊讶的是」（Incredibly, he agreed.）。 incredibly 口语极频，正式写作可用 remarkably / exceptionally 替换

**immensely**
英/美 /ɪˈmensli/
adv. 极大地，非常
[义] To a very great extent or degree; enormously. 程度或范围极大（immensely grateful 非常感激，immensely popular 极受欢迎，immensely complicated 极其复杂）。偏书面，比 very 强，与 enormously 近义。
[例] ① We are immensely grateful for your support during the crisis. 危机期间非常感谢你的支持。 ② The novel is immensely popular with young readers. 这部小说在年轻读者中极受欢迎。
[源] 源自 immense（巨大的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *immensus*（不可测量的）→ 极大 → 非常。
[族] immense 巨大的, immensity 巨大
[搭] immensely grateful 非常感激, immensely popular 极受欢迎, immensely complicated 极其复杂, immensely valuable 极有价值, enjoy immensely 非常享受
[联] enormously 巨大地, tremendously 极度地, vastly 广泛地, greatly 大大地, very 很, hugely 极大地
[记] immensely 多修饰抽象形容词（grateful, popular, complicated）。enjoy immensely 为固定搭配。与 immense 形容词成对

**enormously**
英/美 /ɪˈnɔːməsli/
adv. 巨大地，非常
[义] To a very great degree or extent. 程度或规模极大（enormously successful 非常成功，enormously helpful 帮了大忙，enormously expensive 贵得离谱）。强调「规模大、程度深」，口语与书面均常见。
[例] ① The project was enormously successful and exceeded all targets. 项目非常成功，超出所有目标。 ② Your advice was enormously helpful—thank you. 你的建议帮了大忙，谢谢。
[源] 源自 enormous（巨大的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *norma*（规范）+ e-（出）→ 超出常规 → 巨大。
[族] enormous 巨大的, enormity 巨大/暴行
[搭] enormously successful 非常成功, enormously helpful 帮了大忙, enormously expensive 贵得离谱, enormously popular 极受欢迎, enormously complicated 极其复杂
[联] immensely 非常, tremendously 极度地, hugely 极大地, vastly 广泛地, greatly 大大地, slightly 稍微
[记] enormously 与 immense / huge 形容词族近义。enormously 可修饰 increase / improve 等动词的副词用法（increased enormously）

**hugely**
英/美 /ˈhjuːdʒli/
adv. 极大地，非常
[义] To a very great degree (informal to neutral). 程度极大，语气比 enormously 略口语（hugely popular 极受欢迎，hugely important 非常重要，hugely enjoyable 非常享受）。强调影响或规模「巨大」。
[例] ① The film was hugely popular when it was first released. 电影首映时极受欢迎。 ② Technology has hugely changed the way we work. 科技极大地改变了我们的工作方式。
[搭] hugely popular 极受欢迎, hugely important 非常重要, hugely successful 非常成功, hugely enjoyable 非常享受, hugely influential 影响极大
[联] enormously 巨大地, immensely 非常, tremendously 极度地, vastly 广泛地, greatly 大大地, massively 大规模地
[记] hugely 口语与媒体常见。多修饰 popular / successful / important 等。huge 形容词见尺寸章；本章取程度副词义

**vastly**
英/美 /ˈvɑːstli/
adv. 极大地，广泛地
[义] By a very great amount; to a very great extent. 数量或程度上差距极大（vastly different 截然不同，vastly improved 大幅改善，vastly superior 远胜一筹）。强调「差距大、变化幅度大」，偏书面。
[例] ① The two versions of the software are vastly different. 软件的两个版本截然不同。 ② Conditions have vastly improved since the reforms. 改革以来状况大幅改善。
[搭] vastly different 截然不同, vastly improved 大幅改善, vastly superior 远胜一筹, vastly underestimated 严重低估, vastly outnumbered 数量远逊
[联] enormously 巨大地, significantly 显著地, considerably 相当大地, greatly 大大地, slightly 稍微, massively 大规模地
[记] vastly 常出现在比较语境（vastly different / superior / better）。vast 形容词表「广阔的」，副词 vastly 侧重「程度差距大」

**tremendously**
英/美 /trəˈmendəsli/
adv. 极度地，极其
[义] To a very great degree; extremely (also used informally to mean "very well"). 程度极高（tremendously exciting 极其激动人心）；口语亦表「非常好」（You did tremendously! 你干得棒极了！）。形容词 tremendous 亦表尺寸，见尺寸章。
[例] ① The audience responded tremendously to her performance. 观众对她的表演反应极其热烈。 ② Sales have grown tremendously over the past year. 过去一年销售额大幅增长。
[搭] tremendously exciting 极其激动人心, tremendously helpful 帮了大忙, tremendously successful 非常成功, enjoy tremendously 非常享受, grow tremendously 大幅增长
[联] enormously 巨大地, immensely 非常, hugely 极大地, extremely 极其, greatly 大大地, tremendous 巨大的（形容词）
[记] tremendously 与形容词 tremendous 成对。tremendous 在口语可表「太好了」（You look tremendous!）。尺寸义 tremendous 见 05-04

**exceedingly**
英/美 /ɪkˈsiːdɪŋli/
adv. 极度地，极其
[义] To a very great degree; extremely (formal or literary). 程度极高，偏正式或文学语体（exceedingly rare 极罕见，exceedingly polite 极其客气，exceedingly difficult 极难）。比 extremely 略书面、略旧式，但仍见于正式文本。
[例] ① The manuscript is exceedingly rare and kept in a climate-controlled vault. 这份手稿极罕见，保存在恒温库中。 ② She was exceedingly polite throughout the difficult negotiation. 整个艰难谈判中她都极其客气。
[源] 源自 exceed（超越）+ -ing + -ly → 超越寻常程度 → 极其。
[族] exceed 超过, excess 过量, excessive 过度的
[搭] exceedingly rare 极罕见, exceedingly polite 极其客气, exceedingly difficult 极难, exceedingly useful 极有用, exceedingly important 极其重要
[联] extremely 极其, exceptionally 异常地, remarkably 显著地, very 很, excessively 过度地, exceedingly：偏正式
[记] exceedingly 现代口语多用 extremely / incredibly 替代，但学术与法律文本仍常见。exceedingly 不可与比较级连用表「更」

**utterly**
英/美 /ˈʌtəli/
adv. 完全地，彻底地
[义] Completely; to an absolute degree, often with negative or extreme adjectives. 绝对、彻底，常与负面或极端形容词连用（utterly wrong 完全错了，utterly exhausted 精疲力竭，utterly ridiculous 荒谬至极）。比 completely 语气更强、更带感情。
[例] ① I was utterly exhausted after the twelve-hour flight. 十二小时飞行后我精疲力竭。 ② The claim turned out to be utterly false. 这一说法结果完全是假的。
[源] 古英语 *ūtor*（外部的）→ 完全在外 → 彻底。形容词 utter 表「完全的」（utter chaos 彻底的混乱）。
[族] utter 完全的, utterance 话语
[搭] utterly wrong 完全错了, utterly exhausted 精疲力竭, utterly ridiculous 荒谬至极, utterly hopeless 完全没希望, utterly different 截然不同
[联] completely 完全地, entirely 全部地, totally 彻底地, absolutely 绝对地, thoroughly 彻底地, slightly 稍微
[记] utterly 多接负面或极端形容词（exhausted, wrong, ridiculous, hopeless）。utterly 比 completely 更强调「毫无保留、到底」

**absolutely**
英/美 /ˈæbsəluːtli/
adv. 绝对地，完全地
[义] Completely; used to emphasize adjectives and to give strong agreement. 完全、绝对（absolutely sure 绝对确定，absolutely necessary 绝对必要）；口语单独使用表强烈同意（Absolutely! 当然！）。语气肯定、无保留。
[例] ① You are absolutely right about the deadline. 关于截止日期，你完全正确。 ② "Do you agree?" "Absolutely." 「你同意吗？」「当然。」
[源] 源自 absolute（绝对的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *absolvere*（松开）→ 不受限制 → 绝对。
[族] absolute 绝对的, absolutely 绝对地
[搭] absolutely sure 绝对确定, absolutely necessary 绝对必要, absolutely right 完全正确, absolutely not 绝对不, absolutely amazing 绝对棒
[联] completely 完全地, entirely 全部地, totally 彻底地, utterly 彻底地, definitely 肯定地, relatively 相对地
[记] absolutely 口语应答极频（Absolutely! = Of course!）。absolutely 可修饰 necessary / essential / convinced 等。× absolutely perfect 虽口语常见，部分风格指南认为 redundant

**completely**
英/美 /kəmˈpliːtli/
adv. 完全地，彻底地
[义] In every way or as much as possible; totally. 各方面都；尽可能彻底地（completely different 完全不同，completely finished 全部完成，completely agree 完全同意）。中性、通用，口语书面均常见。
[例] ① The two proposals are completely different in approach. 两份方案在思路上完全不同。 ② I completely agree with your analysis. 我完全同意你的分析。
[源] 源自 complete（完整的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *complere*（填满）→ 填满 → 完全。
[族] complete 完整的, completion 完成, incomplete 不完整的
[搭] completely different 完全不同, completely agree 完全同意, completely finished 全部完成, completely wrong 完全错了, completely new 全新的
[联] entirely 全部地, totally 彻底地, utterly 彻底地, absolutely 绝对地, partially 部分地, fully 完全地
[记] completely 为 complete 的副词形式。completely vs. totally：totally 略口语。not completely 表「不完全是」

**entirely**
英/美 /ɪnˈtaɪəli/
adv. 完全地，全部地
[义] In every part; completely; solely. 每个部分都；完全；仅仅（entirely new 全新的，entirely responsible 全责，entirely up to you 完全由你决定）。强调「整体、无一遗漏」，略偏书面。
[例] ① The building was entirely destroyed in the fire. 大楼在火灾中全部焚毁。 ② Whether we go or not is entirely up to you. 去不去完全由你决定。
[源] 源自 entire（整个的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *integer*（完整的）→ 完整 → 全部。
[族] entire 整个的, entirety 全部
[搭] entirely new 全新的, entirely responsible 全责, entirely up to sb. 完全由某人决定, entirely different 完全不同, not entirely 不完全是
[联] completely 完全地, totally 彻底地, wholly 全部地, fully 完全地, partially 部分地, partly 部分地
[记] not entirely 极常用，表「不完全是、不太同意」。entirely 与 whole / entire 形容词族相关。entirely 可表「 solely 仅仅」（entirely because of you）

**totally**
英/美 /ˈtəʊtəli/
adv. 完全地，彻底地
[义] Completely; absolutely (informal). 完全、彻底，口语极常见（totally agree 完全同意，totally different 完全不同，totally worth it 完全值得）。比 completely 略口语、略随意。
[例] ① I totally forgot about the meeting this morning. 我完全忘了今早的会议。 ② The experience was totally worth the long trip. 这次经历完全值得长途奔波。
[源] 源自 total（总的）+ -ly；拉丁语 *totus*（全部）→ 完全。
[族] total 总的, totally 完全地
[搭] totally agree 完全同意, totally different 完全不同, totally worth it 完全值得, totally wrong 完全错了, totally free 完全免费
[联] completely 完全地, entirely 全部地, absolutely 绝对地, utterly 彻底地, partially 部分地, fully 完全地
[记] totally 口语与年轻人用语极频。totally 可单独应答表强烈同意（Totally!）。正式写作可换 completely / entirely

**fully**
英/美 /ˈfʊli/
adv. 完全地，充分地

**highly**
英/美 /ˈhaɪli/

[例] ① The two versions are essentially the same apart from the cover design. 除封面设计外，两个版本基本相同。 ② What you heard is essentially true, though some details were exaggerated. 你听到的大体属实，但有些细节被夸大了。
[搭] essentially the same 基本相同, essentially true 基本属实, essentially unchanged 基本未变, essentially correct 基本正确, essentially about 与……在本质上相关
[联] fundamentally 根本上, basically 基本上, superficially 表面上, essentially vs. fundamentally：essentially 偏「实质相同/核心一致」，fundamentally 偏「原理/本质层面」
[记] essentially 与 essential 形容词成对。× essentially good 不自然。essential 另有名词义「要素」

**basically**
英/美 /ˈbeɪsɪkli/
adv. 基本上；大体上
[义] In the most important respects; fundamentally; used to simplify or summarize. 在主要方面；根本上；用于简化或概括（basically correct 基本正确，basically the same 基本相同，basically means 意思是）。比 essentially 更口语；句首 basically 常引出让步或重述。
[例] ① The two plans are basically the same, with a few minor tweaks. 两份方案基本相同，只有几处小改动。 ② Basically, we need more time to finish the analysis. 说白了，我们需要更多时间完成分析。
[搭] basically correct 基本正确, basically the same 基本相同, basically means 意思是, basically unchanged 基本未变, basically agree 大体同意
[联] essentially 本质上, fundamentally 根本上, simply 简而言之, basically vs. essentially：basically 更口语，essentially 更书面
[记] 句首 Basically, … 口语极常见，正式写作宜节制。basic 形容词，basically 副词

**superficially**
英/美 /ˌsuːpəˈfɪʃəli/
adv. 表面上；肤浅地
[义] On the surface; in a shallow way without deep understanding. 在表面；缺乏深度地（superficially similar 表面相似，superficially attractive 表面诱人，superficially wounded 皮外伤）。与 profoundly / fundamentally 相对；形容词 superficial 在属性章有另条。
[例] ① The two products look superficially similar but work very differently. 两款产品看起来表面相似，运作方式却大不相同。 ② He was only superficially wounded in the accident. 他在事故中只是皮外伤。
[搭] superficially similar 表面相似, superficially attractive 表面诱人, superficially wounded 皮外伤, superficially resemble 表面相像, superficially plausible 表面似是而非
[联] profoundly 深刻地, fundamentally 根本上, faintly 轻微地, superficially vs. fundamentally：superficially 偏「表面/不深究」
[记] superficially similar 为对比常用搭配。superficial 形容词另有「肤浅的」义；superficially 副词本章取「表面上」

**negligibly**
英/美 /ˈneɡlɪdʒəbli/
adv. 可忽略地；微不足道地
[义] To an extent so small as to be not worth considering. 小到不必计入（negligibly small 小得可忽略，negligibly different 差异可忽略，contribute negligibly 贡献微不足道）。形容词 negligible 在尺寸章有另条；本章取程度副词义，强调「影响/差异可忽略」。
[例] ① The extra weight of the label affects the total negligibly. 标签增加的重量对总量影响可忽略不计。 ② The two estimates differ negligibly for practical purposes. 就实际用途而言，两个估计值差异可忽略。
[搭] negligibly small 小得可忽略, negligibly different 差异可忽略, contribute negligibly 贡献微不足道, affect negligibly 影响可忽略, change negligibly 变化可忽略
[联] slightly 稍微, marginally 略微地, significantly 显著地, substantially 在很大程度上, negligibly vs. slightly：negligibly 强调「不必计入」，slightly 仅表「幅度小」
[记] negligibly 多见于数据、工程与报告。negligible 形容词见 05-04 尺寸语境；negligibly 副词本章主归属
