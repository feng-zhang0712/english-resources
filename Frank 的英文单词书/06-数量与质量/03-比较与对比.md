# 比较与对比

**compare**
英/美 /kəmˈpeɑː(r)/
v. 比较；把……比作
[义] To examine two or more things in order to show how they are similar or different; to say that something is like something else in a particular way. 核心义：并列审视以找出异同；亦可喻指「把 A 比作 B」（compare her to a rose 把她比作玫瑰）。compare A with B 侧重对照差异，compare A to B 侧重比喻；口语中常混用。
[形] compares / compared / compared / comparing
[例] ① If you compare the two phones side by side, the differences are obvious. 若把两部手机并排放在一起比较，差异一目了然。 ② The poet compares autumn to a mellow friend who visits briefly. 诗人把秋天比作一位短暂来访的温厚友人。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *comparare*（配对、并列），*com-*（共同）+ *par*（相等）→ 放在一起衡量。
[族] comparison 比较, comparable 可比较的, comparative 比较的
[搭] compare A with/to B 把 A 与 B 比较/比作, compare notes 交换意见, compared with/to 与……相比, beyond compare 无与伦比, compare favourably 相形之下更好
[联] contrast：强调差异对照, contrast with 与……形成对比, match 匹配, differ 不同, similar 相似的, equivalent 等价的, distinguish 区分, liken 把……比作, side by side 并排, benchmark 基准对照
[记] 无比较级。compare notes 不是「记笔记」，而是交换各自掌握的信息。compared with 较 compared to 更偏对照差异。

**comparison**
英/美 /kəmˈpærɪsn/
n. 比较；对照；比拟
[义] The act of comparing; a statement about how similar or different two things are. 指比较这一行为，或比较后得出的结论（make a comparison 作比较，by comparison 相比之下，in comparison with 与……相比）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① The report draws a comparison between urban and rural healthcare access. 报告对比了城乡医疗可及性的差异。 ② Her latest novel is good, but it suffers by comparison with her earlier work. 她最新这部小说不错，但与早期作品相比仍显逊色。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *comparatio*，与 compare 同族。
[族] compare v., comparative adj., incomparable 无与伦比的
[搭] make/draw a comparison 作比较, by/in comparison 相比之下, in comparison with 与……相比, comparison shopping 货比三家, beyond comparison 无与伦比
[联] contrast：强调差异对照, analogy 类比, benchmark 基准, evaluate 评估, measure 衡量, relative 相对的, side by side 并排, stack up 比得上, stack up against 与……较量, differ 不同
[记] 可数/不可数均可。by comparison 常接前文整体语境，指「相较之下」。comparison 后接 between A and B 或 with。

**contrast**
英/美 /ˈkɒntrɑːst/（名词） /kənˈtrɑːst/（动词）
n. 对比；对照；差异
v. 使形成对比；对照
[义] A noticeable difference between two things when compared; to compare in order to show differences. 名词指并列后显出的鲜明差异（a sharp contrast 鲜明对比）；动词指有意对照以突出不同（contrast A with B 把 A 与 B 对照）。与 compare 不同，contrast 默认焦点在「不同」。
[例] ① The film uses light and shadow in stark contrast to suggest inner conflict. 影片以明暗的强烈对照暗示内心冲突。 ② The guide contrasted life in the capital with conditions in remote villages. 导游把首都生活与偏远村落状况作了对照。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *contra*（相对）+ *stare*（站立）→ 相对而立 → 对照。
[族] contrasting adj., contrastingly adv.
[搭] in contrast to/with 与……形成对比, by contrast 相比之下, sharp/stark contrast 鲜明/强烈对比, contrast A with B 把 A 与 B 对照, stand in contrast 形成对照
[联] compare：可兼指异同, differ 不同, opposite 相反的, distinction 区别, distinguish 区分, juxtapose 并置对照, whereas 然而, on the other hand 另一方面, similarity 相似, parallel 平行对应
[记] 名词重音在第一音节 /ˈkɒntrɑːst/，动词在第二音节 /kənˈtrɑːst/。in contrast to 与 by contrast 常连用：前文述 A，后文 by contrast 引出 B。

**similar**
英/美 /ˈsɪmələ(r)/
adj. 相似的，类似的
[义] Almost the same as something else but not exactly the same. 大体相同、细节可异（similar taste 口味相近，similar problems 类似问题）。比较级 more similar，最高级 most similar；亦可用 similar to each other。
[例] ① The two languages are similar in grammar but very different in pronunciation. 两种语言语法相近，发音却大不相同。 ② We noticed similar patterns in sales data from three regions. 我们注意到三个地区的销售数据呈现类似模式。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *similis*（相像的），与 simulate、simile 同族。
[族] similarity 相似, similarly adv., simile 明喻
[搭] similar to 与……相似, similar in 在……方面相似, strikingly similar 惊人地相似, broadly similar 大体相似, look/sound similar 看起来/听起来像
[联] alike 相似的, alike 多作表语, like 像, resemble 像, equivalent 等价的, identical 完全相同的, different 不同的, dissimilar 不相似的, counterpart 对应物, parallel 平行的, analogous 类似的
[记] 只作形容词；不说 × a similar。similar to + 名词/动名词；similar in + 方面。similarly 作连接副词引同类情况。

**similarity**
英/美 /ˌsɪməˈlærəti/
n. 相似；类似之处
[义] The state of being similar; a feature that things have in common. 相似的状态，或共同特征（bear a similarity to 与……有相似之处，similarities and differences 异同）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① There is a striking similarity between the two paintings—same composition, same palette. 两幅画惊人地相似——构图与色调都一样。 ② The report lists similarities and differences between the two systems. 报告列了两套系统的异同。
[族] similar adj., similarly adv.
[搭] similarity to/between 与……/两者之间的相似, bear a similarity 有相似之处, striking similarity 惊人的相似, share similarities 有共同之处, similarities and differences 异同
[联] resemblance 相像, likeness 相似, parallel 平行对应, overlap 重叠, difference 差异, distinction 区别, analogy 类比, pattern 模式, common ground 共同点, match 匹配
[记] 可数/不可数。复数 similarities 指多处相似点。similarity to 接单一对象，similarity between A and B 接两者。

**differ**
英/美 /ˈdɪfə(r)/
v. 不同；有分歧
[义] To be unlike or dissimilar; to have a different opinion. 事物之间不一样（differ from 与……不同，differ in 在……方面不同）；人之间意见不合（differ with sb. on sth. 在某事上与某人有分歧）。比较级不适用；用 differ greatly/significantly。
[形] differs / differed / differed / differing
[例] ① The two versions differ in several key details. 两个版本在若干关键细节上不同。 ② Experts differ on whether the policy will reduce inflation. 专家对该政策能否降低通胀意见不一。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *differre*（分开、推迟），*dif-*（分开）+ *ferre*（携带）→ 各走各路 → 不同。
[族] difference 差异, different 不同的, differently adv., differential 差别的
[搭] differ from 与……不同, differ in 在……方面不同, differ on/over 在……上意见不一, differ greatly 大不相同, agree to differ 求同存异
[联] contrast 对照, distinguish 区分, disagree 不同意, vary 变化, diverge 分歧, deviate 偏离, unlike 不像, same 相同的, identical 完全相同的, consensus 共识
[记] 不及物动词，无被动。人作主语表「意见不同」：We differ on this point. 物作主语表「不一样」：Prices differ from shop to shop.

**difference**
英/美 /ˈdɪfrəns/
n. 差异；不同；差额
[义] A way in which people or things are not the same; the amount by which one thing is greater or less than another. 不一致之处；亦指数值差额（tell the difference 分辨差异，make a difference 有影响/起作用，difference of opinion 意见分歧）。可数/不可数。
[例] ① Can you tell the difference between the original and the copy? 你能分辨出原件和复制品吗？ ② A good teacher can make a real difference in a child's life. 好老师能真正改变孩子的一生。
[族] differ v., different adj., differential adj.
[搭] tell the difference 分辨差异, make a difference 有影响, difference between A and B A 与 B 的差异, difference of opinion 意见分歧, significant difference 显著差异
[联] distinction 区别, distinction 更偏「可辨别的特征」, contrast 对照, gap 差距, discrepancy 出入, disparity 悬殊, similarity 相似, change 变化, divide 分歧, margin 差额
[记] 可数/不可数。make a difference 可指「有影响」或「带来改变」，依语境。different from（英式）/ different than（美式口语）/ different to（英式口语）。

**different**
英/美 /ˈdɪfrənt/
adj. 不同的；各式各样的
[义] Not the same as another or each other; unusual or separate. 与另一事物不一致；亦可指「各式各样的」（people from different backgrounds 不同背景的人）。比较级 more different。
[例] ① The two proposals are completely different in approach and cost. 两份方案在思路与成本上完全不同。 ② She tried a different recipe and the soup tasted much better. 她换了个做法，汤好喝多了。
[搭] different from/than 与……不同, completely/totally different 完全不同, different kinds of 不同种类的, in different ways 以不同方式, look different 看起来不一样
[联] distinct 截然不同的, separate 分开的, unlike 不像, similar 相似的, same 相同的, identical 完全相同的, diverse 多样的, unique 独特的, ordinary 普通的, alike 相似的
[记] 比较级 more different（不说 × differenter）。different 最通用；distinct 强调「清晰可辨、不容混淆」。difference / different / differently 同族。

**same**
英/美 /seɪm/
adj. 相同的；同一的
pron. 同样的事物
[义] Exactly alike; not different; the identical thing or person already mentioned. 完全一样（the same size 同样大小，at the same time 同时，the same as 与……相同）。比较级不可用；用 identical 或 exactly the same 加强。
[例] ① We ordered the same dish by coincidence and laughed when it arrived. 我们碰巧点了同一道菜，上桌时都笑了。 ② Her answer was the same as mine, word for word. 她的回答与我一字不差。
[搭] the same as 与……相同, at the same time 同时, all the same 仍然/一样, much the same 大致相同, one and the same 完全是同一回事
[联] identical 完全相同的, alike 相似的, equal 相等的, equivalent 等价的, different 不同的, similar 相似的, duplicate 副本, repeat 重复, unchanged 未变的, counterpart 对应物
[记] 常与 the 连用：the same + 名词。all the same 表「尽管如此仍然」（He refused, but I asked all the same.）。same 与 identical 近义，identical 语气更强、强调「毫无二致」。

**alike**
英/美 /əˈlaɪk/
adj. 相似的，相像的（仅作表语）
adv. 同样地；相似地
[义] Similar to each other; in the same way. 彼此相像，只作表语（The twins look alike.）；副词指「以同样方式」（treat everyone alike 一视同仁）。不可说 × an alike person。
[例] ① The two sisters talk alike and even laugh alike—it is uncanny. 两姐妹说话像、笑也像，简直神似。 ② The policy affects rich and poor alike. 这项政策对贫富一视同仁。
[搭] look alike 看起来相像, sound alike 听起来像, treat alike 同等对待, great minds think alike 英雄所见略同（习语）, much alike 非常相像
[联] similar 相似的, similar 可作定语, like 像, resemble 像, identical 完全相同的, same 相同的, different 不同的, twin 双胞胎, duplicate 副本, equally 同样地
[记] 形容词仅作表语：The brothers are alike. 定语用 similar：similar brothers（较少说）。副词 alike 放句末常见：They were treated alike.

**unlike**
英/美 /ʌnˈlaɪk/
prep. 不像；与……不同
adj. 不同的，不相似的
[义] Different from; not typical of. 与……不一样（Unlike his brother, she loves math.）；形容词较少用，表「不像的、各异的」（children of unlike temperaments 性情各异的孩子）。
[例] ① Unlike last year, we had plenty of rain this spring. 与去年不同，今年春天雨水充沛。 ② The two apps look similar but, unlike the first, this one stores data locally. 两款应用界面相似，但与第一款不同，这款在本地存数据。
[搭] unlike sb./sth. 与……不同, quite unlike 完全不同, not unlike 并非不像/有点像, unlike anything 前所未见
[联] different 不同的, dissimilar 不相似的, unlike vs like：like 表「像」, opposite 相反的, contrary 相反的, whereas 然而, compared to 与……相比, similar 相似的, exception 例外, unusual 不寻常的
[记] 介词 unlike 后接名词或动名词：Unlike me, she enjoys cooking. not unlike 为委婉「有点像」（The scene was not unlike a painting.）。勿与 unlikely（不太可能）混淆。

**identical**
英/美 /aɪˈdentɪkl/
adj. 完全相同的
[义] Exactly the same in every detail; the same person or thing. 细节上一模一样（identical twins 同卵双胞胎，identical copies 完全相同的副本）。比较级 more identical 极少用；强调时用 exactly identical。
[例] ① The two keys are identical—you cannot tell them apart. 两把钥匙一模一样，分不出来。 ② Her outfit was almost identical to the one worn by the actress on the red carpet. 她的穿搭与那位女演员红毯上的几乎完全相同。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *identicus*，*idem*（同一）+ -ical → 同一的。
[族] identity 身份/同一性, identify 识别, identification 识别
[搭] identical to/with 与……完全相同, identical twins 同卵双胞胎, nearly/almost identical 几乎一样, identical copy 一模一样的副本
[联] same 相同的, same 语气较弱, alike 相像的, equal 相等的, equivalent 等价的, duplicate 副本, match 匹配, different 不同的, distinct 截然不同的, twin 双胞胎
[记] identical to/with 均可。identical twins 指同卵双胞胎；fraternal twins 为异卵。identity 指「身份」或抽象「同一性」，不同义。

**equal**
英/美 /ˈiːkwəl/
adj. 相等的；平等的
n. 同等的人/物
v. 等于；比得上
[义] The same in size, amount, value, or status; to be as good as. 数量、价值或地位相同（equal pay 同工同酬，be equal to the task 能胜任）；动词表「等于」或「比得上」（Two plus two equals four. / No one can equal her dedication.）。比较级 more equal 用于抽象平等语境。
[例] ① All citizens should have equal access to healthcare. 所有公民都应享有平等的医疗可及性。 ② The challenge was tough, but she proved equal to it. 挑战很艰巨，但她证明了自己能胜任。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *aequalis*（平等的），*aequus*（平坦、公平）。
[族] equality 平等, equally adv., equation 等式, equate 等同
[搭] equal to 等于/能胜任, equal pay 同工同酬, on equal terms 平等条件下, equal importance 同等重要, treat as equals 平等对待
[联] equivalent 等价的, same 相同的, identical 完全相同的, fair 公平的, unequal 不平等的, match 匹配, compare 比较, balance 平衡, parity 均等, counterpart 对应物
[记] 动词 equal 规则变化；第三人称 equals。be equal to 既可表「等于」亦可表「能应付」。equal 强调「均等、公平」；equivalent 强调「功能/价值对等」。

**equivalent**
英/美 /ɪˈkwɪvələnt/
adj. 等价的，相等的
n. 等价物；对应物
[义] Equal in value, amount, function, or meaning; something with the same function or value. 价值、功能或含义对等（the equivalent of $100 相当于一百美元，English equivalent 英文对应说法）。比较级 more equivalent。
[例] ① One euro is roughly equivalent to seven yuan at today's rate. 按今日汇率，一欧元约等于七元人民币。 ② Is there an English equivalent for this Japanese concept? 这个日语概念有英文对应说法吗？
[源] 源自拉丁语 *aequivalere*（价值相等），*aequi-* + *valere*（有价值）→ 价值相同。
[族] equivalence 等价, equivalently adv.
[搭] equivalent to 相当于/等同于, rough equivalent 大致等同, moral equivalent 道义上的对应, English equivalent 英文对应说法, functional equivalent 功能等价物
[联] equal 相等的, equal 偏数量/地位, same 相同的, identical 完全相同的, counterpart 对应物, substitute 替代品, comparable 可比的, analogous 类似的, parallel 平行的, convert 换算
[记] 名词可数：an equivalent / several equivalents。equivalent to 后接名词或从句。equivalent 强调「对等、可互换」；identical 强调「完全一样」。

**resemble**
英/美 /rɪˈzembl/
v. 像；与……相似
[义] To look or be like someone or something else. 外貌或性质上相像（resemble one's father 长得像父亲，closely resemble 非常相似）。只作及物动词，不用 resemble to；比较级不适用，用 closely/greatly resemble。
[形] resembles / resembled / resembled / resembling
[例] ① The puppy resembles its mother in colouring but has longer ears. 小狗毛色像妈妈，但耳朵更长。 ② The new building resembles a glass ship sailing beside the river. 新楼像一艘玻璃船泊在河边。
[源] 源自法语 *ressembler*，*re-*（再）+ *sembler*（像）→ 再次显得像 → 相像。
[族] resemblance 相像, resembling adj.
[搭] closely resemble 非常像, resemble sb./sth. 像某人/某物, bear a resemblance 有相似之处, resemble in appearance 外貌相似
[联] look like 看起来像, similar 相似的, alike 相像的, mimic 模仿, mirror 映照, echo 呼应, duplicate 复制, differ 不同, unlike 不像, counterpart 对应物
[记] 及物动词，直接接宾语：× resemble to his father → resemble his father。resemblance 为名词。resemble 较正式；口语 look like 更常见。

**resemblance**
英/美 /rɪˈzembləns/
n. 相似；相像
[义] The fact of being or looking similar to someone or something. 外貌或特征上的相像（bear a strong resemblance to 与……极为相像，family resemblance 一家人之间的相似）。可数/不可数；常 a resemblance。
[例] ① There is an uncanny resemblance between the actor and the historical figure he plays. 这位演员与他所饰历史人物惊人地相像。 ② The sisters share a family resemblance, especially around the eyes. 姐妹俩有家族式的相似，尤其眼睛。
[族] resemble v.
[搭] bear a resemblance to 与……相像, strong/striking resemblance 极为相像, family resemblance 家族相似, resemblance to 与……的相似, superficial resemblance 表面相似
[联] similarity 相似, likeness 相像, look-alike 酷似者, duplicate 副本, imitation 仿制品, contrast 对照, difference 差异, identical 完全相同的, echo 呼应, parallel 平行对应
[记] 可数/不可数。bear/have a resemblance to 为固定搭配。superficial resemblance 指「表面像、实质不同」。

**match**
英/美 /mætʃ/
n. 比赛；火柴；匹配
v. 与……相配；比得上
[义] To be as good as or equal to; to correspond or fit together. 动词核心义：与……相称、配得上（match the description 符合描述，match sb. in skill 在技能上与某人相当）；亦指使协调（match the curtains to the sofa 让窗帘与沙发配色）。名词义「比赛、火柴」依语境区分。
[形] matches / matched / matched / matching
[例] ① No other candidate matched her experience in international trade. 没有其他候选人能拥有与她相当的国际贸易经验。 ② The shade of blue matches the brand logo perfectly. 这种蓝色与品牌标识完美相配。
[搭] match up to 比得上, match with 与……相配, perfect match 绝配, match sb. in 在……方面与某人相当, mix and match 自由搭配
[联] equal 等于, equivalent 等价的, compare 比较, pair 配对, fit 合适, suit 适合, rival 匹敌, surpass 超越, mismatch 不匹配, counterpart 对应物
[记] match 作动词表「相配/比得上」；a perfect match 指「绝配」。match up with/to 口语常见。与 game 不同，match 可指一对一较量或配色协调。

**distinguish**
英/美 /dɪˈstɪŋɡwɪʃ/
v. 区分；辨别；使杰出
[义] To recognize or show the difference between things; to make someone or something seem different or special. 辨清差异（distinguish A from B 把 A 与 B 区分开）；亦指使脱颖而出（distinguish oneself 表现突出）。比较级不适用。
[形] distinguishes / distinguished / distinguished / distinguishing
[例] ① It can be hard to distinguish genuine leather from high-quality imitation. 真皮革与优质仿品有时很难区分。 ② She distinguished herself as a calm leader during the crisis. 危机中她以沉着的领导者形象脱颖而出。
[源] 源自拉丁语 * distinguere*（分开标记），*dis-* + *stinguere*（刺、点）→ 点出不同 → 区分。
[族] distinction 区别, distinctive 独特的, distinguished 杰出的, distinguishable 可区分的

adj./adv. 并驾齐驱；不相上下
[义] Level with each other in a competition or comparison; equally close. 对照进程中差距极小（run neck and neck 并驾齐驱，neck and neck with 与……不相上下）。固定习语。
[例] ① The two firms have been neck and neck in market share for years. 两家公司市场份额多年来不相上下。 ② Polls show the candidates running neck and neck going into election day. 民调显示选前两人支持率并驾齐驱。
[搭] neck and neck 并驾齐驱, run neck and neck 不相上下, neck and neck with 与……并驾齐驱, remain neck and neck 仍然难分高下
[联] dead heat 难分胜负, tie 平局, equal 相等的, close 接近的, margin 差额, rival 匹敌, compare 比较, lead 领先, lag 落后, compete 竞争
[记] 只作表语或后置定语：a race that is neck and neck。源自赛马「颈并颈」。与 dead heat 近义；neck and neck 可指「过程胶着」。

**in the same league**
英/美 —（短语）

[搭] not in the same league 不在同一档次, in the same league as 与……相提并论, same league 同一档次
[联] match 比得上, compare 比较, measure up 比得上, rival 匹敌, outclass 远胜, inferior 较差的, superior 更好的, benchmark 基准, tier 档次, level 等级
[记] 多用于否定 not in the same league as。肯定用法 in the same league as 表「够格相提并论」。league 此处指「等级、层次」非仅「联赛」。

**conform**

[义] To behave according to accepted standards; to match or be similar when compared with a norm. 与规范对照后一致（conform to/with standards 符合标准，conform to expectations 符合预期）。比较级不适用。

[搭] unfavourable comparison 不利的比较, unfavourable to 对……不利, unfavourable impression 负面印象, unfavourable terms 不利条件
[联] favourable 有利的, negative 负面的, disadvantageous 不利的, compare 比较, criticise 批评, oppose 反对, bias 偏见, worse 更差的, hostile 敌对的, reject 拒绝
[记] 反义 favourable。unfavourable to 表「对……不利」。unfavourable weather/terms 为常见搭配。

**catch up**
英/美 /kætʃ ʌp/
v. 赶上；补上
[义] To reach the same level as someone or something after being behind in comparison. 与 lead/lag/behind 相对：从落后对照位置追平（catch up with 赶上，catch up on 补上）。短语动词。
[形] catches up / caught up / caught up / catching up
[例] ① The challenger caught up with the leader in the final lap. 挑战者在最后一圈赶上了领先者。 ② Emerging markets are catching up with developed economies in digital adoption. 新兴市场在数字化采用上正追赶发达经济体。
[搭] catch up with 赶上, catch up on 补上, catch up to 赶上（美式）, play catch-up 处于追赶状态
[联] lag 落后, behind 落后, lead 领先, ahead 领先, overtake 超越, compare 比较, close 缩小差距, fall behind 落后, progress 进展, level 持平
[记] catch up with + 人/物；catch up on + 工作/睡眠等「补上」。play catch-up 名词化「被动追赶局面」。

**overtake**
英/美 /ˌəʊvəˈteɪk/
v. 超过；赶上
[义] To go past or become greater than someone or something in comparison. 对照排名中从后超前（overtake a rival 超过对手，overtaken by events 为时势所超—另一义）。比较级不适用。
[形] overtakes / overtook / overtaken / overtaking
[例] ① Electric sales overtook diesel models for the first time last quarter. 上季度电动车销量首次超过柴油车型。 ② She overtook her colleague and became the top performer in the region. 她超过了同事，成为本区业绩第一。
[搭] overtake a rival 超过对手, overtaken by 被……超过, overtake in sales 销量超越, fast overtaking 快速赶超
[联] surpass 超越, exceed 超过, outpace 速度上超过, lead 领先, lag 落后, catch up 赶上, compare 比较, pass 超过, beat 打败, rank 排名
[记] 不规则 overtook / overtaken。及物动词。overtake on the road 超车；比喻 overtaken by events 指「计划赶不上变化」。

**trail**
英/美 /treɪl/
v. 落后；拖在后面
n. 踪迹；小径
[义] To be losing or behind others in a comparison or competition. 与 lead/ahead 相对（trail by ten points 落后十分，trail the leader 落后于领先者）。可数/动词。
[形] trails / trailed / trailed / trailing
[例] ① The incumbent still trails her opponent in most polls. 多数民调中现任仍落后于对手。 ② Our region trails the national average on literacy scores. 本区读写分数低于全国平均。
[搭] trail by 落后……, trail the leader 落后于领先者, trail behind 落在后面, trailing edge 落后的一端（技术）
[联] lag 落后, behind 落后, lead 领先, ahead 领先, follow 跟随, compare 比较, score 得分, poll 民调, rank 排名, catch up 赶上
[记] 不及物 trail by/behind。trailing 形容词：trailing candidate 落后候选人。名词 trail 指「路径/踪迹」不同义。

**dominate**
英/美 /ˈdɒmɪneɪt/
v. 主导；占绝对优势
[义] To be much stronger or more important than others when compared. 对照中明显占上风（dominate the market 主导市场，dominate the conversation 主导谈话）。比较级不适用。
[形] dominates / dominated / dominated / dominating
[例] ① One brand dominates the market, leaving little room for newcomers. 一个品牌主导市场，新入者空间很小。 ② The home team dominated possession but failed to score. 主队控球占优，却未能进球。
[源] 源自拉丁语 *dominari*（统治），*dominus*（主人）→ 支配。
[族] dominant adj., dominance 主导地位, domination 统治
[搭] dominate the market 主导市场, dominate the conversation 主导谈话, dominate in 在……占优, dominated by 由……主导
[联] lead 领先, surpass 超越, overshadow 使失色, control 控制, compare 比较, rival 匹敌, majority 多数, monopoly 垄断, inferior 较差的, compete 竞争
[记] 及物/不及物均可。dominant 形容词：dominant position 主导地位。dominate 强调「对照中压倒性优势」。

**against**
英/美 /əˈɡenst/
prep. 对照；对比；反对
[义] In contrast to; compared with; in opposition to. 引出对照参照（compare A against B 把 A 与 B 对照，against expectations 与预期相反，against the law 违法—不同义）。无比较级。
[例] ① We benchmarked our costs against those of three competitors. 我们把成本与三家竞争对手对照。 ② Sales rose 5% against the same period last year. 与去年同期相比，销售额升百分之五。
[搭] compare against 与……对照, against expectations 与预期相反, against the backdrop of 在……背景下, weigh A against B 权衡 A 与 B
[联] versus 对, compared with 与……相比, contrast 对照, oppose 反对, benchmark 基准, measure 衡量, compete 竞争, relative to 相对于, match 匹配, evaluate 评估
[记] compare/weigh/benchmark … against … 为高频结构。against 多义：对照、反对、倚靠（against the wall）依语境区分。

**one-to-one**
英/美 /ˌwʌn tə ˈwʌn/
adj./adv. 一对一的；一一对应的
[义] Matching each member of one set with exactly one member of another for comparison or teaching. 两组逐项对照（one-to-one comparison 逐项对照，one-to-one mapping 一一映射）。常作复合形容词。
[例] ① The study includes a one-to-one comparison of matched patients in two hospitals. 研究包括对两家医院配对患者的一对一对照。 ② There is no one-to-one correspondence between English and Chinese idioms. 英汉习语之间并非一一对应。
[搭] one-to-one comparison 逐项对照, one-to-one mapping 一一映射, one-to-one correspondence 一一对应, one-to-one meeting 一对一会议
[联] match 匹配, correspond 对应, parallel 平行, equivalent 等价的, compare 比较, individual 个人的, pair 配对, map 映射, direct 直接的, relationship 关系
[记] 连字符 one-to-one。not a one-to-one relationship 指「非一一对应」。one-to-one 亦可指「一对一辅导/会议」。

**tell the difference**
英/美 —（短语）
v. 分辨差异；看出区别
[义] To recognize how two or more things differ when compared. tell apart 的近义表达（tell the difference between A and B 区分 A 与 B，hard to tell the difference 很难分辨）。固定短语。
[例] ① Can you tell the difference between butter and margarine in this blind test? 这场盲测你能分辨黄油与人造黄油吗？ ② I cannot tell the difference—the two versions look identical. 我分不出来——两个版本看起来一模一样。
[搭] tell the difference between A and B 区分 A 与 B, hard to tell the difference 很难分辨, tell the difference 分辨差异
[联] tell apart 区分, distinguish 区分, differentiate 区分, indistinguishable 无法分辨的, compare 比较, similar 相似的, identical 完全相同的, spot 看出, notice 注意到, blur 模糊
[记] 常 can/can't tell the difference。与 tell A and B apart 近义可互换。tell the difference 更口语。
